# Valuation draft review

The reviewer answers each question in one line with the cell or quote that settles it. Apply [§13](../../../../docs/review.md#section-13): a repairable defect is REVISE; missing required evidence is BLOCKED, never PASS; a genuinely inapplicable check is N/A with a reason. The two-pass limit still applies. Complete unaffected checks, retain unsupported inputs as null, and report the affected cases. The analyst requests specific evidence from the runner under rule 16; it does not fetch directly. Do not invent inputs to obtain a passing validator exit code.

1. Do bear, base and bull have credible operating mechanisms, severity and timing under the case definitions? They may share the same core business. Identify what changes, what persists, and management's feasible response; do not demand moat loss or extra setbacks merely to make bear distinct.
2. Did usable consensus actually seed the near-term base under rule 10? Inspect the original table, provider/statistic (mean by default), dates, cutoff eligibility, periods, metric definitions and available coverage/spread. Reproduce period/accounting adjustments, aligned revenue/profit, implied growth/margins and material changes to the selected forecast. Compare with current performance and guidance. If coverage is missing or unusable, verify the documented search and supported fallback; missing consensus alone is not BLOCKED.
3. Does the path beyond consensus coverage follow evidenced demand, market opportunity, competition, capacity and investment needs, with the duration of growth drivers explained? Challenge a slowdown caused only by a coverage/model boundary and indefinite high-growth extrapolation. Check final-year scale/share against mature comparables and a sourced demand bound.
4. Is the target margin placed against observed mature-company results or a documented substitute, with accounting and business-mix differences reconciled, and does the argument for its position hold?
5. Do growth and margin agree, or does a niche-margin story carry mass-market revenue?
6. Is sales to capital chosen among the firm's lagged record, its latest marginal ratio and the industry figure, with the position explained; and is the final-year implied return on capital in `--diagnostics-only` output supported by comparable observed returns and the business story, with any excess return explained?
7. Does the investment-payoff check reconcile committed/discretionary spending, commissioning/utilization, revenue, costs and management response? Was year-five pressure or recovery explicitly assessed through years 6–10, including any decision to retain default interpolation? Challenge both unsupported payoff and costs that persist without a feasible business response.
8. Is the cost of capital supported by relevant dated evidence, shared unless an override is justified under rule 6, and free of failure risk and management quality? Compare classifications and mature rates with economically similar library companies; explain material differences instead of merely confirming that a dataset row exists.
9. Does every terminal premium, including bear's zero or positive choice, have independent mature-business support, consistent capital definitions and a sensible reinvestment implication? Were transition flags investigated for every case rather than used to set forecast capital efficiency or terminal return?
10. Is the `story_to_numbers` table complete in both directions, and does each row's number match its cell?
11. Is the selected base a supported central outlook? Distinguish modest, explained business caution from an arbitrary consensus haircut. Test the implied hypothesis against contrary evidence; a story written to defend imported numbers, automatic optimism discount or assumed permanent outperformance requires revision.
12. Against the previous draft, are the revisions justified by evidence rather than a systematic preference for higher or lower numbers? One-direction movement alone is not a failure; cite the reasons. Use N/A on a first draft.

13. For each material input, can you reproduce the connection from sourced fact through explicit analyst judgment to the chosen number, distinguish the evidence from the inference, and identify its range and revision trigger? For the few judgments that most change the operating outcome, independently compare a materially higher/lower credible path and explain why the evidence favors or fails to distinguish the selected one. Check linked growth, margin and investment assumptions together. A stronger explanation alone resolves a finding only when it supplies the missing economic support; otherwise revise the numbers or retain REVISE/BLOCKED.
14. Were suitable mature peers actually researched, with definitions, source dates and limitations recorded in `notes-valuation-evidence.md`? If unavailable, does a sourced substitute support the target, or is the check BLOCKED?
15. Do all explicit revenue years reconcile to the operating build, and do year-5 and final-year revenue and mix fit a sourced addressable-demand check or defensible alternative bound? A missing market-size figure cannot pass merely because the company is investing heavily or has a large backlog.

16. Does `notes-valuation-evidence.md` record the model-fit decision, accounting reconciliation and any unsupported capability? Does the actual YAML treatment match it?
17. Are tax losses, R&D, leases, share compensation, dilution and cash/holdings handled consistently where material, without reclassifying only the favorable side of an adjustment?
18. Are the major uncertainties mapped once, with dependencies and counterevidence explicit? Does the proposed weight make sense for the combined scenario severity, not just each event considered separately? Identify provisional odds and material mismatches, preserve owner weights, and distinguish a central base from the weighted result. A risk-factor disclosure is not measured likelihood; published analyst ranges are not automatic case outcomes or probability weights.
19. If a prior eligible forecast exists, were its original targets and exact periods assessed using the [forecast-learning procedure](forecasts-and-learning.md)? Where an original consensus baseline exists, were our adjustments compared with it against the same actuals? Does the new draft address applicable lessons with evidence rather than automatically moving every estimate in one direction?
20. Is the post-PASS forecast snapshot plan complete: exact inputs, model windows, accounting basis, overrides, origin and creation time, plus original consensus and its alignment bridge under the [learning procedure](forecasts-and-learning.md)? No backdating, later revisions substituted for the original baseline, or valuation results in an analyst-proposal snapshot.

## Evidence of using the references

In the review file, include a compact table: reference read | applicable judgment | YAML cell or evidence-note section | finding. Read the playbook and the same topical references used by the analyst, plus a topic the draft materially omitted. Verify material source claims directly in the cached original using the locator cited; do not treat the practical note as company evidence. Review missing company evidence separately from unavoidable forecasting uncertainty. A recorded, defensible estimate can pass without a source that predicts its exact value.

For prospective learning records, verify the frozen inputs and target definitions after PASS. On refresh, independently derive material actuals, matching dates and accounting basis before reading the writer's error attribution. No new valuation computation is required for that comparison. Apply the existing two-pass limit; these are checks within the same review, not extra review rounds.
