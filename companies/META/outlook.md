## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame: AI pays off in ads now and funds two further acts. Zuckerberg: "we are now at a point where our investments in AI are accelerating every major part of our core business" [Q2 2026 call, p.1]. The next acts are personal agents and "a large enterprise opportunity to sell to businesses, including APIs, business agents, potentially selling compute directly" [Q2 2026 call, p.1] (an API lets other companies' software use Meta's models; compute is computing capacity). Purpose: "We're the only major company building AI with the primary goal of putting superintelligence directly into people's hands" [Q2 2026 call, p.4].

The CFO: "we are today, and expect to be in the sort of foreseeable future, supply constrained, and that really includes our core business, too" [Q2 2026 call, p.21] (supply constrained: short of computing capacity). On Meta Superintelligence Labs: "I'm quite happy with the trajectory that we're on"; on open-source models (models published for anyone to use): "We plan to do a combination of open and closed models." and "we expect that we will get back to releasing some open source models at some point soon" [Q2 2026 call, p.16] [Q2 2026 call, p.18].

## 3. Growth engines

**AI ranking and ads models.** Instagram time spent grew "double digits"; a Reels ranking release drove "a 15 basis point increase in sessions" (a basis point is 0.01%); Threads' global ads expansion is complete [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 call, p.6]. Working if impressions and price keep growing.

**Advantage+ and AI creative tools.** "over $75 billion in annual revenue run-rate" (the current pace, annualized); 9 million small businesses use an AI creative tool [Q2 2026 call, p.7] [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Working if the run-rate keeps rising.

**WhatsApp paid messaging and subscriptions.** Other Family of Apps revenue $1.0 billion, up 73% [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 follow-up call, p.3]. Working if growth stays above ad growth.

**Business agents.** "more than 1 million businesses using them to talk to their customers or complete sales every week"; charging starts in the second half via Meta One subscriptions and per-token pricing (a token is a unit of AI text) from August 1 [Q2 2026 call, p.3] [Q2 2026 follow-up call, p.10]. Working if a revenue or paying-business figure appears.

**Meta AI, Muse models, API, Meta One.** 60% more people use Meta AI daily since the Muse Spark rebuild; a model API and the Meta One subscription launched [Q2 2026 call, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.8]. Working if user or revenue figures appear.

**Glasses.** Reality Labs revenue up 16% on AI glasses; a new line with EssilorLuxottica whose "Early sales have been strong, exceeding our expectations" [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 call, p.4]. Working if Reality Labs revenue grows while its loss stays flat.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim from the Q2 2026 release; the call repeated the same items [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.9]. A "headwind" is a drag.

- **Q3 2026 revenue:** "We expect third quarter 2026 total revenue to be in the range of $61-64 billion. Our guidance assumes foreign currency is an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year total revenue growth, based on current exchange rates." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **FY2026 expenses:** "We are raising the lower-end of our expense outlook to incorporate the $2.4 billion charges related to legal proceedings recognized in the second quarter. We now expect full year 2026 total expenses to be in the range of $165-169 billion." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **FY2026 operating income:** "We continue to expect to deliver operating income this year that is above 2025 operating income." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **FY2026 capex:** "We anticipate 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, to be in the range of $130-145 billion, narrowed from our prior outlook of $125-145 billion." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **Tax:** "Absent any changes to our tax landscape, we expect our tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 to be between 15-17%, an increase from our prior outlook of 13-16%." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **Legal:** "Finally, we continue to monitor active legal and regulatory matters that could significantly impact our business and financial results. For example, we continue to see scrutiny on youth-related issues in several markets and have a number of youth-related trials scheduled for this year in the U.S., which may ultimately result in a material loss." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **2027 capex (call only; no number):** "we aren’t providing a specific outlook for 2027 CapEx at this time." [Q2 2026 call, p.10]
- **Reality Labs (10-Q only):** "we expect our full-year 2026 RL operating losses to remain similar to 2025." [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2]
