## Conversion method

Python 3 with BeautifulSoup (lxml parser): dropped `script`, `style`, `noscript`, `head`, `ix:header` and any element styled `display:none`; each table row replaced by its non-empty cells joined with " | " between `[TABLE]` / `[/TABLE]` markers (cells that are only ")", "%" or "$" merged into their neighbour); `p`, `div`, `br`, headings and list items converted to newlines; whitespace collapsed; curly apostrophes and other UTF-8 kept. BeautifulSoup emitted `XMLParsedAsHTMLWarning` for the inline-XBRL 10-Ks (they start with an XML declaration); output checked and unaffected. Same method as the earlier run used for the reused files. The 8-K cache files concatenate the body and the chosen exhibits with `===== <label> =====` separators naming the source document, accession and filing date.

## Notes for the writer

- **Fiscal calendar and labels.** FY ends May 31; FY2026 = June 2025–May 2026. Q4 has no 10-Q; Q4 figures are FY minus nine months (computed) unless taken from the press release. Quarter label: `Q4 FY2026 (quarter ended May 31, 2026)`.

- **Presentation changes to watch.** (1) "Wholesale equivalent revenues" (a non-GAAP measure) stopped in FY2025; the Men's/Women's/Kids'/Jordan split moved to a reported basis in the FY2025 10-K and the FY2026 10-K dropped Men's/Women's/Kids' entirely (Jordan Brand dollars survive only in a footnote: 7,034 / 7,270 / 8,701 for FY2026 / FY2025 / FY2024). (3) Endorsement-contract obligations from FY2025 onward "include associated marketing commitments" previously inside other purchase obligations, so the $10.6 billion (May 2024) to $16.2 billion (May 2025) jump is partly reclassification and the 10-Ks do not quantify it. (4) The FY2025 10-K's $7.9 billion product purchase obligation was corrected to approximately $5 billion by the 2025-07-28 8-K. (5) The FY2026 10-K labels the cash-flow line "Depreciation and amortization" (older 10-Ks: "Depreciation"); amounts for overlapping years agree. (6) The FY2026 10-K balance sheet shows no "Notes payable" line for May 31, 2025 (the $5 million sits inside accrued liabilities, 5,916 vs 5,911 in the FY2025 presentation).
- **Numbering quirks.** Segment note is Note 15 in FY2023–FY2026 (titled "Segment Information" from FY2025) and Note 17 in FY2022; restructuring/severance is Note 19 (FY2023–FY2024) and Note 18 (FY2025–FY2026); divestitures Note 18 (FY2023–FY2024). 10-Q segment note is Note 11, severance Note 13. ## Reference / tag scheme used in notes-transcript.md

The PDFs have **no printed page numbers** (checked: no digit-only lines in either the layout or the reading-order conversion; every one of the 25/26 PDF pages begins and ends mid-body-text; the only page furniture is the title block on page 1 and the "# # #" sign-off on the last page). `transcript.txt` has no line numbers. So, per AGENTS.md §3 rule 2, notes are tagged by section and speaker: `[Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, <speaker surname>]` or `[Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, <analyst surname>/<firm>]` (the analyst whose question is being answered, whoever speaks); the operator and the header are tagged `[Q4 FY2026 call, operator]` / `[Q4 FY2026 call, header]`. Supplement notes use `[Q3 FY2026 call, <section>]` against `transcript-FY2026-Q3.txt`. Where a quote does not open its paragraph, or a bullet paraphrases, the note adds `¶“<first five words of the paragraph>”` so the paragraph can be found with `grep -F`. **Both transcripts use straight apostrophes (') only — 260 in Q4, 186 in Q3 — and no curly quotes**, so locators and quotes are copied with straight apostrophes; the curly marks in `¶“…”` are the locator delimiters, not part of the searched text. Speaker turns are bracketed upper-case labels on their own line (`[ELLIOTT HILL]`, `[MATT FRIEND]`, `[OPERATOR]`, `[ADRIENNE YIH]`, …). The writer and reviewer should use the same tags.

A PDF-page map is given at the top of notes-ir.md for anyone opening the PDFs.
- Image-only content: none. Every table extracted cleanly in `-layout` form; the plain reading-order copies scramble tables and should not be used.
- What the release does not contain: cash flow statement (cash from operations, capex, D&A, cash paid for buybacks and dividends are "not disclosed" in the release; take them from the 10-K); a Wholesale / NIKE Direct table (only text bullets rounded to $0.1 billion, with reported and currency-neutral growth); geography-by-channel splits; period-end share count; remaining buyback authorization (only "four-year, $18 billion program"); store counts; digital revenue in dollars; order book; headcount; any guidance table or outlook section. Nike gives guidance only on the call.
- Guidance: none in either release. The only forward-looking wording is inside the CEO/CFO quotes (Q4: "focused on consistent execution, improved profitability and scaling our wins"; "adjusting our operating costs for greater efficiency over time"; Q3: "Win Now actions will continue to impact results over the balance of the calendar year"). Use the transcript for guidance.
- Definitions (verbatim in notes §8 and §10): "currency-neutral" / "% change excluding currency changes" = prior-year actual exchange rates applied, a non-GAAP measure; EBIT = net income before interest (income) expense, net and income tax expense, with EBIT margin = EBIT / revenues (non-GAAP; the EBIT table is its own reconciliation); Global Brand Divisions = NIKE Brand licensing and miscellaneous revenue on the revenue side, centrally managed product creation/design, NIKE Direct global digital operations and enterprise technology costs on the EBIT side; Corporate = FX hedge gains/losses on revenue, and unallocated G&A, HQ depreciation, unallocated insurance/benefit/compensation including stock-based compensation, and certain FX gains/losses on EBIT. NIKE Direct is not defined in the release (the text splits it into "NIKE Brand Digital" and "NIKE-owned stores"); the 10-K defines it.
- IEEPA tariff recovery (Q4 FY2026 only): the Supreme Court ruling of February 20, 2026 led Nike to book an expected recovery in Q4: $986 million benefit to gross margin ("approximately 900 basis points"), split $965 million North America EBIT and $21 million Converse EBIT, $0.52 of the $0.72 diluted EPS, and "approximately $0.3 billion of cash received from IEEPA tariff recoveries" inside FY2026 operating cash flow. The release says the FY2026 benefit "largely offsets the impact of the IEEPA tariffs recognized during fiscal 2026" but does not quantify that tariff cost. Notes §1c gives gatherer-computed ex-recovery figures (labelled "computed"); the release itself gives no such adjusted measure. Any comparison of Q4 gross margin (49.2%) or North America EBIT (2,000) with prior quarters must say so.
- Reclassifications / restatements: none in either release. Nine-month FY2026 figures in the Q3 release plus Q4 FY2026 reconcile exactly to the FY2026 figures in the Q4 release (checked for revenues, gross profit, demand creation, operating overhead, net income, EBIT by segment, dividends per share). Footnote numbering differs between the two releases only because the Q4 tables add the IEEPA footnote (EBIT fn 2) and the Jordan Brand footnote (Divisional Revenues fn 3). The Q3 balance sheet has a "Notes payable" row (— vs 4); the Q4 balance sheet has no such row.
- Basis of the NIKE Brand Digital and NIKE-owned stores percentages ("12 percent decrease", "7 percent decrease" in Q4; "9 percent" and "5 percent" in Q3; "12 percent" and "4 percent" for FY2026) is not stated as reported or currency-neutral in the sentences; quote them as printed.
- Jordan Brand revenue is disclosed only for the full year (FY2026 $7,034 million vs $7,270 million, down 3% reported / 5% currency-neutral) in a Q4 footnote; no quarterly figure.
- Accounts receivable rose 26% year over year at May 31, 2026 (5,931 vs 4,717) and 20% at February 28, 2026 with no explanation in either release; not explained.
- The "Q4FY26 Financial Schedules & Key Financial Metrics" PDF (supplemental.txt) adds nothing to the release; cite the release for prose and either file for table figures.
