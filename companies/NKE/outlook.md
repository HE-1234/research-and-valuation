## 2. What management says about the core business

Hill's frame is sport first: "So when we lead with sport, we win." [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Yih/Barclays]. The "Win Now" actions begun in December 2024 are to be sunset "by the end of this calendar year"; the model that follows, "the Sport Offense", has "about 8,000 teammates" in "vertical sport teams" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Sherman/Bernstein] [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]. He was blunt: "Overall, the results aren't there yet. We know we're not living up to our full potential, particularly in NIKE Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear, where sell-through remains challenged, impacting both current discounting and future order books." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]

Three moves define the year. Wholesale: "We've been rebuilding our wholesale relationships", with wholesale up 4% and "more than 15,000 spaces in wholesale doors" refreshed [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]. Full price: "we're discounting less on NIKE Digital", to "reposition digital to be a premium business" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill] [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Boss/JPMorgan]. Supply: "we have taken $2 billion out of the market in FY '26 of our classic franchises" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Yih/Barclays].

Friend's frame is the consumer and the cost base: "Our consumer is under pressure around the world", and "we see meaningful opportunity to structurally improve our gross margins, really focused on taking out cost of our supply chain and the make of our product" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend] [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Sherman/Bernstein].

## 3. Growth engines

**Running.** "We've now delivered 5 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth in NIKE Running", adding "roughly $1 billion" and "5 points of running market share in statement footwear" in Western Europe and North America [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]. Working if the Q1 call reports a sixth double-digit quarter.

**Global football and the World Cup.** Kits sold at "2.5x" the 2022 pace, and Q1 demand creation "grows high single digits as we invest into the World Cup" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill] [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]. Working if football keeps growing double digits and Q1 gross margin still expands despite the spend.

**The wholesale rebuild.** North America wholesale grew 10% in Q4, but "we didn't sell in up 10%. There was a meaningful amount of that revenue growth that was associated with lower returns or sales-related reserves and returns, lower discount and lower cancellations." (sell-in: Nike's shipments to retailers) [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Boruchow/Wells Fargo]. Working if wholesale grows while inventory stays flat; Q2 "has a tough compare in North America" (a strong year-ago quarter to beat) [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Boruchow/Wells Fargo].

**The Digital reset.** Digital fell 12%, but in EMEA "off-price was down over 50%" (off-price: sales through discount channels) with a "15-point improvement in full price realization" (the share of sales made at full price) [Q4 FY2026 release, headline bullets] [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]. Working if the Digital decline narrows while gross margin rises.

**Greater China.** Revenue fell 17%; inventory "was down double digits with units also down double digits"; a local team will deliver "locally designed, developed and manufactured product in China in holiday '27" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend] [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Hutchinson/Bank of America]. Working if EBIT stops falling before revenue does.

**Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear.** The drag, not yet an engine: "approximately half of our revenue", negative all year, with "more than a dozen new footwear styles" in the second half [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]. Working if the declines narrow in the back half. Women's was given no separate figure [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Drbul/BTIG].

## 5. Claims to verify next quarter

1. **Q1 FY2027 reported revenue falls 2% to 5% year over year.** Our sharpening of "low to mid-single digits" into a 2–5% band, not management's numbers. "we expect reported revenues to be down low to mid-single digits" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
2. **Q1 FY2027 gross margin is above Q1 FY2026's.** "We expect gross margin in Q1 to be slightly positive." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
3. **Q1 FY2027 demand creation expense grows 7% to 9% year over year.** Our sharpening of "high single digits", not management's numbers. "demand creation grows high single digits as we invest into the World Cup" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
4. **Q1 FY2027 operating overhead expense is below Q1 FY2026's in dollars.** "We expect Q1 SG&A dollars to be flat with operating overhead declining" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
5. **Q1 FY2027 Greater China revenue falls at least 10% currency-neutral.** Our sharpening of "in line with recent performance" into a 10% floor, not management's number. "expect revenue trends in Greater China over the near term to be in line with recent performance" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
6. **Inventories at August 31, 2026 are below the August 31, 2025 level.** Our sharpening of "manage inventory" into a year-over-year decline, not management's number. "we are taking actions to tighten buys, reduce future sell-in and manage inventory" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
7. **David Denton signs the Q1 FY2027 10-Q as Chief Financial Officer.** "David M. Denton will join the company as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective August 17." [8-K 2026-06-16]
8. **On the Q1 FY2027 call, management reaffirms earnings "flattish" for Q4 FY2026 through Q2 FY2027 excluding the tariff benefit.** "We reiterate our expectation for earnings to be flattish over that time period, excluding the benefit from tariff recovery." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
9. **On the Q1 FY2027 call, management states that Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear declined in the quarter.** "We expect Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear to continue to be negative this fiscal year" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]
10. **Disclosure check (quarter column): the Q1 FY2027 10-Q, Part II Item 2, shows no shares repurchased in the quarter.** Not a management claim. "We paused repurchases under this program during the first quarter of fiscal 2026" [10-K FY2026, Item 5]
11. **Nike holds its Investor Day on November 16–17, 2026 and gives full-year and long-term guidance there.** Horizon: Q2 FY2027; carries until then. "we look forward to sharing the next phase of our growth strategy at our Investor Day on November 16 and 17" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hill]; "we will return to providing full year and long-term guidance at our Investor Day in the fall" [Q3 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Friend]
