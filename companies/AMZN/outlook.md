## 2. What management says about the core business

The call was built around AWS. Jassy opened with "I'll start with AWS, which is booming right now" and said AWS could "very possibly be a trillion-dollar annual revenue business for us in time" [Q2 2026 call]. AI pulls the older business along ("growth in one is driving growth in the other"), "85% of the global IT spend is still on premises" (computers companies run in their own buildings), and supply, not demand, is the limit: Amazon "will still not have enough capacity to meet all the demand we have in 2026" [Q2 2026 call].

The capex defence, his "ROIC equation" (return on invested capital): data centers are paid for two years before they earn but then earn "for 30-plus years", servers break even in "a little less than three years" against contracts of "at least five-year terms", so Amazon will "encounter free cash flow headwinds until these data centers come online" (a headwind is a drag), and "We've done this before in the first era of cloud computing" [Q2 2026 call]. That is management's argument, not a result.

On the store, the words were "record delivery speeds" and "the second largest grocer in the U.S." [Q2 2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**AWS, AI infrastructure and Trainium.** Revenue $42.2 billion, up 37%, the fastest in 18 quarters; operating margin 39.4%; backlog about $496 billion; the AI and chips businesses each past "a $25 billion annual revenue run rate" (a quarter's pace expressed as a yearly figure) [Q2 2026 release] [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 1]. Jassy says 2027 capacity is "largely reserved" and selling Trainium outside AWS has "a real chance" [Q2 2026 call]. Working or not: backlog rising; growth above 30%; margin holding as depreciation arrives.

**Advertising.** $19.8 billion, up 26%; Sponsored Products is the largest format and live-sports inventory (ad slots) "all sold out" [Q2 2026 release] [Q2 2026 call]. Working or not: growth stays ahead of third-party seller services.

**Marketplace and Prime.** Seller services up 16%, paid units up 17%, sellers 61% of units, Prime membership growth "double-digit" [Q2 2026 release] [Q2 2026 call]. Working or not: unit growth holds after the Prime Day shift; North America margin at or above 7%.

**Grocery, everyday essentials and same-day.** Grocery was "over $150 billion in gross sales in 2025"; same-day perishables reach "2,300 cities"; Amazon Now (30-minute delivery) is in nine countries and over 250 cities [2025 shareholder letter, p.3] [Q2 2026 call] [Q2 2026 release]. Working or not: management keeps saying grocery outgrows the rest of the store.

**Amazon Leo.** Nearly 400 satellites in orbit, "enough to begin initial satellite internet service this year"; the $10.9 billion Globalstar deal should close in 2027 [Q2 2026 release] [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 4]. Working or not: service starts in 2026 with paying customers named.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim from the Q2 2026 release, which gave no full-year, segment, margin, capex or free cash flow guidance [Q2 2026 release].

- "Net sales are expected to be between $197.0 billion and $202.0 billion, or to grow between 9% and 12% compared with third quarter 2025. Excluding the impact of Prime Day in both 2025 and 2026, third quarter 2026 year-over-year growth would be nearly 400 basis points higher. This guidance anticipates an unfavorable impact of approximately 80 basis points from foreign exchange rates." [Q2 2026 release]
- "Operating income is expected to be between $22.5 billion and $26.5 billion, compared with $17.4 billion in third quarter 2025." [Q2 2026 release]
- "This guidance assumes, among other things, no impact from energy derivative contract remeasurements, and that no additional business acquisitions, restructurings, or legal settlements are concluded." [Q2 2026 release]

In plain words: 100 basis points is one percentage point, and the energy derivative remeasurements are the paper gains or losses on long-term power contracts described in business.md §4.

From the call (a machine transcript; trust the wording, not the digits), with the filed figure alongside:

- **2026 capex (Jassy):** "We now believe we will spend approximately $220 billion in cash CapEx in 2026. The higher cost of memory pushing this number up from our prior estimate of about $200 billion." No filing gives a 2026 figure; first-half cash capex was $96.3 billion [Q2 2026 call] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2].
- **Capacity (Jassy):** "Even at that amount, we will still not have enough capacity to meet all the demand we have in 2026, and I believe this dynamic will also be true in 2027, too." [Q2 2026 call]
- **Free cash flow (Jassy; "monetized" means earning revenue):** "we'll spend a lot of CapEx and encounter free cash flow headwinds until these data centers come online, can be monetized, and we get a few years into these servers being utilized." Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was $(7.6) billion [Q2 2026 call] [Q2 2026 release].
