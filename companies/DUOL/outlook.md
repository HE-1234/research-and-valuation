## 2. What management says about the core business

The ambition: "Our ambition is to teach a billion people, and every step we take toward a better product brings us closer to that goal" [Q2 2026 release]. The target: "our medium-term goal is to reach 100 million DAUs in 2028", restated on the call as "We remain focused on reaching 100 million DAUs in 2028" [Q1 2026 letter, p.4] [Q2 2026 call]. 2026 puts retention and learning outcomes ahead of monetisation: "Our strategy this year has been to prioritize the first two, while working on ways to monetize that are not at odds with user growth" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. The CEO called the financial side a "box": "roughly 11% year-over-year bookings growth and then the rest of the efforts are in increasing DAUs and in teaching better" [Q2 2026 call]. On users: "Since two of these are permanent, we expect DAU year-over-year growth throughout the rest of the year to remain above the 20% we had previously guided to" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4].

## 3. Growth engines

**DAU growth through product and marketing.** 58.7 million DAUs, up 23%, from "product changes, marketing impact, and a one-time event to revive lost streaks" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. Performance marketing (paid advertising) is "already" showing "up in top-of-funnel growth" (more people trying the app) [Q2 2026 letter, p.5]. Working if: Q3 DAU growth stays above 20% and CURR is reported at or above 84%.

**Video Call into Super, and the fate of Max.** Video Call's cost fell from "like $0.30 per call" to "under $0.01", so most new Super subscribers now have it and existing ones follow "later this year" [Q2 2026 call] [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. The top tier is undecided: "we may actually sunset Max ... we're going to have an answer to this in the next couple of quarters ... without a loss of revenue" [Q2 2026 call]. Max's share of subscribers is not disclosed. Working if: the rollout completes with gross margin near 71%.

**Monetisation "not at odds with user growth".** Free trials are moving from seven days to one month for "most" trials; an ad-supported Super Lite at "about half the price" is in test [Q2 2026 call]. The Family Plan is not discussed [Q2 2026 letter, p.12]. Working if: paid-subscriber growth (17%) closes toward DAU growth (23%) and Q4 bookings growth exceeds Q3's.

**New subjects.** Chess had "more than seven million DAUs in less than a year" at the Q4 letter; Math and Music "have single-digit millions of DAUs" [Q4 2025 letter, p.5] [Q2 2026 call]. No subject figures were updated in Q1 or Q2. Working if: subject DAUs are disclosed again and grow.

**Advertising.** $21.1 million in Q2, up 2% [Q2 2026 letter, p.19]. "over the next few quarters ... you're going to see some improvements in our ads business" [Q2 2026 call]. Working if: ad revenue growth returns to double digits.

**Duolingo English Test.** $10.1 million, flat; not mentioned on the call [Q2 2026 letter, p.19] [Q2 2026 call]. Working if: the DET row grows.

**China and Asia.** "Asia is still the fastest growing"; China is the second-largest DAU market, expected to be the largest "in a year or 2" [Q2 2026 call]. No regional figures are disclosed. Working if: China becomes the largest market without regulatory disruption.

- "We expect a gross margin of approximately 71.0% in Q3 and approximately 71.6% for the full year, better than the trajectory we outlined on our Q1 call based on AI cost trends." [Q2 2026 letter, p.10]
- "We expect stock-based compensation to be around 15.0% of revenue in 2026, with absolute expense increasing through the year. We continue to expect dilution from equity grants of approximately 3.5-4.0%, before the effect of any share repurchases. We assume an effective tax rate of approximately 23-25% for the year." [Q2 2026 letter, p.10]
