## 3. KPIs the company highlights that are NOT in the 10-Q (checked against `10-Q-2026-Q2.txt` by text search)

- **Current User Retention Rate (CURR)**: "our CURR is at an all-time high of 84%, up by about 1% from last year." Definition (verbatim): "the proportion of 'current user DAUs' who come back the next day. A 'current user DAU' is one who was also active another time in the previous seven days." Called "one of our most important leading indicators". Rounded statement; no series. [Q2 2026 letter, p.4] (10-Q: no mention of CURR; it says DAU growth was "driven largely by an increase in retention of current users".)
- **Streak Revival** (one-time June event): "15.4 million learners revived their streaks, including nearly 8 million users who had no active streak when the event began." Eligible learners restored their longest-ever streak by opting in and completing three lessons. [Q2 2026 letter, p.5] (Not in 10-Q.)
- **Organic social impressions**: "our social accounts have surpassed one billion organic impressions each quarter"; "we now also work with hundreds of creators around the world"; "In countries like China, Indonesia, and India, roughly two thirds of our social impressions have come from influencer-generated content." [Q2 2026 letter, p.5] (Not in 10-Q.)
- **Performance marketing**: "tripling both the number of channels in our mix and our performance creative output globally" over the past year; "the vast majority of our user growth continues to be organic". [Q2 2026 letter, p.5] (Not in 10-Q.) Marketing spend in dollars: not disclosed (only GAAP/non-GAAP S&M).
- **Video Call access**: "most new Super Duolingo subscribers now have access to Video Call, and we expect to extend that access to existing Super subscribers later this year." [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]; Q1: "we've more than doubled the average number of words spoken per user in Video Call" over the last year. [Q1 2026 letter, p.4] (Not in 10-Q.)
- **Longer free trials**: "offering longer free trials, which we expect to increase both revenue and DAUs" — "good early results". [Q2 2026 letter, p.4] (Not in 10-Q beyond the paid-subscriber definition.)
- **Fully diluted share count** ~50.7M and the dilutive-securities table with share-price basis. [Q2 2026 letter, p.9, p.11] (10-Q has the options footnote wording but no "Total estimated diluted" table.)
- **Buyback through 2026-08-01**: ~$71.9M / ~708K shares cumulative, "nearly all of 2024 and 2025 dilution". [Q2 2026 letter, p.9] (10-Q covers only through 2026-06-30: 694,630 shares, $70.3M, avg $101.15; Q2 432K shares at avg $102.80.)
- **Non-GAAP operating expense by line** (R&D, S&M, G&A) — see §6. (10-Q has Adjusted EBITDA and FCF but no non-GAAP opex lines.)
- **Gross margin guidance and the "ahead of our expectation of approximately 71.0%" comparison.** [Q2 2026 letter, p.9, p.10] (Not in 10-Q.)
- **Content output** (Q1 letter): "In Q1 2026 alone, we published 20,500 course units ... up from 7,100 per quarter in 2025 and 1,800 per quarter in 2024"; content "up to Duolingo Score 129 (CEFR B2) across courses teaching our 9 most-learned languages". - **DAU growth history**: "from Q2 2022 to Q2 2025, we did not have a single quarter with year-over-year DAU growth below 40%". [Q4 2025 letter, p.4]
- **Medium-term target**: "reach 100 million DAUs in 2028". [Q4 2025 letter, p.5] [Q1 2026 letter, p.4]

## 5. Management commentary in the letters (by topic; paraphrase unless quoted)

**Why they prioritise DAU growth**
- Q4 2025 letter (CEO, p.4–5): DAU growth "decelerated throughout 2025"; "Some of this deceleration is natural as we scale; and some of it, we believe, is a function of our increased focus on monetization in recent years." Friction (ad load, upsells) "is part of the reason our DAU growth has slowed, so we've decided to prioritize user growth over monetization." Medium-term goal 100M DAUs in 2028: "a more resilient brand; a business with meaningfully higher bookings and profit". Alternative path "would be a smaller and less valuable business in the long run ... our position as the leading education app in the world would be less defensible." Short-term cost: bookings growth ~11% vs "nearly 20%" otherwise; Adjusted EBITDA margin down to ~25%. [Q4 2025 letter, p.4–5]
- Q1 2026 letter (CEO, p.4, p.6): "We believe that a larger user base will make Duolingo a significantly more valuable company"; "We are early in our strategic shift"; "Teaching better not only translates into better learning outcomes, but over time it can drive engagement, retention, growth, and brand credibility." [Q1 2026 letter, p.4, p.6]
- Q2 2026 letter (CEO, p.4, p.6): "Last quarter, we said it was too soon to know whether our strategy of teaching better and prioritizing user growth was working. While we're still early in executing this strategy, these results reinforce our confidence that we're on the right path." "A better product creates more engaged users, which ultimately helps us build a larger and more durable business." [Q2 2026 letter, p.6] Three factors for Q2 DAU acceleration: "product changes, marketing impact, and a one-time event to revive lost streaks. Since two of these are permanent..." [p.4]
- "The Green Machine": "We test hundreds of changes to the product, measure the results, and double down on what works." Applied "mainly to optimize three things: user retention, learning outcomes, and monetization. Our strategy this year has been to prioritize the first two, while working on ways to monetize that are not at odds with user growth." [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]

**Product changes**
- Speaking (Q1): "Speaking practice, historically the biggest gap for learners on Duolingo, is now a core part of the product." Spoken tokens (speak answers to most exercises), Flashcards (say words aloud), Speaking Adventures (real-world tasks with characters). [Q1 2026 letter, p.4] Q4 plan: "More AI-powered speaking ... allowing them to answer most exercises with voice instead of tapping or typing ... a new free AI-powered lesson type called Speaking Adventures. More speaking practice is particularly important for English learners." [Q4 2025 letter, p.4]

Outside of the frontier model companies, we have created one of the most commercially successful consumer AI products. And yet ... we will move Video Call to be part of the Super Duolingo tier and experiment with changes to our top subscription tier, Duolingo Max." [Q4 2025 letter, p.4] Q1: "Video Call continues to improve and remains one of our most impactful features ... we're starting to expand access to Video Call to Super subscribers." [Q1 2026 letter, p.4] Q2: most new Super subscribers have Video Call; extension to existing Super subscribers "later this year"; "demonstrating how AI is helping us deliver higher-quality learning at scale." [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]

## 7. KPI and non-GAAP definition changes

- **Free cash flow recast (Q4 2025 letter, verbatim)**: "Prior to the first quarter of 2025, free cash flow added back taxes paid related to stock-based compensation equity awards, acquisition transaction costs, and acquisition earn-out payments. Beginning in Q1 2025, we have aligned our calculation of free cash flow to this definition, and prior periods in this letter have been recast to conform to this presentation." FY2024 and Q4 2024 FCF in the Q4 letter are recast figures. [Q4 2025 letter, p.22; p.3 fn 2; p.8 fn 3] The Q1 and Q2 2026 letters carry the simple definition with no recast language.
- **Adjusted EBITDA**: integration costs added to the definition from Q3 2025 (see §6.1); impairment of capitalized software present in the Q4 2025 and Q2 2026 definitions, absent from the Q1 2026 definition text. [Q4 2025 letter, p.20] [Q1 2026 letter, p.21] [Q2 2026 letter, p.20]
- **Bookings definition** unchanged across the three letters (verbatim, Q2): "Subscription bookings represent the amounts we receive from a purchase of any Duolingo subscription offering. Total bookings include subscription bookings, income from advertising networks for advertisements served to our users, purchases of the Duolingo English Test, and in-app purchases of virtual goods ('IAPs'). We believe bookings provide an indication of trends in our operating results, including cash flows, that are not necessarily reflected in our revenues because we recognize subscription revenues ratably over the lifetime of a subscription, the majority of which are twelve months in duration." DET is included in total bookings. [Q2 2026 letter, p.12]
- **Paid subscribers** definition unchanged: unique user accounts with an active paid subscription at period end; excludes free-trial users and non-paying family-plan members. [Q2 2026 letter, p.12]
- **DAU / MAU** definitions unchanged (average of daily / monthly uniques over the quarter; app or "learning section of our website"). MAU is "a supplemental measure". Ordering change only: the Q4 2025 letter lists MAUs first in the summary table and definitions; the Q1 and Q2 2026 letters demote MAUs to a footnote. The Q1 footnote says MAUs "as of March 31" while Q2 says "the three months ended June 30" (the definition is the quarterly average in both). [Q4 2025 letter, p.8, p.13] [Q1 2026 letter, p.8] [Q2 2026 letter, p.8]
- Standing caveat (verbatim): "Because we update the methodologies we employ to create metrics, our operating metrics may not be comparable to those in prior periods." [Q2 2026 letter, p.12]
- Constant-currency method (verbatim, Q2): "We calculate constant-currency revenues by translating current period foreign currency revenues using prior-year exchange rates applied consistently over the full revenue recognition period. We calculate constant-currency bookings by using current period foreign currency bookings and translating them to constant currency using prior-year comparable period exchange rates." [Q2 2026 letter, p.13]
