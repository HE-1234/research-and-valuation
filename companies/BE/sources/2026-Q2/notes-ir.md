**Structural notes for the writer.**
1. The Q2 2026 release shows three quarters side by side (Q2'26 | Q1'26 | Q2'25). It has **no six-month / year-to-date column** (statements of operations and cash flows are three-month only). Six-month figures below are Q2 + Q1 sums marked (computed); the 10-Q is the source for reported six-month numbers.
2. Neither the Q2 2026 release nor the Q2 2026 deck gives backlog, MW/GW deployed or accepted, manufacturing capacity, named customers, or international/hydrogen items. The only backlog disclosure in these documents is in the Q4 2025 release and deck (section 4). The only customer names are Oracle and Brookfield Asset Management on the Q4 2025 deck; the Q2 2026 CEO quote speaks of "all the major US hyperscalers" without names.
3. Related-party revenue (footnote 1 to the statement of operations) collapsed from $574.2M in Q4'25 (73.8% of revenue **(computed)**) and $373.3M in Q1'26 to $2.8M in Q2'26 (section 5).
4. The Q2 2026 deck dropped the "Driving Profitability and Generating Cash" page (Adjusted EBITDA and operating-cash-flow charts, with 2026E ranges) that the Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 decks carried; the Q2 documents give no Adjusted EBITDA or cash-flow guidance.
5. The company does not define or report "free cash flow" in these releases (the Q4 2025 release says "2nd consecutive year of positive free cash flow" without a figure or definition).
