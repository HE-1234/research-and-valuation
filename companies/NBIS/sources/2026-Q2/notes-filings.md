## 1. Business description (company's own words), customers, products, other businesses, segments

**What it is.**
- "Nebius, a global AI cloud platform, the core business of Nebius Group N.V. ... delivers a unified full-stack AI cloud that spans the complete AI journey – from compute capacity to software and services – that enables fast and efficient training and inference at scale. Founded around deep in-house technological expertise, Nebius offers a comprehensive and integrated suite of AI and ML cloud solutions, including both hardware and software built in-house. This combination of AI-optimized hardware and software enables us to deliver high-performance GPU compute clusters, storage, managed services, and advanced tools for AI model training and inference at enterprise-scale." [6-K Q2 2026 MD&A, Overview]
- "Nebius offers one of the few global, at scale, multi-tenant clouds purpose-built for AI, with a significant presence in Europe, the U.S., and other geographies around the world." [6-K Q2 2026 MD&A, Overview]
- Note 1 wording: "a technology company whose core business is the operation of a global artificial intelligence ('AI') cloud platform. The Group builds and operates full-stack infrastructure to serve the AI industry, spanning data centers, large-scale GPU clusters and servers designed in-house, an AI-optimized cloud platform, and software, tools and managed services that support AI model training and inference at scale." [6-K Q2 2026 FS, Note 1]
- "Nebius provides a full-stack AI cloud - from silicon to software - encompassing data centers, in-house-designed infrastructure, and an integrated software layer". "We build our infrastructure from the ground up, designing servers and racks in-house". "a consistent track record of being one of the first-to-deploy the latest generation of NVIDIA GPU chips." [20-F FY2025, Item 4]
- Headquartered in Amsterdam; registered office Schiphol Boulevard 165, Schiphol, the Netherlands; Class A shares listed on Nasdaq Global Select Market, ticker NBIS. Principal operating subsidiaries: Nebius B.V., Nebius Inc. and EdTech Plus B.V. [20-F FY2025, Item 4]
- Incorporated in the Netherlands in June 2004; became the parent of the group in 2007. [6-K Q2 2026 FS, Note 1; 20-F FY2024, Item 4]

**How revenue is generated (who pays and why).**
- "Our Nebius AI cloud business generates revenue by providing our customers with a comprehensive and integrated AI cloud platform, underpinned by high-performance GPU compute capacity, storage, and networking resources, as well as value-add software solutions ... Revenue from the cloud platform is recognized as services provided in accordance with customer contract due dates and the applicable contract model. We offer both on-demand 'pay-as-you-go' pricing and fixed 'reserved capacity' contracts." [6-K Q2 2026 MD&A, Components of Results of Operations]
- "Pay-as-you-go (PAYG) access is provided to customers on a consumption basis and is billed monthly in arrears based on usage of services in the period. For Reserved Capacity arrangements, the Group recognizes revenue ratably over time as services are provided." "A substantial portion of the Group's revenue is collected on a prepaid basis." [20-F FY2025, F-pages, Note 1]
- Customer types described: "Our primary customers today range from large enterprises, established software vendors, scaled AI companies and startups to research labs and individual developers building the next generation of AI models, applications and services." Target segments listed: Enterprise Customers; Software Vendors; AI-Native Tech Companies ("generally VC-backed"); AI Labs; and Hyperscaler Contracts. [20-F FY2025, Item 4]
- On hyperscalers, verbatim: "AI hyperscalers represent important large scale customers for AI compute capacity. The purchasing behavior of these industry-leading technology companies (some of which may offer competing solutions to our AI cloud) differs from our primary customer base and may not include our primary AI cloud services. We may engage in such customer contracts when the supply conditions and economic terms are in the best interests of the company. In 2025 and early 2026, we secured significant long-term committed contracts with two large hyperscalers, Microsoft and Meta. These multi-year, multi-billion dollar agreements offered attractive economics and financing terms, which enabled us to invest in and grow our core AI cloud business." [20-F FY2025, Item 4]
- "This enables us serve hundreds of customers, reduce concentration risk". "While the majority of our customer base is currently concentrated in the US, AI adoption is accelerating worldwide." [20-F FY2025, Item 5]
- FY2024 20-F wording (a year earlier): "While most of our revenue currently comes from AI-native startups, we believe we can supplement this business with demand from frontier AI labs and large technology companies." [20-F FY2024, Item 5]
- Industry verticals named: "physical AI, healthcare and life sciences and media and entertainment, as well as retail and e-commerce and financial services." [20-F FY2025, Item 4]

## 2. History: Yandex origins, 2024 sale of the Russian businesses, rename, relisting, financings

**Yandex origins and trading halt.**
- "Our founders began the development of our search technology in 1989 and launched the yandex website in 1997. In May 2011, the company's Class A shares were listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, under the ticker YNDX, and subsequently listed on the Moscow Exchange in June 2014. On February 28, 2022, Nasdaq and the New York Stock Exchange halted the trading in securities of a number of companies with material operations in Russia, including Yandex N.V." [20-F FY2023, Item 4]
- The four businesses retained after the sale (Nebius AI, Toloka AI, Avride, TripleTen) "have generated 1.8 billion rubles of revenue and incurred an Adjusted EBITDA loss of 24.9 billion rubles" for full-year 2023, and "currently employ approximately 1,300 people". The FY2023 20-F converts at RUB 89.6883 per $1.00 (Dec 31, 2023 CBR rate). (computed) RUB 1.8bn ≈ $20M at that rate, in line with the $20.9M FY2023 continuing revenue (incl. Toloka) later reported in the FY2024 20-F. "To date, these international businesses have been unable to obtain sufficient capital for their development as a result of significant limitations on the group's ability to transfer funds from the group's historically profitable businesses in Russia." [20-F FY2023, Items 3 and 4]
- Nebius AI in early 2024 (Yandex's own words): "an AI-centric cloud platform ... Nebius AI will seek to address the shortage of AI compute by being one of the largest providers of GPU capacity in Europe ... Nebius AI's proprietary data center in Finland ... houses Europe's most powerful commercially available supercomputer that ranks as the 16th among the 500 most powerful globally ... the Nebius team includes over 400 experts". [20-F FY2023, Item 4]

**The 2024 divestment (buyer, consideration, dates).**
- Agreement announced February 5, 2024 (share purchase agreement dated February 4, 2024, amended March 28, 2024) "with Closed-end Mutual Investment Fund 'Consortium First', a closed-end mutual investment fund under the trust management of JSC Solid Management, a joint stock company incorporated under the laws of the Russian Federation (the 'Purchaser')", to sell IJSC "Yandex" (the "Divestment HoldCo"). "the Company agreed to sell its entire equity interest in the Divestment HoldCo at a valuation of RUB 475 billion, to be satisfied in a combination of cash and the Company's own shares ... The consideration amount gave effect to a 50% discount to fair value of the assets to be divested, as required under Russian law." Shareholders approved in March 2024. [20-F FY2024, Item 10; Item 5]
- "The Divested Businesses accounted for more than 95% of the Group's consolidated revenues in 2023, and approximately 95% of the Group's consolidated assets and employees immediately prior to the Divestment." [20-F FY2025, F-pages, Note 1]
- First closing May 17, 2024: sold a 68% controlling stake for "the cash equivalent of RUB 237.5 billion less transaction expenses and other applicable adjustments, payable in Chinese Yuan ('CNH'), and 67.6 million of the Company's Class A shares. The Company received the cash payment in the amount of CNH 17.8 billion or approximately $2.4 billion on May 16, 2024." Second closing July 12, 2024: "cash of CNH 1.3 billion (approximately $0.2 billion) and 94.9 million Class A shares. The total amount of cash proceeds received in the transaction amounted to $2.6 billion, and the total number of consideration shares received was 162.5 million Class A shares." [20-F FY2025, F-pages, Note 1]

- Dutch tax on the share consideration: treated as a share repurchase subject to 15% dividend withholding tax unless "temporary investments"; a contingent liability of $180.9 was accrued in Q4 2024 "in respect of approximately 117 million repurchased Class A shares" and "In February 2025, the Group filed declarations for dividend withholding tax to the Dutch tax authorities and subsequently paid EUR173.7 ($181.5 at the exchange rate on the date of payment)." ## 10. Company-specific risk factors (not boilerplate), with the company's wording

- **Not profitable, capital-intensive, needs external capital.** "Our core business is capital-intensive and currently not profitable, and our ability to continue to operate and to grow will depend in large part on our ability to raise additional equity or debt financing ... We will likely also pursue secured financing arrangements, including asset-backed or other collateralized structures". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **GPU supply dependence on Nvidia.** "We currently rely on Nvidia for the GPU chips we use and on a limited number of other suppliers for other key components in our infrastructure." Risks listed include "the potential for binding price or purchase commitments with our suppliers at higher than market rates", "the prioritization by our suppliers of other customers", and "where our customers have contractually specified our use of certain suppliers for specified components". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Customer concentration / long-term contracts inexperience.** "while we have secured longer-term customer contracts with Meta and Microsoft, most of our customer engagements to date have been relatively short-term. As a result, we have limited experience in delivering large customer contracts." "These arrangements will require substantial upfront capital expenditures, long-term infrastructure commitments and ongoing operational support, and may expose us to performance obligations, service credits, penalties, termination rights, pricing adjustments and other contractual liabilities. Longer-term contracts also require us to forecast customer demand, utilization levels, costs and technological developments over extended periods, and our assumptions may prove inaccurate." [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Demand risk / efficiency risk.** "If there is insufficient customer demand to utilize the compute capacity we build, we would not realize the expected returns on our expansion efforts". "Technological developments in generative AI and inference, such as the development of AI models that require less computation power than earlier models, may result in decreased or differing demand for our offerings." "We may also encounter pricing pressure in respect of capacity for older generations of GPUs as newer generations are introduced." "A slowdown, deferral or reprioritization of AI-related customer spending, or the development of excess industry capacity, could materially adversely affect our revenue growth". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Power and data-center build risk.** "Any difficulties in identifying appropriate sites, entering into greenfield or build-to-suit arrangements or co-location agreements, or obtaining reliable power with sufficient capacity and on acceptable terms, will limit the growth of our revenues and anticipated profitability." "We expect the cost of power to remain volatile, unpredictable, and subject to inflationary pressures." Governments "would require data center operators to bear a greater share of the electricity generation, transmission, grid expansion, or related infrastructure costs ... including requirements to procure or construct dedicated or on-site power generation facilities". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Useful-life / depreciation estimate risk.** "Our results of operations may be adversely affected if we are not able to accurately estimate the value and useful lives of our long-term infrastructure assets". "Given our limited history of operations in respect of our current business, and the immature and evolving market in which we operate, our estimates of the useful lives of such assets may be subject to change." [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Convertible notes / dilution.** "We issued convertible notes in June and September 2025 and March 2026, which are convertible into up to an aggregate of approximately 66 million Class A shares. If and when such notes are convertible, and if the holders elect to convert them and to sell, it could create downward pressure on our share price." "We expect to issue additional equity or equity-linked securities from time to time, which may dilute the interest of our existing shareholders". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Founder control / dual-class / Controlled Company.** "As of March 31, 2026, our CEO, directors, employees and other pre-IPO shareholders together held Class A and Class B shares carrying approximately 59% of the voting power of our ordinary shares; and a family trust established by our CEO held Class B shares carrying approximately 52% of the voting power of our ordinary shares (representing an approximately 11% economic interest in our company)." "Because Mr. Volozh beneficially holds approximately 52% of the voting control ... Nebius is a 'Controlled Company' as defined by Rule 5615 of the Nasdaq Stock Market rules." Key-person: "The loss of existing key personnel, particularly Arkady Volozh, our founder and Chief Executive Officer". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Russian legacy.** "We divested all our group's businesses in Russia and related businesses in certain international markets in 2024. We do not have any customers, suppliers or business partners in Russia, own or hold any interest in any businesses or legal entities in Russia, or derive any revenues from Russia ... Nevertheless, despite these efforts, our legacy could create challenges for our businesses, including more protracted client on-boarding and 'know-your-customer' processes with counterparties and financial institutions." Dutch tax: "the Class A shares we received as partial consideration for the divestment are considered for Dutch tax purposes as having been repurchased, and therefore subject to dividend withholding tax ... it is possible that the Dutch tax authorities may challenge our assessment and determine that additional tax is due." [20-F FY2025, Item 3; 20-F FY2024, Item 3]
- **Sanctions / export controls.** "we are monitoring regulatory developments concerning export controls regarding the semiconductor industry and their impact on our sourcing of equipment ... we are monitoring a proposed rule from BIS, which if implemented as proposed, would impose requirements on Infrastructure-as-a-Service ('IaaS') providers and their foreign resellers to verify the identity and beneficial ownership of foreign person customers". [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Israel / geopolitics.** "Ongoing and potential escalation of geopolitical conflicts, including hostilities involving the United States, Israel, and Iran, could disrupt our operations and supply chains ... A portion of our operations, personnel, assets and business relationships are located in or connected to Israel." Headcount in Israel: 185 at Dec 31, 2025. [20-F FY2025, Items 3 and 6]
- **Internal-control material weaknesses (two, unremediated at Dec 31, 2025).** "Our controls related to fixed assets were not adequately designed and were not operating effectively. Specifically, we did not fully implement and ensure the effectiveness of the relevant controls and procedures over depreciation start dates, and timely reconciliation around the asset count process." and "We did not adequately and timely implement and maintain effective information technology general controls and have not consistently documented the execution of business process controls supporting revenue recognition in respect of our TripleTen business unit (representing approximately 10% of total revenues)". "We believe that these control deficiencies did not result in a misstatement". Two of three weaknesses identified at Dec 31, 2024 were remediated; the company "believe[s] we will make further significant progress in our remediation plan by the end of 2026." Auditor: Reanda Audit & Assurance B.V. (Netherlands). [20-F FY2025, Item 3 and Item 15; cover]
- **Avride capital needs.** "Our autonomous vehicles business unit, Avride, is a capital-intensive and early-stage business operating in a highly regulated sector. If it is unable to secure significant third-party financing and/or commercial partnerships, its ability to meet its business objectives may be limited." [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Foreign private issuer / Dutch law / PFIC.** Follows Dutch governance practices on quorum and proxies; "Dutch law does not grant appraisal rights"; Dutch public offer rules do not apply (no EU regulated-market listing); PFIC status cannot be assured. [20-F FY2025, Item 3]
- **Macro paragraph in the notes:** "Supply chain constraints affecting the procurement of high-performance GPUs and related semiconductor components remain a key operational challenge ... data center energy procurement and power grid capacity constraints". "During the year ended December 31, 2025, these factors outlined above did not have a meaningful impact on our financial results." [6-K Q2 2026 FS, Note 11; 20-F FY2025, F-pages, Note 11; 20-F FY2025, Item 5]
