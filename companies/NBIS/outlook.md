## 2. What management says about the core business

The CEO's own summary: "We build capacity ahead of contracts and use our multi-tenant cloud and our software stack to support our customers, AI-natives, Agentic AI leaders, neolabs and some of the most sophisticated enterprises. Our strategy is working. We choose when to sell, to whom we sell and on what terms, and how we finance everything" (multi-tenant: many customers share the same machines) [Q2 2026 call].

Two choices. Capacity is deliberately held back: "We could sell our entire 2027 capacity on these terms today. We are deliberately not doing so because we see higher value in retaining some capacity for immediate customer needs" [Q2 2026 letter, p.3]. And growth is meant to tilt toward software: "we expect our asset-light model, along with high-value services such as agentic and inference solutions, to contribute an increasing share of revenue while supporting even higher margins" [Q2 2026 call]. The long-run ambition, stated in May, is "our trajectory toward 20-30% EBIT margins" (EBIT: operating profit, after depreciation) [Q1 2026 letter].

## 3. Growth engines

**Mid-term dedicated-capacity contracts (the core).** Four Q2 "landmark deals" averaged "more than $1 billion each, and a yield of $20-25 million per megawatt" [Q2 2026 letter, p.3]. The capacity arrives "in late 2026, and will contribute primarily to 2027 revenue" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. Tell: RPO rising and the per-MW figure staying above $20 million.

**Short-term premium deals and auctions.** Three-to-six-month contracts "priced at a significant premium", with "a price opportunity in the $40-50 million per MW range"; the first was signed in early Q3, and a first auction cleared "15% above the highest price we ever charged before" [Q2 2026 letter, p.3; Q2 2026 call]. Size now: one deal, live "in Q4" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. Tell: further deals at or near that price.

**Asset-light partnerships.** Partners "finance, build and operate the facilities" while Nebius brings "the full-stack platform and the demand" [Q2 2026 call]. Size now: "still in very early stage", "dozens of inquiries", some Q2 revenue but no figure [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: a named partner and a disclosed MW or revenue number.

**Token Factory, inference and agents.** "Production inference workloads more than tripled in Q2", and Tavily "has grown to more than 2.5 million developers (from 1 million in February)"; no revenue is disclosed for either [Q2 2026 letter, p.6]. Tell: a first Token Factory revenue or token-volume figure.

**Own data centers.** The CFO expects "capacity coming online from our own data center to begin improving margins in the second half of next year" [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: AI-cloud adjusted EBITDA margin above Q2's 49.7%.

**Avride and TripleTen.** "Both are still early stage companies" and explain the gap between group and AI-cloud margins [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: their combined adjusted EBITDA loss ($49.5 million in Q2, computed) shrinking [6-K Q2 2026 FS, Note 15].

## 4. Guidance

The Q2 release contains no guidance, the letter restates no numbers ("We are reaffirming our full-year 2026 guidance across all metrics"), and no Q3 2026 guidance was given anywhere [Q2 2026 release; Q2 2026 letter, p.3; p.11; Q2 2026 call].

**FY2026.** "We continue to expect annualized run rate revenue of $7 billion to $9 billion, group revenue of between $3 billion and $3.4 billion, group adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 40%, and capital expenditures of $20 billion to $25 billion." [Q2 2026 call]

**Capacity.** "We now anticipate ending 2026 with 5 GW of contracted power, up from the +4 GW we indicated last quarter." [Q2 2026 letter, p.5] "we still expect to meet this guidance from 800 megawatts to 1 gigawatt of connected power this year." [Q2 2026 call] "The pace at which we bring capacity to market will accelerate as we plan to deploy more than 1 GW per year, starting in 2027." [Q2 2026 letter, p.5]

**Prepayments and financing.** "We expect to receive over $9 billion in customer prepayments in 2026." [Q2 2026 letter, p.10] "We are progressing on additional asset-backed financing and continue to evaluate corporate-level debt and additional financing instruments." [Q2 2026 call] (asset-backed: loans secured on specific GPUs or customer contracts.)

**2027.** No numbers: "we will provide a formal guidance later this year." [Q2 2026 call] The second Meta agreement is "on track to come online in early 2027", and Vera Rubin (NVIDIA's next GPU generation) deployments start "late this year or early next year" [Q2 2026 letter, p.5; Q2 2026 call].
