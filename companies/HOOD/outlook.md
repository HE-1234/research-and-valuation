## 2. What management says about the core business

"Robinhood exists to make everyone an owner", through three arcs: "being number one in active traders", "the leader in wallet share for the next generation" and "building the leading global financial ecosystem" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. The CFO's three progress measures: Net Deposits "at over a 20% growth rates"; "Rule of 40", where growth plus margin "has made us more than a Rule of 80 company for the past few years"; and "$100 million ARR businesses" (ARR: annualized revenue), now 13 [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. His "over $5 billion of annual revenue" is a run-rate (latest quarter times four); trailing (last twelve months) revenue was $4.9 billion [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 slides, p.4]. The "financial North Star remains the same - maximize earnings per share and free cash flow per share for shareholders over time", and "we believe we can 10x the business over the next 10 years" [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 call, p.19]. Nobody on the call mentioned interest rates, payment for order flow, credit losses or the June layoffs [Q2 2026 call, p.1–29].

## 3. Growth engines

**Prediction markets and Rothera.** Event contracts earned $156 million in Q2, 12% of revenue; Rothera, the Susquehanna joint-venture exchange, went live in June, handled 2.1 billion contracts and $17 million of that revenue, and "has rapidly become a top three DCM" (designated contract market, a regulated futures exchange) [Q2 2026 release, p.1] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2] [Q2 2026 slides, p.32] [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. Claim: "in the near- to medium-term more than the majority or a good portion will flow through Rothera" [Q2 2026 call, p.25]. Working if Rothera's share rises and the total holds outside sports seasons.

**Gold, Banking and the card.** 4.84 million Gold Subscribers; $3.1 billion of Banking deposits from 245 thousand customers; the Gold Card passed 1 million holders and $100 million of annualized revenue [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 slides, p.34]. Banking is "not yet fully integrated into the main app or the ecosystem" [Q2 2026 call, p.18]. Working if deposits, cardholders and Gold adoption rise.

**Trump Accounts.** "over 7M account sign ups with nearly $1.5 billion deposited" by July 29; $25 million of Q2 service revenue on work "contracted on a cost plus basis with a small margin" [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 slides, p.13] [Q1 2026 release, p.3]. Ambition: "tens of millions [of children]" [Q2 2026 call, p.10]. Working if sign-ups and deposits keep rising.

**Advisory and retirement.** Robinhood Strategies (its managed-portfolio service) has 310 thousand customers and $1.9 billion; TradePMR reached $50 billion; retirement assets are $34.5 billion [Q2 2026 slides, p.34] [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 release, p.3]. The adviser channel is "a good, durable, consistent source of Net Deposits" [Q2 2026 call, p.18]. Working if those assets outgrow markets.

**International.** International Funded Customers passed 1 million, including about 300 thousand from WonderFi; Singapore's licence arrived July 1 and the team is "getting close" to launch [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 slides, p.3] [Q2 2026 call, p.23]. Working if Singapore launches.

**Robinhood Chain, stock tokens and Earn.** Launched in July: "over $12 billion in DEX volume" (trading on decentralized exchanges; call-only), stock tokens (blockchain tokens tracking U.S. stocks) in "more than 120 countries", "over $200 million" in Earn (its stablecoin lending product) [Q2 2026 call, p.3] [Q2 2026 release, p.2]. Economics: "a few basis points" (hundredths of a percent) per transaction, half shared with Arbitrum (the network the chain is built on), a breakdown promised "when we start to get larger and it goes through a couple of quarters" [Q2 2026 call, p.24]. Working if a revenue figure appears.

**Active traders.** Legend (its desktop platform for active traders) passed $100 million of annualized revenue; "nearly 100 thousand customers have opened Agentic Trading accounts" (trading through AI agents; the call said "over 100,000") [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.14]. Working if volumes hold near Q2 records.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim. Expenses are the only quantified outlook; no revenue, margin, tax or capex guidance [Q2 2026 release, p.4].

- **Full-year 2026 expenses:** "we are lowering and tightening our 2026 outlook for Adjusted Operating Expenses and SBC to a range of $2.675 to $2.775 billion to reflect efficiencies we have captured, part of which were used to fund costs related to two new businesses, Rothera and WonderFi." [Q2 2026 release, p.4] The bridge adds "an additional $70 million of costs for two new businesses" against about $110 million of efficiencies [Q2 2026 slides, p.18].
- **Share count:** "we anticipate diluted share count will be roughly flat in 2026 as well" [Q2 2026 slides, p.19].
- **July:** "July average daily volumes, compared to a record Q2, are in a similar area for equities, options, and event contracts. And July Net Deposits are tracking towards the $4 billion area - and this does not yet include deposits into the Trump Accounts." [Q2 2026 call, p.5] "In terms of crypto, it's probably a little bit slower than what we saw in Q2"; take rates "in a similar area to what we saw for the Q2 average." [Q2 2026 call, p.26]
- **Net Deposits:** "our goal, as we've said, is 20% on an annualized basis ... If you look at year-to-date, including July, we're still well north of that 20%" [Q2 2026 call, p.17–18] (labelled Vladimir Tenev; the content is the CFO's).
