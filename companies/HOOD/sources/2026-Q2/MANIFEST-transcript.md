## Cross-check summary (details in notes §4)

- Every headline figure stated on the call matches the Q2 release: revenue $1.3 billion (+32%), Net Deposits $22 billion (28% annualized), Gold 4.8 million (17% attach), Adjusted EBITDA $741 million (57% margin, +35%), diluted EPS $0.62 (+48%), Adjusted OpEx and SBC $641 million, the 2026 outlook $2.675–$2.775 billion, Gold Card 1 million cardholders and $17 billion annualized purchase volume, Banking $3 billion deposits and about 40% direct deposit, Trump Accounts 7 million sign-ups and nearly $1.5 billion, over 1 million international accounts, stock tokens in more than 120 countries, 13 businesses at $100 million ARR.
- Consistent by our arithmetic: "nearly 1 million" Funded Customers added (28.4 million less 27.4 million from the Q1 release); year-to-date buybacks of 7.5 million shares for $664 million (Q1 $250 million / 3.1 million shares plus Q2 $414 million / 4.4 million shares).
- Differences to flag for the writer: (1) agentic-trading accounts "over 100,000" on the call versus "nearly 100 thousand" in the release; (2) new-customer Gold attach "about 40% to 50%" on the call versus "approximately 40 percent" in the release; (3) "no net dilution until our share price exceeds $300" is not a filed figure — the 8-K of 2026-06-25 gives a conversion price of about $174.42 and a capped-call cap of $237.8475, and roughly $300 follows only when the concurrent $290 million repurchase of 2,743,000 shares is netted (our inference); (4) "over $5 billion of annual revenue" is a run-rate reading (trailing twelve months by our arithmetic $4,932 million); (5) "nearly $400 billion" of assets versus $369 billion in the release.
- Call-only figures (unverifiable from cached sources, labelled in the notes): Robinhood Chain "over $12 billion in DEX volume", "fastest chain to get to 100 million transactions", "well north of 150 million transactions"; Robinhood Earn "over $200 million" at "7% of APY"; Rothera "top three DCM"; prediction-market users "closer to 2 million"; "Rule of 80"; Total Platform Assets "quadrupled" and Adjusted EBITDA "more than 8x'd" since IPO; July trends; Chain economics of "a few basis points" with "approximately half" to Arbitrum.
