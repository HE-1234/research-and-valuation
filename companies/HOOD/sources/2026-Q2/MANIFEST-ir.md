## Host quirks worth recording (pipeline lessons)

- `investors.robinhood.com` is Nasdaq/Notified IR hosting (Drupal, `gcs-web.com` origin), not Q4 Inc. The §12.3 Q4 feed URLs return 403 and should not be tried. HTML pages (`/financials/quarterly-results`, `/financials/monthly-metrics`, `/events/event-details/robinhood-q<N>-<YYYY>-earnings`) and `/static-files/<uuid>` documents are fetchable with the standard UA. Document filenames are in the `title` attribute of each anchor; UUIDs are not guessable, so read the listing pages.
- The Akamai edge fails intermittently per request: HTTP/2 returns curl (92) INTERNAL_ERROR within 0.1 s, or HTTP/1.1 hangs until timeout. The same URL then succeeds on a retry seconds later. Working recipe: `curl -4 --http2 --connect-timeout 15 --max-time 30`, retry up to four times with a 3 s pause; do not use long timeouts. `robinhood.gcs-web.com` cannot be used directly.
- Robinhood publishes a company transcript PDF per quarter on the quarterly-results page (tier 1), an Excel "Earnings Supplement" with nine-plus quarters of P&L, balance sheet and KPIs (the cleanest source for series), and monthly operating metrics (press release + table + Excel + dashboard) around mid-month, with the quarter-end month's table posted alongside earnings.
- Decks are Workiva PDFs with a full text layer; tabular appendix pages extract cleanly, chart pages scramble. Definitions pages are two-column and interleave.
