## 2. What management says about the core business

With no transcript, this rests on the release and slides. The CEO: "We delivered another strong quarter, with record results in two of our benchmark businesses - Ratings and Indices ... Post spin, we have a sharper focus on our four core divisions" [Q2 2026 release, p.1]. The three pillars stand: "ADVANCE Market Leadership — Continued investment in our global leadership in benchmarks"; "EXPAND High-Growth Adjacencies"; "AMPLIFY Enterprise Capabilities & AI — Bringing the full data estate to market responsibly through Kensho Data" [Q2 2026 slides, p.6]. Benchmarks are "~65% of S&P Global revenue" and "~80% of S&P Global adjusted operating profit" [Q2 2026 slides, p.7]. Neither document restates the November 2025 medium-term targets [8-K 2025-11-13].

## 3. Growth engines

**Ratings issuance.** Billed issuance rose 25% to $1,268 billion, "driven primarily by large issuances to finance AI infrastructure expansion, as well as strong M&A issuance" [Q2 2026 slides, p.8]. The division's range was raised to 5% to 8% [Q2 2026 supplemental, p.18]. Working if transaction revenue keeps pace with issuance.

**Indices.** ETF assets linked to the indexes reached $6,350 billion, derivatives volume rose 19%, and the range was raised to 12% to 14% [Q2 2026 slides, p.10] [Q2 2026 supplemental, p.18]. Working if asset-linked fees grow close to average assets.

**AI and Kensho Data.** The deck's indicators (ACV: annualized contract value): "1.6x ACV growth among MI AI clients vs. non-AI clients", "3.0x" in Energy, "5.4x API call volume growth compared to 1Q '26" (API: the software connection through which customers pull data), "500+ Active and trialing LLM-Ready API (LRA) customers" (data feeds built for AI models), "~60% of $100 million EDO 2027 savings program achieved" (EDO: the Enterprise Data Office, a name the deck does not expand [DEF 14A 2026, p.33]) [Q2 2026 slides, p.13]. Working if these keep rising and Market Intelligence subscription growth moves above 6%.

**Energy.** Recast Platts grew 4% and CERA (the research, data and conference half of Energy) 1%; the focus list names "Data centers; Power; CERA Titan; AI products", and the company agreed to buy datacenterHawk [Q2 2026 slides, p.18] [Q2 2026 slides, p.11] [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 2]. Working if organic growth returns to the 4.5% to 6% range in H2 after 2% in Q2 [Q2 2026 release, p.29, Exhibit 8].

**Market Intelligence, reshaped.** Kensho Data & Platforms grew 8% (4% excluding acquisitions) and Enterprise Solutions 3% (10% excluding divestitures) [Q2 2026 slides, p.19]. Working if subscription growth accelerates from 6% and the adjusted margin rises from 36.0% [Q2 2026 supplemental, p.14].
