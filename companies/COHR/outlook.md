## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame: AI data centers are replacing copper wiring with light, and Coherent makes the light. "AI runs on compute, but it scales on optical connectivity" [Q4 FY2026 call]. The claimed edge: "the breadth of our photonic technology portfolio, our manufacturing scale, and our significant US production footprint" [Q4 FY2026 call].

Supply, not demand, is the stated limit: "Indium Phosphide capacity continues to be our primary constraint" [Q4 FY2026 call]. On visibility: "Our order coverage through calendar 27 is exceptional. Customer orders now extend into calendar 28. Customer LTAs extend through the end of the decade" [Q4 FY2026 call]. No bookings or backlog dollars are disclosed anywhere [Q4 FY2026 release; 10-K FY2026, Note 3].

The financial frame is the May 2025 target model: non-GAAP gross margin ">42%", operating expenses "18%", operating margin ">24%" within "3 to 4 years", against 40.2%, 18.4% (computed) and 21.8% in Q4 [Investor Day 2025 deck, p.81; Q4 FY2026 release, Table 1]. The CFO: "once we get to our target of greater than 42%, we will no doubt raise the target" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Industrial was "roughly flat on a pro forma basis" (as if the sold businesses were excluded from both periods); "We expect growth to resume over the coming quarters, led by semiconductor capital equipment" [Q4 FY2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**800G and 1.6T transceivers, fed by internal indium phosphide lasers.** The segment was $5,274.6M in FY2026; datacenter alone is not disclosed [Q4 FY2026 release, Table 5]. Claims: internal laser output to double "by the end of the current quarter, 1 quarter ahead of our original plan", then "more than double ... again by the end of calendar 27"; June-quarter laser output was "about 80% more" than a year earlier, so datacenter growth this quarter should "exceed 80%" [Q4 FY2026 call; Q4 FY2026 slides, p.6]. Tell: the doubling restated; segment revenue and gross margin.

**Optical circuit switches.** Dollars not disclosed; "OCS revenue increased sequentially in Q4", the market is "more than $4 billion", and "we expect OCS revenue to grow significantly through fiscal 27" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: whether a dollar figure ever appears.

**Co-packaged optics and PhotonLink.** Coherent supplies the lasers, laser modules, fibers and connectors; "We also expect CPO to begin contributing to revenue growth in fiscal Q2" (the December 2026 quarter), scale-up applications "in the second half of calendar 27" [Q4 FY2026 call]. NVIDIA "has access to five additional Coherent product families related to co-packaged optics" [8-K 2026-03-02]. PhotonLink, an integrated-optics platform, launches September 21 with "initial revenue ... in our December quarter" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: December-quarter CPO revenue confirmed.

**Communications: links between data centers and multi-rail.** Communications revenue rose "56% year over year" in Q4 (transcript figure; the release does not split the segment); multi-rail transport, "4X fiber capacity", is sampling, with revenue "in the first half of calendar 27" [Q4 FY2026 call; Q4 FY2026 slides, p.6]. Tell: sequential (quarter-on-quarter) growth in Q1 confirmed on the call.

**Industrial materials for AI cooling.** A "proprietary thermodynamic material" for processor cooling, samples delivered, revenue "in the second half of calendar 27" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Industrial was $1,843.6M in FY2026 [Q4 FY2026 release, Table 5]. Tell: Industrial revenue turning up.

## 4. Guidance

**Q1 FY2027 (quarter ending September 30, 2026), verbatim.** "Revenue for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 is expected to be between $2.2 billion and $2.4 billion." "Gross margin percentage for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 is expected to be between 39.5% and 41.5% on a non-GAAP basis." "Total operating expenses for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 are expected to be between $400 million and $420 million on a non-GAAP basis." "Tax rate for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 is expected to be between 18% and 20% on a non-GAAP basis." "EPS for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 is expected to be between $1.85 and $2.05 on a non-GAAP basis." [Q4 FY2026 release, Business Outlook; Q4 FY2026 slides, p.9]

**Call only.** "we expect capital expenditures to increase sequentially again in Q1"; "we now expect to achieve our first quarter with over $3 billion of revenue by the end of fiscal 27"; "grow EPS significantly faster than revenue" [Q4 FY2026 call].

**Not guided.** No GAAP figures, no diluted share count, no capex dollars, no full-year figures of any kind, no segment guidance [Q4 FY2026 release, Business Outlook]. "The Company’s financial guidance will be limited to the comments on its public quarterly earnings call and the public business outlook statements contained in this press release" [Q4 FY2026 release].
