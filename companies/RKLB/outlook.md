## 2. What management says about the core business

Management describes "the space value chain in three key verticals": launch, satellites and components, and "space applications" [Q2 2026 call]. With Iridium, "Rocket Lab will become a self-launching Tier 1 space power, delivering critical communications capability to millions of users worldwide" (Tier 1: top-rank) [Q2 2026 call; Q2 2026 release]. Why buy rather than build: "It will take a decade or more to build out a constellation from scratch" (constellation: a fleet of satellites working as one network) [Q2 2026 call]. Iridium is "not the endpoint from an applications play ... it's really the starting point" [Q2 2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**Neutron.** No revenue yet; backlog unquantified, priced at "a $50 million to $55 million ASP" (average price) [Q2 2026 call]. Claims: pad delivery in Q4 2026; "the window for an end year launch is narrowing"; then "a 1, 3, 5 ramp"; adjusted EBITDA positive "in the quarter after" first launch [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: whether the next two releases keep the Q4 2026 pad date.

**Electron and HASTE.** Launch revenue was $44.6 million, down 30% from Q1 on a similar launch count, because two of the six missions were HASTE flights whose revenue had partly been booked in earlier quarters; only the 10-Q gives the count [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Q2 2026 call]. Claims: "13 launches this year with 100% mission success and on track to beat last year's" 21; a $266 million Space Force contract for 12 suborbital launches plus six options; "90+ launches" in backlog [Q2 2026 call; 10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Q2 2026 release]. Tell: launches per quarter; launch gross margin above 40%.

**Space Systems: satellite programs.** The segment made $189.5 million, up 94% year on year, "primarily due to spacecraft manufacturing growth and acquisitions" [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2]. Programs: Space Development Agency (SDA) Tranche 2 and Tranche 3 (the Tranche 3 award alone is $816 million for 18 satellites, delivery 2029; Tranche 2's size is not in the sources), the $397 million Space Force Flatellite contract (July 30), "more than $160 million" for three geostationary satellites (satellites that hover over one spot on Earth), and the late MDA program [10-K FY2025, Item 7; 10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Note 3; Q2 2026 release]. Claims: programs follow a "10, 40, 40, 10 kind of curve" over about four years; margins recover "beyond Q3" [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: segment gross margin.

**Components, including Mynaric.** Management calls components "a big moat"; Mynaric (laser terminals) added $13.2 million of revenue and a $13.2 million operating loss in Q2 after "an insolvency process" (bankruptcy) [Q2 2026 call; 10-Q Q2 2026, Note 4]. Claim: Mynaric margins will match the rest of the components business, "It may take us a few quarters to get there" [Q2 2026 call].

**Iridium (pending).** $54 per share, about $8.0 billion enterprise value, "expected to close in 2027" per the 10-Q and "mid-2027" per the call and deck [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Q2 2026 call; Iridium deck 2026-06-29, p.11]. Claim: "the table gets reset pretty significantly" because "that business generates pretty significant free cash flow" [Q2 2026 call]. Tell: the S-4 (merger registration document) and the Iridium shareholder vote.

## 4. Guidance

**Q3 2026 (quarter ending September 30, 2026).** "For the third quarter of 2026, Rocket Lab expects: Revenue between $250 million and $265 million. GAAP Gross Margins between 29% and 31%. Non-GAAP Gross Margins between 35% and 37%. GAAP Operating Expenses between $143 million and $149 million. Non-GAAP Operating Expenses between $121 million and $127 million. Interest Income, net $21 million. Adjusted EBITDA loss of between $17 million and $23 million. Basic Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding of 641 million, including approximately 41 million of Series A Convertible Participating Preferred Shares." "Stock-based compensation is currently expected to range from $18 million to $20 million in Q3 2026." [Q2 2026 release]

The gross-margin guide is below Q2's actual 36.1% GAAP and 41.5% non-GAAP; the slides call it "driven by weaker mix within several of our product lines" (mix: which products make up the quarter's sales), and the CFO said the margins "are accounting for a shift in mix within our Space Systems business, and we expect a beneficial remixing impact on gross margins as we look beyond Q3" [Q2 2026 release; Q2 2026 slides, p.33; Q2 2026 call]. Cash: "consistent with prior quarters, we expect negative non-GAAP free cash flow in the third quarter to remain at elevated levels" [Q2 2026 call].

**Full year 2026:** none in the release, slides or call [Q2 2026 release; Q2 2026 call]. The transcript's Q3 revenue sentence misreads "second quarter"; the range above is from the release [Q2 2026 call].
