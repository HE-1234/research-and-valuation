## 2. What management says about the core business

Scale turning into cash: Gross Bookings grew "22% year-on-year to more than $58 billion, above the high end of our guidance and marking our fourth consecutive quarter above 20% growth" [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. The CEO covers three things: the core business growing with "operating leverage" (profit growing faster than sales), Delivery Hero to roughly double the markets with both rides and delivery, and autonomous vehicles as "one of the largest opportunities in Uber's history" [Q2 2026 call, p.3].

On the falling cut: about 4 of the 5 points of Mobility Revenue Margin decline are the UK accounting change, "an optical impact", and the rest "deliberate investments ... in our lower-cost offerings" [Q2 2026 call, p.11]. On AVs, "the long-term winner in AVs will be determined by who can commercialize the technology at global scale" and "first-party and third-party distribution models will coexist" [Q2 2026 remarks, p.5].

## 3. Growth engines

**Mobility: premium, low-cost and sparse markets.** $29.0 billion of Q2 bookings, up 20% cc; US trips and bookings "accelerated" [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 remarks, p.1]. Suburban markets grow "~1.5x faster than denser markets while generating higher margins" [Q2 2026 remarks, p.2]. Tell: Mobility cc growth holding at 20%.

**Delivery: grocery and retail, Türkiye.** $27.5 billion, up 25% cc, with Grocery & Retail at "$15 billion in annualized Gross Bookings, growing roughly 40% YoY" [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 remarks, p.2]. Getir closed in July, but lapping Trendyol GO (comparing against a year-ago quarter that already included it) makes Q3 "a headwind to Delivery reported growth on a net basis" while "our organic Delivery business is accelerating quite nicely" (organic: excluding acquisitions) [Q2 2026 remarks, p.8] [Q2 2026 call, p.15]. Tell: Delivery cc growth against 25%.

**Uber One.** Members passed 50 million in April, "up 50% YoY", and generate "more than 70% of Delivery Gross Bookings, up roughly 20 percentage points in just two years" [Q1 2026 remarks, p.4] [Q2 2026 remarks, p.3]. Tell: both figures rise.

**Advertising.** "Advertising exceeded a $2.5 billion annualized revenue run-rate this quarter, still growing roughly 50% YoY" (run-rate: the quarter's pace times four) [Q2 2026 remarks, p.4]. Tell: the run-rate reappears and rises.

**Autonomous vehicles.** Live in 7 cities, "on track for as many as 15 by year-end"; "approximately 120,000 vehicles" committed by partners; "over $10 billion of capital over the coming years" [Q2 2026 remarks, p.5]. Tell: the live-city count; whether a profit drag is sized.

**Freight.** $1.6 billion, up 25%, "volumes growing at their fastest pace since the Transplace acquisition", still a $24 million segment loss [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 remarks, p.3] [Q2 2026 release, p.3]. Tell: the loss narrowing toward zero.

**International, including Delivery Hero.** Category position improved "across all 10 of our largest international markets"; the pending Delivery Hero offer would take Uber to 99 markets [Q2 2026 remarks, p.2] [Delivery Hero release, p.1]. Tell: the offer document published and the bridge refinanced.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim. The remarks state "All growth rates reflect YoY growth and are on a constant-currency basis, unless otherwise noted" [Q2 2026 remarks, p.9]. No revenue, capex or full-year Gross Bookings guidance is given.

- **Q3 2026:** "Gross Bookings of $58.25 billion to $60.25 billion, representing growth of 18% to 22% YoY on a constant-currency basis. Our outlook assumes a roughly 1 percentage-point currency headwind to total reported YoY growth. Non-GAAP EPS of $0.84 to $0.88, representing growth of 28% to 35% YoY. Our outlook translates to Adjusted EBITDA of $2.86 billion to $2.96 billion." [Q2 2026 release, p.1]
- **Full year 2026:** "We expect 2026 stock-based compensation of $1.95-2.10 billion, narrowed from our prior outlook of $1.95-2.15 billion." "We expect 2026 Non-GAAP depreciation and amortization expense of $450-550 million." "We expect our full-year 2026 Non-GAAP ETR to be in the range of 22-24%. We expect our full-year 2026 cash tax rate to be in the mid-to-high single digits. As a reminder, over the next few years, we expect our cash tax rate to be in the range of 10-15%" [Q2 2026 remarks, p.9] (ETR: effective tax rate)
- **UK accounting:** "We expect this accounting headwind to revenue and tailwind to cost of revenue to persist at roughly the same magnitude for the remainder of 2026." [Q2 2026 remarks, p.7]
- **US Mobility:** "we expect the US to accelerate through this year ... We continue to hold our expectation for the remainder of the year." [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
- **Insurance:** "legislation that we expect will generate significant recurring savings beginning in 2027." [Q2 2026 remarks, p.7]
- **AVs:** "AVs are live on Uber in 7 cities, on track for as many as 15 by year-end." "we expect to commit over $10 billion of capital over the coming years to bring AVs to market at scale." [Q2 2026 remarks, p.5]
- **Buybacks:** "We expect to remain active in share repurchases each quarter, and expect to steadily return to a more normalized level of activity ... We remain committed to durably reducing our share count." [Q2 2026 remarks, p.8]
- **Delivery Hero:** "closing targeted for the second half of 2027. We expect our gross leverage to remain below two times while preserving a strong investment grade balance sheet. We expect the transaction to be accretive to Non-GAAP EPS upon closing, with high-single-digit accretion by year three as synergies are realized." [Q2 2026 remarks, p.8] (gross leverage: debt relative to yearly earnings; accretive: adds to earnings per share; synergies: gains from combining.) Integration: "assuming we close in the second half of 2027, we'll spend 2028 on planning and development before executing the primary migrations in 2029" [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
