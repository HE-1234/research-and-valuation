## 2. What management says about the core business

Ready's frame: "Pinterest helps people discover what they want and then go do it in the real world"; revenue lags that intent: "clicks to advertisers are up 5x over the last three-year period, but we certainly didn't find 5x revenue" [Q2 2026 call, p.1] [Q2 2026 call, p.9]. The answer is "actionability" and Pinterest Assistant, "available to the vast majority of U.S. users as of the end of July", which Ready says is not a "build it now and monetize it later" product [Q2 2026 call, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.15]. On AI: "AI by itself does not have style or taste. People have style and taste" [Q2 2026 call, p.16]. On international: "applying our UCAN playbook internationally" (UCAN: U.S. and Canada) [Q2 2026 call, p.4].

## 3. Growth engines

**U.S. and Canada (UCAN) go-to-market (sales approach).** Revenue growth accelerated five points to 18%, credited to "clear seller accountability", returning large-retailer spend and growth "across mid-market, managed SMB, and emerging verticals" (SMB: small and medium-sized businesses; verticals: industry categories) [Q2 2026 slides, p.4] [Q2 2026 call, p.3] [Q2 2026 call, p.11]. Working if: UCAN growth stays in the high teens on ARPU, not users.

**International monetization.** Europe grew 12% and Rest of World 38%, down from 27% and 59% in Q1, blamed on go-to-market changes, tough comparisons and European regulatory pressure on cross-border retailers; a new international sales head is in place and third-party demand is being tested there [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q1 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.7] [Q2 2026 call, p.15]. Working if: Europe and RoW ARPU growth re-accelerates [Q2 2026 call, p.15].

**Performance+, shopping and the measurement-bidding pilot.** About 30% of lower-funnel revenue ran through Performance+ as of Q1 (no Q2 update) [Q2 2026 call, p.11]. A pilot linking large advertisers' measurement systems to Pinterest's bidding "contributed to our outperformance in Q2" and expands to "a small number of additional advertisers" in Q3; shopping-ad delivery and Smart Assembly (creative for advertisers without catalogs) were also upgraded [Q2 2026 call, p.9] [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. Working if: the 30% figure is updated and rises, and the pilot moves past "small number".

**tvScientific and connected TV.** Acquired February 2026 for $465.1 million; it lifted UCAN revenue and cost of revenue; its revenue is not disclosed [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 3] [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. "In 2027, we expect to fully integrate tvScientific capabilities directly into Pinterest Performance+" [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. Working if: a CTV revenue or advertiser figure appears.

**Pinterest Assistant.** Live for most U.S. users since late July; memory and product comparisons being added; no usage figures [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Working if: usage figures are disclosed and U.S. and Canada MAUs rise.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim; no full-year revenue guidance was given.

- **Q3 revenue:** "For Q3 2026, we expect revenue to be in the range of $1,190 million to $1,210 million, representing 13% - 15% growth year over year, which assumes a modest headwind from foreign exchange based on current spot rates." [Q2 2026 release, p.3]
- **Sequential (quarter-to-quarter) effects (FX: currency swings; Prime Day: a mid-year retail sale):** FX turns from "a 1-point tailwind in Q2" to "a modest headwind in Q3"; the Prime Day shift into Q2 becomes "a roughly 0.5-point headwind to Q3"; Q2's "nearly 1-point benefit from World Cup-related spend" "will not repeat in Q3." [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
- **Q3 Adjusted EBITDA:** "we expect Q3 Adjusted EBITDA to be in the range of $335 million to $355 million." [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
- **Q3 cost of revenue:** "We anticipate Q3 non-GAAP cost of revenue expense to be roughly flat versus Q2 2026 due to the accelerated recognition of certain contractual benefits in Q3 associated with our recently executed multiyear infrastructure agreement." [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
- **Second-half cost of revenue:** "we continue to expect modest headwinds from cost of revenue as a percentage of revenue in the second half as a result of investments in areas such as additional GPU capacity, as well as the impact from the inclusion of tvScientific." [Q2 2026 call, p.5–6]
- **Full-year margin (slides and call only):** "we now expect full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA margins of approximately 30%, versus our prior expectation of approximately 29%." [Q2 2026 call, p.6] [Q2 2026 slides, p.4]
- **Full-year GAAP profit:** "As in recent years, we also expect to be profitable on a full-year GAAP net-income basis." [Q2 2026 call, p.12]
- **Stock-based compensation:** "starting in Q3, we do expect both stock-based compensation expense dollars and the year-over-year growth in stock-based compensation to step down versus Q2." [Q2 2026 call, p.12]
- **Long-term margin:** "consistent with the long-term range we gave at Investor Day a few years ago of 30% to 34%"; "no specific commentary yet on 2027." [Q2 2026 call, p.14]
