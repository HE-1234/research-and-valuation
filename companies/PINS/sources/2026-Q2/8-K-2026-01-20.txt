Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure

Effective January 20, 2026, the Company has appointed Claude (Lee) Brown to serve as Chief Business Officer of the Company. In this new role, Mr. Brown will oversee the Company's global sales, content, customer-facing operations and advertising product marketing.

Mr. Brown most recently served as Chief Revenue Officer of DoorDash, Inc., overseeing all revenue-generating functions. Prior to that, Mr. Brown served for six years as Vice President, Global Head of Advertising at Spotify Technology S.A. In other experience, Mr. Brown served as Chief Revenue Officer of BuzzFeed, Inc., and held senior leadership positions at Tumblr Inc., Groupon, Inc., and Yahoo! Inc.
