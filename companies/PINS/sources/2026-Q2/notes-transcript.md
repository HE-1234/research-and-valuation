## 2. Vision and direction

- What Pinterest is for: "At the center of our strategy is a simple idea. Pinterest helps people discover what they want and then go do it in the real world." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.1]
- Brand line: "The best thing you can find online is a reason to go offline." / "People come here with intent. They find inspiration that is personalized for them, refine their style and taste, and can take action directly from our platform." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.1]
- Three strategic priorities: "First, continuing to build a differentiated visual search, discovery, and shopping experience for users. Second, keeping AI at the core of everything we do, from powering the user experience to our ads platform and our internal operations. And third, accelerating monetization through improved go-to-market and measurement capabilities." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.1]
- Human curation as the differentiated AI input: "The human curation that is so unique to Pinterest is the highly differentiated signal that trains our AI to make exceptionally relevant recommendations to users." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.1] / "Stated very simply, AI by itself does not have style or taste. People have style and taste." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.16]
- "We've transformed Pinterest into an AI-powered shopping assistant. Effectively, every Pin a user sees is personalized and served by AI." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Taste Graph as the asset: "Our scale and the unique human curation that occurs on our platform have fueled our proprietary Taste Graph ... We have a uniquely valuable signal of human taste and curation at scale. This enables us to train our AI with a deep understanding of taste, context, and intent that gets more personalized every time a user comes back. This powerful feedback loop is what helps users discover what they want before they have the exact words, brand, or product in mind. That's why so many commercial journeys begin on Pinterest." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Unbranded intent as the advertiser pitch: "Today, over 96% of text-based searches on our platform are unbranded. For instance, 'cool running shoes' or 'new fall wardrobe ideas.' This is a great moment for marketers to meet prospective customers when they have clear commercial intent, but haven't yet decided what to buy." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Actionability, the through-line: "Stepping back, this is the next iteration of work we've been doing for years: taking the discovery and intent that already exists on Pinterest and making it even more actionable for users, helping them move seamlessly from dreaming to deciding, doing, and buying." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- AI moving to the foreground: "To handle this part of the commercial journey, we're bringing AI further into the foreground of the user experience with our AI conversational layer, Pinterest Assistant." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Model strategy: "Our approach to model deployment includes our own compact, fit-for-purpose models built for Pinterest-specific use cases, and suitable open-source models post-trained in our own environment within our secure cloud infrastructure." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2] / "we take a model-agnostic approach. We use the right model for the task, whether that is one of our in-house compact, fit-for-purpose models, an open-source model that is post-trained on our own data, or, in some very limited cases, a selected closed third-party model." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.13] / open source gives "three important advantages: cost, customization, and control." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- Competitive view of search: "On the competitive landscape, we have long believed search will continue to fragment, with a small number of horizontal, general-purpose platforms, but more vertical-specific experiences that solve distinct consumer needs." / "As AI becomes more widely available and open-source models continue to improve, we believe there can be many winners, particularly in vertical-specific search." / "You're also seeing general-purpose AI applications discover what those of us that have built much larger platforms have known for a long time: it's very hard to be all things to all people. You've seen them pull back from many of their vertical-specific experiences." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.8]
- Focus: "At Pinterest, we're very focused on visual search, particularly around shopping, and we're using AI to make the experience more actionable across the shopping journey." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.8]
- Monetization thesis (revenue lags engagement): "we're still a long way from having fully monetized all the commercial intent that already exists on our platform." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.9] / "clicks to advertisers are up 5x over the last three-year period, but we certainly didn't find 5x revenue." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.9]
- Monetization design principle: "First, it has been about using AI for highly commercial moments where monetization is baked in right from the start. Second, it has been about the cost efficiency we have discussed with compact models and using open-source or open-weight models." / "But we think about monetization from the beginning of our product design." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.15]
- Direct relationship with users: "Importantly, we have a direct relationship with our users. One hundred percent of our reported users are logged in, and 85% come to Pinterest directly through our mobile app, meaning we are not heavily reliant on third parties for traffic." — Julia Donnelly, CFO [Q2 2026 call, p.4] / "We are not dependent on others for that traffic. Pinterest is a destination that is beloved by its users, and I think that has proven to be quite durable as well." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.16]
- Positive platform: "As the global conversation around online safety and youth well-being continues to grow, we believe that foundation matters more than ever. ## 3. Growth engines as management named them

### 3a. User growth, engagement and Gen Z
- What it is: record and double-digit MAU growth, led by Gen Z. "Q2 marked our 12th consecutive quarter of record users and our 11th straight quarter of double-digit user growth. In our core UCAN market, users grew 4%. We're also winning significantly with the next generation of users, as Gen Z continues to be our largest and fastest-growing cohort, representing over half of our user base." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.1–2]
- Size: 640 million global MAUs, +11%; UCAN 106 million, +4%; Europe 157 million, +8%; Rest of World 377 million, +15%. — Julia Donnelly, CFO [Q2 2026 call, p.4–5]
- Engagement scale: "more than 80 billion monthly searches on Pinterest. The vast majority of searches are visual, and over half are commercial. There are also more than 16 billion boards created on our platform." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]; "hundreds of billions of unique connections across our Taste Graph" — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.8]
- Engagement depth in UCAN (no figure): "in UCAN, searches and boards created continued to grow faster than users in Q2." — Julia Donnelly, CFO [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- Gen Z evidence: "We have previously cited an Adobe study, independent of us, that found 39% of Gen Z now thinks of Pinterest as a first place to go to search. The reason for that is more than 70% of Gen Z sees Pinterest as more personalized." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.16]
- Management's claim: durability rests on "12 straight quarters of record-high users" driven by the same things ("a highly shoppable platform with great personalization and AI powering great recommendations") plus human curation. — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.15–16]

### 3b. Pinterest Assistant (AI conversational layer for users)
- What it is: "our AI conversational layer, Pinterest Assistant" for "later-stage research questions" ("Do these shoes run true to size?"), "woven throughout the user experience ... with contextual entry points and prompt suggestions". — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Rollout: available to "the vast majority of U.S. users as of the end of July". — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Basis: "built on our Taste Graph", "visual-first", with memory and conversational history beginning. — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
- Performance claim: "When we leverage open-source models, such as with Pinterest Assistant, we are seeing superior performance for our use cases compared with closed third-party models because we are able to post-train open models on our highly unique data." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.2]; "our latest multimodal models ... outperform closed models by 34 percentage points on the relevancy of shopping recommendations." — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.16]
- Monetization: "it has monetization built in right from the start" because it sits inside the existing, already-monetized app. — Bill Ready, CEO [Q2 2026 call, p.15]
- Usage numbers: not disclosed on the call. [Q2 2026 call]
