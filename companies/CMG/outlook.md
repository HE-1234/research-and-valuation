## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is "Recipe for Growth", five pillars: the core (operations and value), technology and Rewards, brand and menu innovation, talent, and international expansion "through proven company-owned and partner-operated markets as well as strategic new regions" [Q2 2026 call, l.25]. The core comes first: "that starts with running great restaurants. For our guests, that means delicious food, generous portions, exceptional throughput and exceptional hospitality" (throughput: how many orders a line serves in a set time) [Q2 2026 call, l.26]. Value "isn't just about discounting and price point. It's about convenience. It's about execution. It's about menu innovation" [Q2 2026 call, l.68]. The vision is "building Chipotle into an iconic global brand" with "at least 7,000 restaurants" in North America (spoken; the 10-K's goal is "7,000 restaurants in the U.S. and Canada") [Q2 2026 call, l.47] [Q2 2026 call, l.52] [10-K FY2025, Item 1A (Our failure to effectively manage and support our growth)]. The deck's undated longer-term goals are "$4+ MILLION AUVs", "7,000+ N.A. RESTAURANTS", a restaurant-level operating margin "APPROACHING 30%" and openings growing "8-10% per year" [Q4 2025 slides, p.7] [Q4 2025 slides, p.9].

On the quarter, the release headline was "CHIPOTLE RAISES FULL YEAR COMPARABLE SALES GUIDANCE ON STRONG Q2 MOMENTUM"; the CFO's caution was that "trends have been softer in recent weeks" and Q3 "is the toughest lap that we have this year" (the comparison against last year's quarter) [Q2 2026 release, Headline] [Q2 2026 call, l.50] [Q2 2026 call, l.79]. The 10-Q puts it in writing: "low-single-digit headwinds to our comparable restaurant sales trends... as customers respond to, among other things, recent U.S. food safety concerns and geopolitical developments"; the CEO said "we are impacted from a sales perspective, but we're not involved in the cyclospora conversation today" (cyclospora is a food-borne parasite behind an industry outbreak) [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2 (Sales Trends)] [Q2 2026 call, l.84].

## 3. Growth engines

**New restaurants.** The written plan is 350–370 openings including 10–15 partner-operated; the CEO said "approximately 350 restaurants this year with about 80%, including a Chipotlane", and 149 company-owned opened in H1 [Q2 2026 release, Outlook] [Q2 2026 call, l.43] [Q2 2026 release, Headline] [Q1 2026 release, Headline]. The CFO's unit economics, spoken only: "New restaurant productivity has remained stable in the 80% range, and year 2 cash-on-cash returns continue to be around 60%" (yearly restaurant cash profit as a share of the build cost) [Q2 2026 call, l.52]. Working if: about 200 company-owned openings arrive in H2 (computed) and trailing average restaurant sales stop falling.

**Throughput and the equipment package (HEEP).** New back-of-house equipment "is now installed in more than 1,000 restaurants, and we still expect to reach approximately 2,000 restaurants by year-end", with the whole fleet "sometime in 2027"; the gains are "translating into hundreds of basis points of improvement in comparable sales" (a basis point is a hundredth of a percentage point) [Q2 2026 call, l.28] [Q2 2026 call, l.101] [Q2 2026 slides, p.6]. Working if: the count reaches about 2,000 and transactions stay positive while the rollout continues.

**Menu innovation.** Returning Honey Chicken reached an "attachment rate over 25%" (share of orders that included it); the pipeline holds "2 additional limited time protein options" in H2 [Q2 2026 slides, p.7] [Q2 2026 call, l.38]. Working if: transactions stay positive in a quarter with a protein launch.

**Rewards and digital.** Digital is 38.3% of sales; Rewards relaunched in mid-April, and "only about 20% of the transactions scan for Rewards compared to nearly 90% of owned digital transactions" (spoken) [Q2 2026 release, Results] [Q2 2026 call, l.32] [Q2 2026 call, l.33]. A "frictionless in-restaurant Rewards experience beginning in August" will award points at payment without a scan [Q2 2026 call, l.34]. Working if: digital share holds above 38% and the in-store scan rate is reported rising.

**New occasions.** Catering pilots plus Build-Your-Own Chipotle (a family meal kit) "represent 2% to 3% of sales today" and are headed for "a national launch in 2027" (spoken) [Q2 2026 call, l.40]. Working if: the 2027 date holds on later calls.

**Pricing.** Menu price added 1.6% in Q2 [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2 (Revenue table)] and rises to "the mid-2% range in Q3", landing "near the high end of the 1% to 2% range" for the year; Q3 inflation of "about 3%" should be matched by price "from Q4 forward" (the Q3 and full-year figures spoken only) [Q2 2026 call, l.51] [Q2 2026 call, l.143] [Q2 2026 call, l.144]. Working if: restaurant-level operating margin stops falling year over year by Q4.

**International partners.** Alshaya runs 15 restaurants in the Middle East; Alsea opened Monterrey on July 16 with Mexico City in 2027; SPC Group opens Seoul "this year" and Singapore "in early 2027" [Q2 2026 release, Supplemental Financial and Other Data] [Q2 2026 call, l.45] [IR release 2026-07-13 (Mexico)]. Company-owned Europe posted "high single-digit comp sales growth" in each country (spoken) and stays owned: "you can think about the rest of the world as partner operated" [Q2 2026 call, l.44] [Q2 2026 call, l.91]. Working if: the partner count climbs each quarter and the 10–15 partner openings for 2026 are met.
