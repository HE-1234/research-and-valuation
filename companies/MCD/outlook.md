## 2. What management says about the core business

Kempczinski declared the six-year Accelerating the Arches strategy a success: "We've grown systemwide sales roughly $40 billion and operating income by over $3 billion" (spoken figures, not in the release, and a different $40 billion from the release's trailing loyalty sales) [Q2 2026 call, l.24]. "The McDonald's brand remains one of one in our industry" [Q2 2026 call, l.25]. On the weak U.S. quarter: "We don't have a strategy problem. We simply didn't execute", with the failures in "3 buckets": uneven execution of the new value menu, restaurant teams "overwhelmed by too many deployments", and marketing that "didn't deliver against expectations" [Q2 2026 call, l.29] [Q2 2026 call, l.30]. Borden on price: "We have been consistent. We will not get beaten on value" [Q2 2026 call, l.36]. The successor strategy, McDonald's > NEXT, is meant to "earn the right to be more customers' first choice", to be "meaningfully self-funded" by restaurant productivity, and is "not a remodel program" [Q2 2026 call, l.57] [Q2 2026 call, l.49] [Q2 2026 call, l.173]. Detail was deferred to an Investor Day on September 23 in Chicago, which had not happened at this report's cutoff; management said it would cover NEXT, refranchising (selling company-run restaurants to franchisees) and the G&A (overhead) outlook, marketing, the U.S. value plan, franchisee investment and beverages [Q2 2026 call, l.57] [Q2 2026 call, l.44] [Q2 2026 call, l.89] [Q2 2026 call, l.159] [Q2 2026 call, l.173] [Q2 2026 call, l.205].

## 3. Growth engines

**New restaurants.** 46,028 at June 30, up 672 in six months (computed); the 2026 plan is about 2,600 openings and 2,100 net additions; the 50,000 target moved from "end of 2027" to "in 2028" because of "the current pressured consumer environment, coupled with the cumulative inflationary impact on development costs" [Q2 2026 supplement, p.13] [10-K FY2025, Note: Summary of Significant Accounting Policies] [10-Q Q2 2026, Outlook] [Q2 2026 call, l.44]. Working if: about 1,430 net additions arrive in the second half (computed) and the 2028 date holds.

**Digital and loyalty.** Nearly 220 million 90-day active users and $40 billion of trailing loyalty sales, against 2027 targets of 250 million and $45.0 billion [Q2 2026 release, p.1] [10-K FY2025, MD&A (Growth Pillars)]. The U.S. fix leans on it: "national digital flash offers" from the week after the call and "more personalized digital offerings" [Q2 2026 call, l.35]. Working if: users climb toward 250 million and loyalty sales keep growing over 20%.

**Delivery.** Offered from nearly 41,000 restaurants; the CEO put it at "more than $20 billion in annual systemwide sales" (spoken, not in the release); the 10-K target is 30% of delivery sales through the app by end-2027 [10-K FY2025, MD&A (Growth Pillars)] [Q2 2026 call, l.26]. Working if: the app share is updated; there is no quarterly metric.

**Drive-thru.** Nearly 29,000 locations; in Q2 U.S. "service times went up, satisfaction scores went down" [10-K FY2025, MD&A (Growth Pillars)] [Q2 2026 call, l.30]. Working if: service times stop being named as a problem; no metric is disclosed.

**Beverages.** A new platform launched in May in the U.S., Canada and Germany, Australia in mid-July, with Red Bull Energizers due in the U.S. "in the coming weeks"; management claims U.S. sales "ahead of plan" and checks "up about 50%" on those orders (spoken; no beverage sales are disclosed) [Q2 2026 call, l.50] [Q2 2026 call, l.51] [Q2 2026 call, l.203] [Q2 2026 call, l.204]. Working if: more markets launch and a beverage number appears.

**Chicken.** McCrispy is in nearly all major markets; Australia and Germany were "gaining chicken share in the quarter" [10-K FY2025, MD&A (Growth Pillars)] [Q2 2026 call, l.38]. An analyst named U.S. chicken quality a top opportunity; management did not respond [Q2 2026 call, l.66]. Working if: chicken is named as a comparable-sales driver.

**U.S. value.** McValue now has $5 Meal Deals, Extra Value Meals at "15% discount or better" and, since late April, a "10 items for under $3" menu that "has not delivered": only "60% to 65%" of restaurants price it as recommended [Q2 2026 call, l.70] [Q2 2026 call, l.71] [Q2 2026 call, l.73] [Q2 2026 call, l.74]. Marketing money moves to Extra Value Meals in the second half and franchisee meetings follow in early September [Q2 2026 call, l.36] [Q2 2026 call, l.88]. Working if: U.S. comparable sales turn positive with guest counts no longer described as negative.

## 4. Guidance

The written outlook is full-year 2026 only; it is identical in the 10-Q and the supplement and unchanged in substance from Q1, except that the Q2 version adds the 50,000-unit target with a 2028 date, which the Q1 outlook did not mention [10-Q Q2 2026, Outlook] [Q2 2026 supplement, p.11] [Q1 2026 supplement, p.8]. No comparable-sales, revenue, operating-income or EPS guidance was given for Q3 or the year, in writing or on the call. 4. **The Q3 2026 Outlook still says "approximately 2,100 net restaurant additions in 2026".** [10-Q Q2 2026, Outlook]
