## 2. What management says about the core business

The CEO: "Our core message is simple: strong demand for our products continues to outpace our growing supply, our design and manufacturing execution is improving, and the operating discipline we put in place fifteen months ago is beginning to show tangible results" [Q2 2026 call, p.1]. He counts "the seventh consecutive quarter of exceeding our financial expectations" and builds on scarcity: "The industry is facing one of the most severe supply constraints in its history", shortages that "will persist for the foreseeable future" [Q2 2026 call, p.1].

Stated priorities: "leverage our X86 computing franchise to strengthen our product leadership and establish Intel Foundry as a world-class wafer and packaging foundry business" [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. Nearer term the priority is factory output; a deeper Google Cloud collaboration is meant to make Intel "AI-first" in how it runs itself [Q2 2026 call, p.2; p.1]. The remarks never mention the US government stake, Altera, Mobileye, dividends, buybacks, tariffs or China [Q2 2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**Server CPUs for AI (DCAI).** $6.3 billion a quarter at a 40% margin [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 2; Item 2]. Claim: AI inference (running trained AI models) needs more general-purpose CPUs, so "our core server CPU franchise is growing faster than ever", with industry unit growth "strong double-digit" through 2027 and "momentum extending into 2028" [Q2 2026 call, p.1; p.5]. Tell: server volume and price change in the 10-Q.

**Client and "physical AI" (CCPG).** $8.9 billion a quarter; AI PCs are "two thirds of our client revenue mix" and edge deployments (chips in factories and devices, not data centers) "roughly 10 percent of CCPG revenue" [Q2 2026 call, p.4]. Claim: "the Edge and Physical AI opportunity is likely to at least match the client TAM overtime" (TAM: the size of the whole market), tempered by "We still have work to do" in that ecosystem [Q2 2026 call, p.4; p.2]. Tell: the edge share of CCPG revenue restated and rising.

**Foundry outside customers and Intel 14A.** Outside revenue $293 million, mostly Altera; no external 14A customer is named [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Claim: "increasing momentum on customer engagements for Intel 14A", risk production (the first small runs before full volume) in late 2027, "high volume ramps in 2028", and the 0.9 PDK (the design rulebook customers need) "on track for October" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Tell: a named external customer; the October PDK.

**Advanced packaging (EMIB-T).** Size not disclosed; "a growing EMIB-T backlog, yield and reliability are hitting targets" (backlog: orders not yet delivered), with "customer ramps in 2027" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Tell: packaging revenue appearing in Foundry outside revenue in 2027.

**Purpose-built silicon (custom chips for one customer) and Design Services.** Dollar size not disclosed; revenue "up roughly 20 percent sequentially and nearly tripling year-over year" (sequentially: versus the prior quarter) inside DCAI, with Fortinet the one named customer [Q2 2026 call, p.4; p.3]. Tell: "other DCAI revenue" in the 10-Q, $951 million in Q2 2026 [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2].

## 4. Guidance

**Q3 2026 (release outlook table).** "Revenue | $15.8-16.8 billion"; "Gross margin | 41.0% | 42.0%" (GAAP | non-GAAP); "Tax Rate | 1% | 11%"; "Earnings (Loss) Per Share Attributable to Intel—Diluted | $0.31 | $0.38", all "based on the midpoint of the revenue range" [Q2 2026 release]. In the remarks: "we are guiding Q3 revenue to a range of $15.8 to $16.8 billion. At the midpoint of $16.3 billion, we forecast gross margin of 42 percent, a tax rate of 11 percent, and EPS of $0.38 cents, all on a non-GAAP basis" [Q2 2026 call, p.5].

**Full year 2026.** "We continue to tightly manage non-gaap operating expenses to roughly $16.5 billion for the year" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. "now expect our cap ex to be more than $20 billion, which is up significantly versus our expectations entering the year" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. "we expect non-controlling interest, or NCI, to net to approximately $250 million in each of Q3 and Q4 of this year and be approximately $1.1 billion for 2027 and 2028, on a GAAP basis" [Q2 2026 call, p.5].

**2027 and beyond.** "we are forecasting 2027 capital expenditures to be significantly above the 2026 levels with the vast majority spent across our US network" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. "we remain on track for 14A risk production for our internal products in the second half of 2027 and we made the decision in Q2 to fully commit to high volume ramps in 2028" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]. Intel 18A-P entered risk production in June 2026 [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2].

**Not guided.** 2026 adjusted free cash flow (April's "positive ... for the full year" was not repeated); segments; whether the "$20 billion" is gross or net of partner and government money; 2027 operating expense; 2027 debt retirement [Q2 2026 call; Q1 2026 call, p.6].

## 5. Claims to verify next quarter

1. **Q3 2026 revenue lands between $15.8 billion and $16.8 billion.** "Revenue | $15.8-16.8 billion" [Q2 2026 release]
2. **Q3 2026 non-GAAP gross margin is 42.0% or higher.** "Gross margin | 41.0% | 42.0%" (non-GAAP column) [Q2 2026 release]
3. **Q3 2026 net income attributable to non-controlling interests is between $200 million and $300 million (our sharpening of "approximately $250 million").** "NCI, to net to approximately $250 million in each of Q3 and Q4 of this year" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
4. **On the Q3 2026 call, management keeps or raises the 2026 capex outlook of "more than $20 billion".** "now expect our cap ex to be more than $20 billion" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
5. **Q3 2026 gross capital expenditures in the release reconciliation exceed Q2's $2,652 million (our sharpening from first-half gross capex of $7.6 billion against "more than $20 billion").** "we are meaningfully increasing our investments in equipment, clean room space, and substrates" [Q2 2026 release]
6. **The Q3 2026 release keeps the full-year 2026 non-GAAP operating expense figure at roughly $16.5 billion, not raised.** "We continue to tightly manage non-gaap operating expenses to roughly $16.5 billion for the year" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]
7. **Intel Foundry's Q3 2026 operating loss is smaller than Q2's $2,089 million (our sharpening of the April statement, which July did not repeat).** "we expect Intel Foundry's operating loss to improve through the year" [Q1 2026 call, p.4]
8. **On the Q3 2026 call, management reaffirms 14A risk production for internal products in the second half of 2027 and high-volume ramps in 2028, with neither date pushed out.** "we remain on track for 14A risk production for our internal products in the second half of 2027 and we made the decision in Q2 to fully commit to high volume ramps in 2028" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
9. **By the Q3 2026 call or 10-Q, management reports that the Intel 14A 0.9 PDK was delivered in October 2026.** "PDK 0.9 is on track for October" [Q2 2026 call, p.2]
10. **By the Q4 2026 call, management reports that the primary Panther Lake chip's cost fell a further roughly 20% in the second half of 2026.** "on track for an additional 20 percent this year" [Q2 2026 call, p.4–5]
11. **The Q4 2026 revenue guidance given on the Q3 call has a midpoint above Q3 2026 actual revenue (our sharpening of the supply-timing statement).** "the near-term linearity of our supply growth is more skewed towards the end of Q3 and into Q4, especially for servers" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]

**Not sharpenable without a Q&A:** whether April's window for 14A "early design commitments" (late 2026 into early 2027) still holds; the Q2 CCPG inventory charge; the size of Design Services and the EMIB-T backlog; the margin path beyond Q3 [Q2 2026 call; Q1 2026 call, p.2].
