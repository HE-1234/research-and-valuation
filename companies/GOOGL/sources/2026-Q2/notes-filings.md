## 10. Risk factors: items the 10-Q flags as "material changes ... since our Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025"

The 10-Q lists the following under Part II, Item 1A. It does not label each individually as new vs. modified; the writer should compare against the FY2025 10-K text. [10-Q Q2 2026, Part II Item 1A]

- "Our increasing investment in new businesses, products, services, and technologies is inherently risky..." Includes TPU-supply and backstop language: "To meet the AI compute capacity demands of our customers, we are engaging in the supply of TPU systems which may increase our costs and operational complexity.
