## 1. Business description and segments (company's own words)

- "Alphabet is a collection of businesses — the largest of which is Google. We report Google in two segments, Google Services and Google Cloud, and all non-Google businesses collectively as Other Bets. Supporting these businesses, we have centralized certain AI-related research and development focused on advanced research in AI and developing the frontier models that serve our businesses, which is reported in Alphabet-level activities." [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Mission statement: "Our mission to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful is as relevant today as it was when we were founded in 1998." [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- "since 2016, be an AI-first company." Company "invested more than $200 billion in research and development in the last five years". [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- "Today, all 15 of our half-billion-user products — including seven with two billion users — use our Gemini models." [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Google Services core products: "ads, Android, Chrome, devices, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Gemini, Google Maps, Google Photos, Google Play, Search, and YouTube". [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- How Google Services makes money: "Google Services generates revenues primarily by delivering both performance and brand advertising that appears on Google Search & other properties, YouTube, and Google Network partners' properties". Non-ad revenue: consumer subscriptions (YouTube TV, YouTube Music and Premium, NFL Sunday Ticket, Google One), platforms (Google Play sales of apps and in-app purchases), devices (Pixel family). [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Google Cloud: "generates revenues primarily from consumption-based fees and subscriptions for infrastructure, platform, applications, and other cloud services" via Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace; offerings listed as AI-optimized Infrastructure (custom TPUs and GPUs), Developer Platform (Vertex AI, "more than 200 foundation models"), Cybersecurity, Data and Analytics, Agents (Gemini Enterprise; Gemini for Google Workspace). [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Other Bets: "Revenues from Other Bets are generated primarily from the sale of autonomous transportation and internet services." Waymo "is now providing fully autonomous, paid ride-hailing services to customers in multiple cities"; Isomorphic Labs applies AI to drug discovery; X is "our moonshot factory". [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Alphabet-level activities (not allocated to segments): "certain AI-focused shared research and development activities, including employee compensation expenses and technical infrastructure usage costs associated with the development of our general AI models"; corporate initiatives (philanthropy); corporate shared costs "including certain fines and settlements"; severance and office-space charges; revenue hedging gains/losses. [10-K FY2025, Note 15]
- Segment history note: "beginning in the first quarter of 2023 DeepMind is reported as part of Alphabet-level activities instead of within Other Bets... Prior periods have been recast"; Google DeepMind (Brain team + DeepMind) "is reported within Alphabet-level activities prospectively beginning in the second quarter of 2023. Previously, the Brain team was included within Google Services." [10-K FY2023, Note 15]
- Chief Operating Decision Maker is CEO Sundar Pichai; segments evaluated on operating income (loss), not assets. [10-K FY2025, Note 15]
- Competition list includes "AI model developers and providers of AI products and services" and "social networks, which users may rely on for product or service referrals, rather than seeking information through traditional search engines". [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
- Custom silicon: "our own custom-built Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), such as Ironwood, our seventh-generation TPU." [10-K FY2025, Item 1]
