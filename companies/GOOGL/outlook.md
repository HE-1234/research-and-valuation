## 2. What management says about the core business

Same frame as Q1: AI is making Search bigger, and capacity is the limit. Pichai: "AI continues to drive an expansionary moment ... driving growth in queries", and "Search usage hit an all‑time high during the World Cup this year" [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. AI Mode has "surpassed one billion monthly active users", and the Gemini app has 950 million monthly users with daily users "tripling in the last year" [Q2 2026 call, p.3] [Q2 2026 call, p.4].

The CFO's caution: Search & other grew 17%, against 19% in Q1, and "in Q3, we will begin lapping an acceleration in Search performance that began in the third quarter last year" (lapping: the year-ago quarter was itself strong) [Q2 2026 call, p.12] [Q1 2026 call, p.7] [Q2 2026 call, p.13]. On capacity: "we continue to be supply constrained" [Q2 2026 call, p.2].

## 3. Growth engines

**Google Cloud.** Revenue $24.8 billion, up 82%; operating income $8.8 billion; margin 35.6% versus 32.9% in Q1 [Q2 2026 call, p.12] [Q1 2026 slides, p.8]. Backlog rose "by more than $50 billion sequentially" to $514 billion [Q2 2026 call, p.12]. New: renting other providers' computers ("third‑party capacity") in Q3 "as a bridging strategy", which "will create modest margin pressure in the near‑term" [Q2 2026 call, p.13]. Working: backlog rises again; the Q3 margin dips only modestly. Not working: a sharp margin fall, or the "exceeding their commitments" statistic (now "more than 50%") disappearing [Q2 2026 call, p.4].

**TPU system sales.** Google's own AI chips, sold as complete systems installed in customers' data centers and booked as Cloud "product revenues" [Q2 2026 release, p.8]. Deliveries began in Q2; revenue was "a small amount", with "the vast majority" due in 2027 [Q2 2026 call, p.19] [Q2 2026 call, p.13]. Building ahead took inventory from $2.4 billion at year end to $10.0 billion: "you see that impact on the cash from operations" [Q2 2026 release, p.5] [Q2 2026 call, p.19]. Working: revenue ramps "as we exit 2026" and inventory turns into sales [Q2 2026 call, p.13]. Not working: inventory climbs without matching revenue, or 2027 slips.

**Gemini and AI products.** The model APIs (the paid connections developers use to call the models) handle "approximately 22 billion tokens per minute" (tokens are the word-fragments a model reads and writes), up from 16 billion a quarter ago; "nearly 90% of the Fortune 100" use Gemini Enterprise [Q2 2026 call, p.2] [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. Working: tokens and Gemini app users rise again. Not working: either figure stops being reported.

**Subscriptions.** Revenue $12.9 billion, up 15% (19% in Q1), led by YouTube Music and Premium and Google One AI plans; the 350 million paid-subscription count from Q1 was not updated [Q2 2026 call, p.12] [Q1 2026 call, p.11] [Q1 2026 call, p.2]. Working: the count reappears, higher.

**Waymo.** Other Bets revenue $382 million, loss $1.8 billion; Q1's figure of over 500,000 weekly Waymo rides was not repeated [Q2 2026 call, p.12] [Q2 2026 call, p.6] [Q1 2026 call, p.6]. Working: rides reported again, rising.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim, CFO; no revenue, margin, or EPS (earnings per share) figure was given for Q3 or 2026 [Q2 2026 call, p.13–14].

- **Capex 2026:** "we are updating our full year 2026 CapEx guidance range to $195‑205 billion, up from our previous estimate of $180‑190 billion." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **Capex 2027:** "we continue to expect our CapEx to increase significantly in 2027, and we'll provide more details at a later date." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **Backlog:** "we expect to recognize just over 50% of the total backlog as revenue over the next 24 months." [Q2 2026 call, p.12]
- **TPU revenue timing:** "We continue to expect to recognize a relatively small portion of the revenues ... this year, ramping as we exit 2026. We anticipate the vast majority of the revenues from these agreements will be realized in 2027." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **Third-party capacity and Cloud margin:** "we plan to expand the use of third‑party capacity in Q3 as a bridging strategy while we build out more internal capacity. ... it will create modest margin pressure in the near‑term as we utilize this capacity." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **Search comparison:** "in Q3, we will begin lapping an acceleration in Search performance that began in the third quarter last year." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **FX (currency; the quoted "headwind" is a drag):** "we would expect a slight FX headwind to our consolidated revenue in Q3, compared to a one percentage point FX tailwind in Q2." [Q2 2026 call, p.13]
- **Free cash flow:** "we expect the free cash flow will remain under pressure driven by our investments in technical infrastructure" [Q2 2026 call, p.14]
- **Equity markets (Q&A; SBC is stock-based pay; an ATM program sells new shares gradually on the open market):** "At this point, we're not planning to go back to the equity markets, with the exception of ... the ATM, or at‑the‑market, offering that we will do to address ... the tax on SBC, which we'll do for some period of time." [Q2 2026 call, p.18]
- **Depreciation and hiring (P&L = income statement):** "will continue to put pressure on the P&L in the form of higher depreciation expense and related data center operations costs, such as energy. We also expect to continue hiring in key investment areas such as AI and cloud" [Q2 2026 call, p.13–14]

**Started saying.** Shareholders' equity is now a funding source: $49.6 billion raised in June and a $40.0 billion ATM program set up, buybacks still zero, while capex guidance rose again and quarterly free cash flow went negative [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 release, p.7] [Q2 2026 call, p.13] [Q2 2026 slides, p.11]. A $99.0 billion paper gain on stakes in SpaceX and a private company added $77.1 billion to net income and $6.26 to EPS [Q2 2026 release, p.9] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2].

**Said differently.** Confidence language is unchanged ("more bullish"), but "compute constrained" became "supply constrained ... just like the rest of the industry", and the Search record moved from a standing state to one event [Q2 2026 call, p.15] [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. Our inference: demand confidence is intact; the build-out is now paid for with shares as well as debt; and investors are being prepared for slower Search growth and a Cloud margin dip.
