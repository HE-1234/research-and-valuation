## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame of the call: AI is making Search bigger, not smaller. Pichai: "AI continues to drive Search usage, and queries are at an all time high" [Q1 2026 call, p.4]. Schindler called it "the expansionary moment we see here for Search", said Gemini has "significantly expanded our ability to deliver Ads on longer, more complex searches", and, asked about showing ads on more than an analyst's figure of about 20% of queries, said "there is upside in that coverage number" [Q1 2026 call, p.20] [Q1 2026 call, p.16].

On paying for Search through subscriptions rather than ads, Pichai said "there may be different ways to accomplish that", noting paid tiers already apply "across your Google user experience, including in Search" [Q1 2026 call, p.23]. On ads inside the Gemini app: "we're not rushing anything here" [Q1 2026 call, p.20].

Pichai said supply, not demand, is the limit: "we are compute constrained in the near term. And as an example, our Cloud revenue would have been higher if we were able to meet the demand" [Q1 2026 call, p.19].

## 3. Growth engines

**Google Cloud.** Revenue $20.0 billion, up 63%; operating margin 32.9%; backlog $462 billion, nearly doubled in a quarter [Q1 2026 call, p.11] [Q1 2026 call, p.12]. Revenue from products built on Google's AI models "grew nearly 800% year over year" [Q1 2026 call, p.4]. Working or not: backlog keeps rising, the two-year share holds, and Cloud margin holds up after the Wiz effect.

**TPU hardware sales.** Google will ship its own AI chips to "a select group of customers in their own data centers", naming Thinking Machines Lab, Hudson River Trading and Boston Dynamics; the agreements cover "multiple gigawatts", and Google is providing credit backstops (promises to cover payments if a project owner cannot) for some customers' data-center and power projects [Q1 2026 call, p.6] [10-Q Q1 2026, Item 2]. Working or not: management confirms the first hardware revenue and reports follow-on agreements [10-Q Q1 2026, Item 2].

**Consumer subscriptions.** About 350 million paid subscriptions; "this was our strongest quarter ever for our consumer AI plans, primarily driven by adoption of the Gemini App" [Q1 2026 call, p.2]. Working or not: the count keeps rising.

**Shopping agents and new ad formats.** A checkout standard for AI Mode and the Gemini app added Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce and Stripe, and "Direct Offers in AI Mode" ads are in pilot [Q1 2026 call, p.8]. Working or not: pilots becoming general releases credited for Search growth.

**Waymo.** Over 500,000 fully autonomous rides per week, doubling in under a year [Q1 2026 call, p.6]. Working or not: rides keep rising.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim, from the CFO's prepared remarks; no revenue, operating margin, or EPS (earnings per share) figure was given for Q2 or full-year 2026 [Q1 2026 call, p.12–13].

- **FX (currency swings), Q2; a "tailwind" here is a boost from exchange rates, and "spot rates" are today's exchange rates:** "At current spot rates, we would expect to see an FX tailwind of approximately one percentage point toward consolidated revenue in Q2, compared to three percentage points FX tailwinds in the first quarter." [Q1 2026 call, p.12]
- **Capex 2026:** "we are updating our full year 2026 CapEx guidance range to $180 to $190 billion, up from our previous estimate of $175 to $185 billion, to now include investment related to the acquisition of Intersect, which closed in March." [Q1 2026 call, p.13]
- **Capex 2027:** "we expect our 2027 CapEx to significantly increase compared to 2026." [Q1 2026 call, p.13]
- **Backlog (GCP = Google Cloud Platform; recognize = count as revenue):** "The majority of the backlog is related to typical GCP contracts, and we expect to recognize just over 50% of the backlog as revenue over the next 24 months." [Q1 2026 call, p.12]
- **TPU hardware revenue:** "We expect to begin recognizing a small percent of the revenues from these agreements later this year, with the vast majority of revenues to be realized in 2027." [Q1 2026 call, p.12]
- **Wiz (a "headwind" is a drag on the margin):** "First, Wiz will be reporting in the Google Cloud segment. And second, we expect a low single digit percentage point headwind to Cloud's operating margin for the remainder of 2026 related to the acquisition." [Q1 2026 call, p.12–13]
- **Expenses (P&L = income statement, the profit-and-loss report):** "the significant increase in our investments in technical infrastructure will continue to put pressure on the P&L in the form of higher depreciation expense and related data center operations costs, such as energy. We also expect to continue hiring in key investment areas such as AI and Cloud, and are investing in marketing to support our AI products." [Q1 2026 call, p.13]
