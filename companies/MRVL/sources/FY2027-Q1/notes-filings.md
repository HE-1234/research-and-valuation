## 1. Business description (company's own words)

- "Marvell Technology, Inc. … is a leading supplier of data infrastructure semiconductor solutions, spanning the data center core to network edge. We are a fabless supplier of high-performance semiconductor products with core strengths in developing and scaling complex System-on-a-Chip architectures, integrating analog, mixed-signal and digital signal processing functionality." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- "Our product solutions serve two end markets: (i) data center and (ii) communications and other." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- "Our current product offerings include custom Application Specific Integrated Circuits ("ASICs"), interconnects, ethernet solutions, fibre channel adapters, processors and storage controllers. In addition, we are developing Ultra Accelerator Link ("UALink") switches and Ethernet for Scale-Up Networking ("ESUN") switches for the emerging scale-out AI market." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Custom ASICs: "We develop custom semiconductor solutions tailored to individual customer specifications … built on our proven ASIC platform which leverages … ultra-high-speed SerDes, ARM compute, security, storage, silicon photonics and advanced packaging, including die to die interconnects, chiplets, co-packaged optics ("CPO") and custom high-bandwidth memory ("HBM"). We have successfully executed multiple 5 nanometer ("nm") designs in the last few years, and are progressing through 3nm designs now and developing our advanced 2nm generation platform." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Interconnects: "PAM (pulse amplitude modulation), coherent and coherent-lite DSPs (digital signal processors), laser drivers, TIAs (trans-impedance amplifiers), silicon photonics, CPO (co-packaged optics), LPO (linear pluggable optics) chipsets, DCI (data center interconnect), AEC (active electrical cable) DSPs and PCIe retimer solutions." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- "In addition to selling standard product solutions, where the exact same product is sold to multiple customers, we also offer optimized solutions which are customized to a specific customer's requirements. The demand for optimized solutions has been increasing as our customers seek greater customization and differentiation for their products and services." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Customers: "Our target customers are original equipment manufacturers ("OEMs") and original design manufacturers, both of which design and manufacture end market devices, and distributors for these products." "We expect that a significant percentage of our sales will continue to come from direct sales to key customers." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Manufacturing (fabless): "By outsourcing manufacturing, we are able to avoid the cost associated with owning and operating our own manufacturing facilities." "We typically outsource all product packaging and testing requirements for our products in production to several assembly and test subcontractors primarily located in Taiwan, Canada, Korea, Singapore and China." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Foundry location: "Most of our products are manufactured by third-party foundries located in Taiwan, and other sources are located in China, Germany, South Korea, Singapore and the United States." The 10-K does not name TSMC or any foundry by name. [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- Process nodes: "Our portfolio of products is based on foundational intellectual property on leading edge Advanced CMOS processes in 5nm, 3nm, 2nm and 1.4nm (A14 node)." Advanced packaging cited: CoWoS, InFO, EMIB, "advanced substrates". [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Single reportable segment: "the design, development and sale of integrated circuits." CEO is the chief operating decision maker. [10-K FY2026, Note 14]
- Patents: "over 10,000 issued patents and pending patent applications" as of January 31, 2026. [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- History markers in filings: prior parent Marvell Technology Group Ltd. (Bermuda) IPO June 27, 2000; redomiciled to Delaware, trading as Marvell Technology, Inc. from April 20, 2021. Inphi acquisition financed with $1.8B term loans entered December 7, 2020; Avera acquisition in fiscal 2021 (brought US government contracts / facility clearances). [10-K FY2026, Item 5; Note 7; Item 1]
- Facilities: US owned 983,000 sq ft and leased 482,000 sq ft; India 418,000 leased; Israel 171,000; Singapore 142,000; Vietnam 99,000; Canada 90,000; Taiwan 63,000; Argentina 50,000 (all leased). [10-K FY2026, Item 2]

## 10. Marvell-specific risk factors (key sentences, verbatim)

- Customer concentration and in-house silicon: "Given their dependence on semiconductor products to operate their data centers and to ensure continuity of supply and reduce direct costs, some large customers may begin developing and making their own semiconductor solutions which could result in a loss of business for Marvell." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- Data center concentration: "our sales have recently been, and in the future may continue to be, concentrated in our data center end market. Sales into this end market have fluctuated significantly from period to period and year to year and will likely continue to fluctuate in the future." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- Custom-program dependence: "We have in the past, and may continue to, make custom or semi-custom products on an exclusive basis for some of our customers for a negotiated period of time. The percentage of our sales related to custom or semi-custom products has been increasing over the last few years. Any revenue from sales of our custom or semi-custom products is directly related to sales of the third-party customer's products and reflective of their success in the market … if these customers are bought by our competitors or other third parties, they may terminate agreements related to these custom or semi-custom products … there may be no other customers for these products due to their custom or semi-custom nature." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- Design wins and program timing: "our failure to win a competitive bid can result in our foregoing revenues from a given customer's product line for the life of that product. In addition, design opportunities may be infrequent or delayed." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- Innovation pace: "some of our large customers depend on rapid and continuous innovation and will select partners who can help them deliver innovation at their pace and if we are unable to deliver on these timelines we may miss significant business opportunities." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- NRE payments (new in forward-looking factors): "risks related to failures of our customers to agree to pay for NRE (non-recurring engineering) costs, failure to pay enough to cover the costs we incur in connection with NREs or non-payment of previously agreed NRE costs due to us." [10-Q Q1 FY2027, Item 2]
- China / export controls (heading): "Adverse changes in the political, regulatory and economic policies of governments in connection with trade with China and Chinese customers have reduced the demand for our products and damaged our business." Body: "While most of our products that are shipped to China are processed and placed into larger systems, after which they are distributed to customers in global markets outside of China, a small portion of our products are shipped into China and remain there … the addition of certain companies to the Entity List … has dampened demand for our products … in the past some of these licenses have been delayed or denied." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]

In January 2026, BIS issued a new licensing policy … including a twenty-five percent (25%) tariff and other requirements … While these U.S. government actions did not impact Marvell, if such revenue sharing were to be imposed on our China-derived revenue, it could erode our gross margins." [10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
- China customer behavior: "concerns that U.S. companies may not be reliable suppliers … has caused, and may in the future cause, some of our customers in China to amass large inventories of our products well in advance of need or cause some of our customers to replace our products in favor of products from other suppliers. Customers in China may also choose to develop indigenous solutions." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]

[10-K FY2026, Item 1A]
