## 2. What management says about the core business

Management's frame is that Marvell is "a key enabler of next generation AI infrastructure", built as "a well diversified company anchored by multiple large existing franchises and complemented by several emerging growth engines" [Q1 FY2027 call]. The direction is acceleration, not maturity: "we were 46% data center growth last year. This year is 50, and next year is looking like 55" [Q1 FY2027 call]. The long-term thesis is optical, "optics is the future of data center connectivity", and management argues that as AI models grow more complex "the role of networking has become significantly more important" [Q1 FY2027 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**Interconnect (electro-optics).** The largest data center business and "the star of the show" [Q1 FY2027 call]. The FY2027 growth expectation has moved from 50% to "more than 70%" [Q1 FY2027 call]. Tells: TIAs and drivers (the receiver-side and laser-side chips in an optical module) to "exceed a $1 billion annualized run rate in the next few quarters" (run rate: one quarter's revenue multiplied by four), and modules linking data centers ("roughly $500 million" in FY2026) to reach "$1 billion annualized revenue during fiscal 28" [Q1 FY2027 call].

**Custom silicon (XPU and companion chips).** More than 20% growth in FY2027 and "more than double" in FY2028, in roughly equal thirds from existing programs, "over 10 XPU-attached programs" and "the ramp of our new tier 1 XPU program into volume production" (tier 1: a top-rank customer); long-range, "over $10 billion in revenue in fiscal 29" [Q1 FY2027 call]. Dollars are not disclosed; tells are the FY2028 target being reiterated and Customer A's share of revenue in the 10-Q (our inference that Customer A is the custom buyer; the filings do not say) [10-Q Q1 FY2027, Item 2].

**Switching.** Scale-out Ethernet switches (the switches that connect servers and racks across a data center) should "exceed $600 million" in FY2027 and track "to more than $1 billion in annualized revenue in fiscal 2028"; scale-up switching, which links AI processors inside a rack, is "not really in any numbers I am talking about right now", so it is pure upside [Q1 FY2027 call]. Tell: the $600 million.

**Scale-up optics (Celestial AI).** FY2028 revenue is now "about $300 million", double the prior "$150 million", after a "tier 1 hyperscaler" chose the Celestial design [Q1 FY2027 call]. Tell: the earn-out liability in indicator 8.
