## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is now growth that is "broad-based" and speeding up on a bigger base: "even as our revenue base becomes significantly larger, our growth rate is accelerating" [Q2 FY2027 call]. The long-range thesis is still connectivity at every distance, with scale-up next: "Scale-up opportunity remains massive and is still largely ahead of us" [Q2 FY2027 call].

Daniel Durn, CFO since June 15, 2026, set his terms on his first call: "I plan to remain intensely focused on expanding operating margins, growing cash flow and driving attractive stockholder returns" [Q2 FY2027 call; 8-K 2026-06-11]. Long-range detail was deferred to the October 6, 2026 Investor Day: revenue "really out until the end of the decade" and a reset of the margin model [Q2 FY2027 call; Q2 FY2027 slides, p.4].

## 3. Growth engines

**Custom silicon (XPU and XPU-attach).** Dollars not disclosed [Q2 FY2027 release]. Management expects "a significant acceleration in custom demand in the second half of this year" and that the business "will more than double year-over-year in fiscal 2028 and accelerate significantly in fiscal 2029"; last quarter's FY2027 ">20%" figure was not repeated [Q2 FY2027 call; Q2 FY2027 slides, p.18]. Management never said "Google" on the call, only "a key hyperscaler"; the 8-K is the document that names the counterparty: an agreement dated July 29, 2026 with Google LLC for chips "that attach to the TPU ecosystem" (TPU: Google's own AI processor) [Q2 FY2027 call; 8-K 2026-08-19]. A warrant is a contract giving its holder the right to buy shares at a fixed price for a set period (terms in the table). Revenue covered through FY2028 "is already reflected in the overall custom revenue target"; "the big impact would be in '29 and beyond" [Q2 FY2027 call]. Tells: warrant slices vested in each 10-Q (each is $500 million of revenue from this customer); the FY2028 "more than double" being restated; Q3 gross margin [Q2 FY2027 call].

**Interconnect and electro-optics.** Dollars not disclosed [Q2 FY2027 release]. "800 gig demand remains strong, while our 1.6T business is ramping rapidly" [Q2 FY2027 call]. TIAs and drivers, DCI modules (modules linking one data center to another) and scale-out switching are each "on or ahead of the trajectory towards the $1 billion annualized revenue run rate" (a quarter's revenue times four) [Q2 FY2027 call]. The raise concentrated in scale-up optics (light-emitting parts placed next to or inside the processor package, called NPO and CPO, rather than in a pluggable module): last quarter's "about $300 million" FY2028 category is now "much larger than we thought just a quarter ago" [Q2 FY2027 call]. The FY2027 interconnect growth rate (">70%" last quarter) was not restated; asked, the CEO said only that optical DSPs are "upsized versus the prior growth rates we talked about" [Q2 FY2027 call; Q2 FY2027 slides, p.8]. Tells: numbers returning on the Q3 call; indicator 8.

**Communications and other.** $567.8M, 21% of revenue, down 3% from Q1 and up 10% from a year earlier [Q2 FY2027 release]. Guided down: Q3 "to decline in the low to mid-teens percentage range", then "a solid sequential recovery in the fourth quarter"; FY2027 growth "to approach our 10% target", softer than Q1's "approximately 10%" [Q2 FY2027 call; Q1 FY2027 call].

**Started saying.** The warrant, and letting analysts' arithmetic stand: when Ben Reitzes of Melius Research put full vesting at "$120 billion divided by 6.5", the CEO answered "your math is not wrong", adding that custom revenue for FY2029 and beyond "definitely goes higher" than prior models, unsized until the Investor Day [Q2 FY2027 call]. Gross margin now has a stated cause: the Q3 range (57.5%–58.5%) sits below Q2's (58.25%–59.25%) and the CFO named custom mix (table) [Q1 FY2027 release; Q2 FY2027 call]. Our reading: this confirms business.md §3's inference that custom chips carry lower gross margins.

**Stopped saying.** Cloud capital-spending growth set to "moderate into the 30%-plus range" as a planning assumption, and the word "CapEx" at all [Q1 FY2027 call; Q2 FY2027 call]. Never said on either call: China, which took 42% of Q2 shipments against 29% a year earlier, mostly for non-China customers' factories [Q1 FY2027 call; Q2 FY2027 call; 10-Q Q2 FY2027, Note 3].

**Changed cash.** Stock-based pay more than doubled year over year (computed; table); unexplained on the call; the 10-Q's $190.1M of Celestial employee awards still to be expensed may be part of it (our inference) [Q2 FY2027 supplemental, p.5; 10-Q Q2 FY2027, Note 10].
