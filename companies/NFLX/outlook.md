## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is three "areas of focus: delivering more entertainment value, leveraging technology to improve every aspect of our service, and improving monetization", measured by two numbers: "Our primary financial metrics are revenue for growth and operating margin for profitability. Our goal is to sustain healthy revenue growth, expand operating profit and margin, and deliver growing free cash flow" [Q2 2026 letter, p.1] [Q2 2026 letter, p.2]. The cost rule is Sarandos's: "We grow the content spend slower than revenue while we're continuing to invest in a huge addressable market" [Q2 2026 interview, p.6]. The revenue lever Peters points to is the ad tier: the gap between ad-tier and no-ads pricing "is essentially near-term underrealized revenue growth" [Q2 2026 interview, p.10]. On runway, the CFO: "under 45% penetrated" of "roughly 800 million addressable households" and "about 5% of TV view share globally" [Q2 2026 interview, p.5]. On capital after Warner Bros.: "there is no change to our capital allocation philosophy ... we are primarily builders, not buyers" [Q2 2026 interview, p.12] [Q2 2026 interview, p.13].

## 3. Growth engines

**Advertising.** Size: "over $1.5B" in 2025, targeted at "approximately $3 billion" in 2026; no quarterly figure [Q4 2025 letter, p.1] [Q2 2026 letter, p.5]. Claims: AI tools "across the full advertising lifecycle", programmatic (automated) buying extended "to Pause Ads and live inventory this summer", the US upfront (the annual advance sale of ad slots) "in advanced stages" [Q2 2026 letter, p.5]. Working if the $3 billion is reiterated in the Q3 letter and the upfront is reported closed.

**Pricing and plan mix.** First-half price changes in "the US, Mexico and Spain, have gone well"; UCAN's 10% growth "reflects only a partial quarter impact" [Q2 2026 letter, p.5] [Q2 2026 letter, p.2]. Working if UCAN growth rises in Q3 as the full quarter of pricing lands.

**Live events and sports.** "just over 5% of our content spend but only ~1% of view hours", yet "six of the top 10 new member sign-up days over the last five years"; an "expanded agreement with the NFL" is ahead [Q2 2026 letter, p.3] [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. Working if live keeps being credited with sign-up records and ad demand.

**Games, video podcasts, creators.** Not quantified in dollars. Netflix Playground "3x growth in daily players since its launch in April"; podcasts "over-index on viewing during the day and on mobile" (take a bigger share of viewing than average), so "incremental" [Q2 2026 letter, p.4] [Q2 2026 letter, p.3]. Investment is "still very small relative to our overall content spend" [Q2 2026 interview, p.11]. Working if any absolute usage figure appears.

**International and local content.** LATAM grew 21% (16% FX-neutral) and APAC 16% (18%) in Q2; Japan "was the largest contributor to member growth in Q1" after the World Baseball Classic; TF1 programming is now inside the French subscription [Q2 2026 letter, p.7] [Q1 2026 letter, p.3] [Q2 2026 letter, p.4]. Working if LATAM and APAC FX-neutral growth stays in the mid-to-high teens.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim from the Q2 2026 letter.

- **Q3 2026:** "For Q3, we expect revenue growth of 12% (or 11% F/X neutral) driven by growth in memberships, pricing, and ad revenue. We project an operating margin of 33.2% compared with 28.2% in the year ago quarter." [Q2 2026 letter, p.2] Table: revenue $12,860M, operating income $4,268M, net income $3,452M, diluted EPS $0.82 [Q2 2026 letter, p.1].
- **FY2026 revenue and margin:** "we are narrowing our revenue forecast to $51.0-$51.4B, which represents 13%-14% growth (~12% F/X neutral), driven by growth in memberships and pricing, and a projected rough doubling of our ads revenue to approximately $3 billion. We continue to anticipate an operating margin of 31.5% for 2026 both on a reported basis and based on F/X rates as of January 1, 2026 vs. 29.5% in 2025. Our forecast implies annual operating income growth of 20%+ for 2026." [Q2 2026 letter, p.2]
- **Content amortization:** "we continue to expect content amortization to grow slower in the second half of the year and to increase ~10% for 2026." [Q2 2026 letter, p.2]
- **Free cash flow and cash content spend:** "For the full year, we continue to expect FCF of approximately $12.5B, and an annual cash content spend to amortization ratio of ~1.1x." [Q2 2026 letter, p.6]
- **Advertising:** "we remain on track to deliver approximately $3 billion in ads revenue in 2026." [Q2 2026 letter, p.5]
- **Debt:** "We have $1B of debt maturing later this year, which we plan to refinance." [Q2 2026 letter, p.6]
- Not guided: tax rate, capex, interest, memberships, ARM, Q3 cash flow [Q2 2026 letter, p.1] [Q2 2026 letter, p.2].
