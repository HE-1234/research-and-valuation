## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame: "The memory industry has been structurally transformed by the proliferation of AI", and memory is now "a strategic asset" because "AI system performance is architecturally dependent on memory subsystem performance and capacity" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.2]. On supply: "We expect tight conditions to persist beyond calendar 2027", and "Even as we expect industry supply to improve gradually in 2028, we currently do not have line of sight as to when memory supply will be able to catch up with increasing demand" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.1; p.2].

Two admissions temper this. Cost per bit is expected to rise: new products "all come with rising bit costs", and with new fabs this "is projected to cause the blended DRAM cost per bit to rise from current levels" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.4]. And Q4 guidance "reflects a meaningful moderation in the rate of price increases" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10].

## 3. Growth engines

**Data-center memory (HBM, high-capacity server modules, low-power server DRAM).** Data-center revenue "exceeded $25 billion in fiscal Q3, or an annualized run rate of over $100 billion" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.1]. "HBM4 12-high volume ramp is tracking twice as fast as HBM3E 12-high, and we have already shipped over $1 billion in HBM4 revenue" (period not stated; "12-high" means twelve DRAM chips stacked in one HBM package) [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.4]. Total HBM revenue is not disclosed [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.4; Q3 FY2026 release]. Tell: Cloud Memory revenue and margin; HBM4 reaching customers beyond the "lead customer" [Q3 FY2026 release].

**Data-center storage.** "Our data center SSD revenue exceeded $5 billion, more than doubling sequentially" (that is, versus the prior quarter), on "AI context memory storage and HDD displacement" (HDD: spinning hard disk drives) [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.1; p.5]. Tell: Core Data Center revenue and the NAND price direction.

**The SCA program.** Sixteen agreements so far; $22 billion of deposits and commitments are expected, about $18 billion in cash, which "will show up on our balance sheet more in FQ4" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.3; p.7]. Management says the deposits "appear in financing-related cash flows and will not affect our free cash flow"; the Q3 10-Q cash flow statement has no such line yet, and only $422 million of contract liabilities is on the balance sheet [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.7; 10-Q Q3 FY2026, Item 1; Note 14]. Tell: RPO, contract liabilities, and the count of signed agreements.

**Edge devices and cars.** For the industry as a whole, "PC and smartphone industry revenue is expected to grow despite unit volume declines" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.5]. Tell: Mobile and Client and Automotive and Embedded bit shipments.

**Capacity.** Idaho ID1 (the first Idaho fab) first wafers mid-calendar 2027, ID2 (the second) late 2028; Tongluo shipments mid-2027, "about a quarter earlier than our prior expectations"; next-generation nodes in volume in the second half of 2027 [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.4; p.6]. Tell: these dates holding.

## 4. Guidance

**Q4 FY2026 (14 weeks, ending September 3, 2026).** Revenue "$50.0 billion ± $1.0 billion" and gross margin "Approximately 86%", both GAAP and non-GAAP; Operating expenses "Approximately $1.86 billion" GAAP and "Approximately $1.65 billion" non-GAAP; Diluted earnings per share "$30.73 ± $1.00" GAAP and "$31.00 ± $1.00" non-GAAP, "based on approximately 1.15 billion diluted shares" [Q3 FY2026 release]. "We expect a fiscal Q4 and fiscal year 2026 tax rate of around 15.0%." "In fiscal Q4, we project capex of around $10 billion, bringing full-year fiscal 2026 capital spending to approximately $27 billion." "We forecast free cash flow to increase substantially again in fiscal Q4." "Any impacts that may occur due to trade or geopolitical developments are not included in our guidance." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]

**Fiscal 2027.** "We project operating expenses to increase by approximately $1 billion in fiscal 2027" and "We expect quarterly capex in fiscal 2027 to be above fiscal Q4 levels, with more than half the increase year over year in fiscal 2027 from construction capex" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]. No FY2027 revenue, margin or capex dollar figure was given [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10].

**Capital return.** "From Dec. 9, 2026, the second anniversary of the signature of our definitive CHIPS agreements, we intend to increase our capital return. Over time, we expect to return 100% of our excess cash to shareholders." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]

1. **Q4 FY2026 revenue lands between $49.0 billion and $51.0 billion.** "We expect FQ4 revenue to be a record $50.0 billion, plus or minus $1.0 billion;" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]
2. **Q4 FY2026 non-GAAP gross margin is at least 85.0% (our sharpening of "approximately 86.0%", not management's number).** "gross margin to be approximately 86.0%;" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]
3. **The Q4 FY2026 slides or remarks state quarter-over-quarter DRAM and NAND price increases both smaller than Q3's "low-60s" and "mid-80s" percentage ranges.** "Our fiscal Q4 gross margin outlook reflects a meaningful moderation in the rate of price increases." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]
4. **Q4 FY2026 "investments in capital expenditures, net" (release definition) lands between $9.0 billion and $11.0 billion (our sharpening of "around $10 billion", not management's number).** "In fiscal Q4, we project capex of around $10 billion" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]
5. **Q4 FY2026 adjusted free cash flow exceeds Q3's $18.3 billion (our sharpening of "increase substantially", not management's number).** "We forecast free cash flow to increase substantially again in fiscal Q4." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.10]

7. **The FY2026 10-K discloses RPO of at least $90 billion at year-end (our sharpening of "approximately $100 billion", not management's number).** "For the SCAs that we have entered into so far, including ones executed after the end of FQ3, RPO is approximately $100 billion." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.7]
8. **The number of signed SCAs reported at Q4 exceeds 16.** "we have now signed 16 strategic customer agreements" [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.1]
9. **Management restates ID1's first wafer output as mid-calendar 2027, with no slip.** "ID1 is on track for first wafer output in mid-calendar 2027 and ID2 in late calendar 2028." [Q3 FY2026 remarks, p.6]
