## 2. What management says about the core business

Management describes a chip company, not a rack or system seller: "we provide chips, technology in the form of chips, whether they be AI compute accelerators we call XPUs or networking chips that cluster them together" [Q2 FY2026 call]. The plan rests on "our six core customers": Google, Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta by name, plus "our other two customers" [Q2 FY2026 call]. Demand is "simply insatiable": "bookings for AI semiconductors were over $30 billion against the $10.8 billion we shipped", and "Our visibility runs all the way to 2028 right now" [Q2 FY2026 call].

New this quarter is a financing role: "Our strategic vision is to bring together Broadcom's leading technology and investor partners with the strongest balance sheets to deliver at scale sufficient compute capacity at the lowest cost and power for the leading AI frontier labs, including Anthropic and OpenAI", through "the AI XPU platform with Apollo and Blackstone and other leading investors" [Q2 FY2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**Custom AI accelerators (XPUs).** Chips designed for one customer; roughly 60% of the $10.8 billion of Q2 AI revenue, since networking was "almost 40%" (computed) [Q2 FY2026 release; Q2 FY2026 call]. Claims by customer: Anthropic, "another 5 gigawatts of next-generation TPU-based compute beginning in 2027"; OpenAI, "on track for production late 26" and "1.3 gigawatts in 2027"; Meta, "3 gigawatts through the end of 2028"; two unnamed customers with "purchase orders totaling $6 billion" [Q2 FY2026 call]. Tell: the quarterly AI figure against $16 billion for Q3 and $56 billion for FY2026.

**AI networking.** Switches, network cards and optics that link accelerators. Claims: "the industry's only 100 terabit Ethernet switch, the Tomahawk 6, for over one year", and a "next-generation 200-terabit switch" taping out (design finished and sent to the foundry) this quarter [Q2 FY2026 call]. Networking's share of AI revenue should drift from about 40% toward "closer to around 30%" as XPUs grow [Q2 FY2026 call]. Tell: the share stated each quarter; the tape-out confirmed.

**VMware / VCF.** Software revenue $7,178 million, up 9%, with "ARR growth of 17% year-over-year" (ARR: the yearly value of subscriptions in force) [Q2 FY2026 release; Q2 FY2026 call]. Tell: segment revenue against the $8.9 billion Q3 forecast; software operating margin near 79%.

**Non-AI semiconductors.** "Q2 revenue of $4.2 billion was up 6% year-on-year. Bookings during the same period exceeded $6 billion, which is a clear indication we are on the path towards a full cyclical recovery" [Q2 FY2026 call]. Tell: semiconductor segment revenue minus stated AI revenue (computed) against $4.5 billion.

## 4. Guidance

**Q3 FY2026 (quarter ending August 2, 2026).** Release: "Third quarter revenue guidance of approximately $29.4 billion;" "Third quarter non-GAAP operating income guidance of approximately 67 percent of projected revenue;" "Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA guidance of approximately 68 percent of projected revenue." "in Q3 we expect semiconductor revenue from AI to grow over 200 percent year-over-year to $16.0 billion." [Q2 FY2026 release] Call: "We forecast semiconductor revenue of approximately $20.5 billion, up 124% year-on-year." "We expect Q3 infrastructure software revenue of approximately $8.9 billion, up 31% year-on-year." "in Q3 we forecast non-AI semiconductor revenue to be approximately $4.5 billion, up 12% year-on-year." "we expect Q3 consolidated gross margin to be down to approximately 74%." "We expect the non-GAAP tax rate for Q3 and fiscal year 2026 to be approximately 16% ... We expect the non-GAAP diluted share count in Q3 to be approximately 4.94 billion shares, excluding the impact of potential share repurchases." [Q2 FY2026 call]

**FY2026.** "For the full year 2026, we expect to achieve AI semiconductor revenue of $56 billion, up approximately 180% from fiscal 2025." [Q2 FY2026 call]

**FY2027.** "we reiterate our AI semiconductor revenue guidance to be in excess of $100 billion." "We are planning to ship 10 gigawatts in 2027, and nothing has changed. Back-half loaded" [Q2 FY2026 call]

**FY2028.** "We expect, in fact, 2028 to be a substantial growth from what we are forecasting in 2027." [Q2 FY2026 call]

Not guided: FY2026 total or software revenue, capex, operating expenses, free cash flow, EPS (earnings per share), buybacks [Q2 FY2026 release; Q2 FY2026 call].
