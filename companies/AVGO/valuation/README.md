# AVGO valuation draft — review guide

**Status: BLOCKED.** The draft uses Q2 FY2026 operating evidence through June 9, 2026, with separately dated September market inputs. Both permitted independent review passes are complete; the verdict remains BLOCKED. No per-share valuation or price target has been produced. [Draft inputs; Validation]

## Four proposed business outcomes

The first forward year runs approximately May 2026–May 2027, not fiscal FY2026. These are analyst scenarios, including the clearly identified analyst completion of management's limited guidance. [Draft inputs]

| Case | Business story | First-year total revenue growth |
|---|---|---:|
| Bear | Existing AI commitments lift sales, then delays, customer self-design and software pricing pressure weaken the business. | 43.1% |
| Base | Several generations of AI chips ship and software customers renew, while stronger buyers gradually slow growth. | 62.7% |
| Bull | Broadcom retains more leading AI programs, expands networking content and keeps stronger software economics. | 84.2% |
| Management | The disclosed AI revenue floor is translated into model periods; unprovided years, margins and capital needs remain analyst assumptions. | 71.1% |

All figures and stories are proposed inputs, not company guidance except where explicitly identified in the full draft. Management's FY2027 AI wording is an open-ended floor; the translation is not a midpoint forecast. [Draft inputs, scenario revenue builds and management guidance]

## Five inputs worth attention

| Input | Central proposal | What must be decided |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 62.7% first-year growth | Whether the timing and scale of AI deliveries justify this increase from the trailing base. |
| GAAP operating margin | 52.5% in year 1; 54.0% in year 5 | Whether scale and expiring acquisition amortization offset mix and customer pricing pressure while research and stock compensation stay expensed. |
| Reinvestment | First five years itemized; years 6–10 unsupported | Establish a coherent funding path into maturity; the rejected path abruptly increased funding in the terminal year. |
| Cost of capital | About 11.0% initially; 9.44% mature | The industry-risk proxy, borrowing spread and separately dated market assumptions. |
| Mature economics | 3% growth and 18% return on new capital | These are hypotheses supported independently from the warning threshold; their connection to the earlier investment path remains unresolved. |

These inputs are documented in the draft, with calculations, ranges and revision triggers. The base/management 8.56-point and bull 12.56-point terminal premiums exceed the house warning thresholds and are explicitly flagged. [Draft inputs; Validation; Independent review]

## What prevents completion

1. **Customer-lease backstop:** the disclosed USD 29 billion maximum does not establish its present economic value. The eligible disclosures do not supply a supported default/recovery valuation. [10-Q Q2 FY2026, Note 11; Independent review]
2. **Corporate investments:** purchases and sales are disclosed, but the reviewed sources do not establish the current carrying balance outside cash equivalents. [10-Q Q2 FY2026, cash-flow statement; Independent review]
3. **Late funding:** the researched capital evidence does not yet reconcile the proposed high operating margins and early funding efficiency with the much lower mature return. Unsupported late inputs remain empty; no fictitious acquisition or financing budget fills them. [Independent review; Draft inputs]

Both validation and the unmodified restricted diagnostic run stop every case. The initial conditional diagnostics are preserved with the rejected first-pass inputs and are not forecasts for the revised draft. No completed-draft commit or passing forecast record has been created. [Validation; Diagnostic scope]

The next decision is whether to refresh the research to Q3 FY2026 and reassess these gaps using the newer disclosures, with a new valuation draft and review. A refresh may improve the financing evidence; it does not by itself resolve the long-term capital-model judgment. [Workflow recommendation; AGENTS.md §§13 and 18.7]
