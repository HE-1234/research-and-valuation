## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame: AI is forcing data centers to replace copper wiring with light, and Lumentum sits at the start of that shift. "As AI compute workloads increase in both speed and bandwidth, data center architects are turning to optical links as a primary means of connectivity" [Q4 FY2026 release]. The results "prove 2 things: Our differentiated technology commands premium value and our operating model delivers outsized leverage"; the company is "a premier laser chip manufacturer" [Q4 FY2026 call].

The financial frame is a target model set at OFC (the industry's annual optical trade show) that the company is beating early: the Q1 midpoint reaches "our $1.25 billion target more than 1 quarter ahead of schedule"; 50% gross margin, "originally targeted ... at a $2 billion quarterly run rate", arrived at $1.0 billion; the "38% to 42%" operating-margin model at $2 billion is "probably moving up 100 to 200 basis points", one to two percentage points (transcript figures except the $1.25 billion, which the release confirms) [Q4 FY2026 call; Q4 FY2026 release]. Supply, not demand, is the stated limit: demand "is outpacing our current supply" [10-K FY2026, Item 1].

## 3. Growth engines

**Laser chips for transceivers: EML and CW (Components).** Record EML shipments; 200G-per-lane EMLs are ">25% of total EML revenue" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.3]. Claims: "over 50% EML unit growth by December 2026 quarter compared to the year ago quarter", 200G at "50% or more of volume by midyear of 2027", still "shipping behind customer demand" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Japan output is "quite a bit better ... than we expected", so spare capacity goes to CW lasers sold at "a significant price premium" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Dollars not disclosed. Tell: the EML milestones restated; Components revenue and gross margin.

**Ultra-high-power lasers for CPO and NPO; ELS modules (Components).** Demand is "very much further behind relative to our ability to supply"; a first ELS module order is for delivery "by the second half of calendar 2027", with margins "not quite as good as the lasers" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Path: "the $50 million range by the end of the calendar year", then a triple-digit quarter in fiscal Q3 (transcript figures, not confirmed elsewhere) [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: those markers.

**Pump and narrow-linewidth lasers for links between data centers (Components).** Pumps grew "80% YoY" (year over year) with "multiple long-term supply agreements now in place"; narrow-linewidth lasers up ">130% YoY" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.3]. Claim: "a fourfold increase in our pump laser shipments over the next several quarters" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: that target restated.

**Cloud transceivers (Systems).** Record 800G shipments and "initial shipments of 1.6T transceivers, with a portion utilizing internal CW lasers" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.3]. Claim: "1.6T transceiver uptake to intensify starting in fiscal Q1 and sustain through calendar 2027" [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: Systems revenue and gross margin.

**Optical circuit switches (Systems).** "more than $90.0 million" of FY2026 revenue; shipments doubled Q3 to Q4, and the Q1 guide "includes our first triple-digit OCS revenue quarter" [10-K FY2026, Item 7; Q4 FY2026 call]. Backed by a "multi-year, multi-billion-dollar purchase agreement"; "tracking to the $400 million" for 2H CY2026 (analyst's framing; transcript figure) [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.3; Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: whether OCS dollars keep being stated.

**Capacity.** Two Japan fabs expanding; Greensboro converting to indium phosphide with "first revenue ... early 2028"; a deal with AXT for substrates, the base wafers the chips are grown on [Q4 FY2026 call]. Tell: capex, net PP&E, any Greensboro agreement.

## 4. Guidance

**Q1 FY2027 (quarter ending late September 2026).** "Net revenue in the range of $1.225 billion to $1.275 billion"; "Non-GAAP operating margin of 39.5% - 40.5%"; "Non-GAAP diluted net income per share of $4.05 to $4.35" [Q4 FY2026 release]. "Diluted Shares – M ... 102.0"; "Guidance assumes effective tax rate of 16.5%" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.7]. On the call: "approximately half of the sequential growth will stem for our components portfolio ... The other half will be powered by the ongoing ramp of our systems portfolio" (sequential means versus the prior quarter; transcription garble "for" = "from"), and "We expect gross margin expansion to continue driven by product mix and tight operational execution" [Q4 FY2026 call].

**Full year, gross margin, operating expenses, capex, GAAP:** none given; reconciliations to GAAP "cannot be provided without unreasonable effort" [Q4 FY2026 release]. Longer-dated: ultra-high-power lasers "on track to deliver meaningful revenue exiting CY 2026" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.3]; "we'll come out with some new financial targets, probably at the next OFC" [Q4 FY2026 call].
