## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is AI inference (running trained AI models to answer requests, which reads and stores large amounts of data): "Underlying our performance is the most important force in our market, the Era of Inference. AI is fundamentally a memory-centric storage-intensive problem. And it is reshaping the demand equation for NAND" [Q4 FY2026 call, CEO prepared remarks]. On Sandisk's edge: "we grow supply primarily through nodal transitions rather than wafer additions ... This is a structural advantage and what makes this franchise such a powerful cash generator" [Q4 FY2026 call, CEO prepared remarks].

The point of the NBMs, in the CEO's words: "We want to get this kind of boom and bust out of it. It doesn't work for anybody" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Cantor]. On price: "I think mid-80s gross margin, I would characterize as a fair return", with NBMs at "about 80% gross margin. And then the rest of the portfolio floats" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Cantor].

At the Investor Day a week later management put numbers on the long term. For FY2028 through FY2030 it "expects revenue to grow mid-to-high teens, consistent with bit growth, and expects non-GAAP gross margins to sustain at approximately 80 percent with non-GAAP operating margins at approximately 75 percent", with adjusted free cash flow margin "at approximately 50 percent" [2026 Investor Day release]. And: "we expect to return 100 percent of excess cash to our shareholders after investing in the business" [2026 Investor Day release]. "Excess cash" is not defined; buybacks, not dividends, are the chosen route as "more tax efficient" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Goldman Sachs]. Our read: the model assumes today's pricing holds for four more years on two-thirds of bits while the other third floats; business.md §3's FY2023 column is the case it must survive.

## 3. Growth engines

**Datacenter (enterprise SSDs).** $2,977M in Q4, a third of revenue and 38% of bits, up from 12% of bits a year earlier [Q4 FY2026 release; Q4 FY2026 slides, p.8]. "This quarter we began shipping our QLC Stargate platform for revenue", a high-capacity drive line (QLC is a denser, lower-cost type of NAND cell) [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.5]. Tell: Datacenter revenue and bit share keep rising; a first QLC revenue figure.

**NBMs.** Eight customers; RPO $59.8B at quarter-end and $91.1B counting two later deals; "more than 50% of our bits in fiscal year 2027, and approximately 2/3 of our bits in fiscal year 2028" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.11; Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]. Management is "still in deep conversations with additional customers" while "highly selective" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Goldman Sachs; Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]. Tell: RPO and customer deposits rising; new signings; no renegotiated floors.

**Node transitions (BiCS 8 to BiCS 10).** BiCS 10 was "Sampling in August 2026" [2026 Investor Day slides, p.27]. FY2027 capex rises in dollars "as we ramp BiCS 8 and BiCS 10", while "the higher inventory levels reduce sellable bits to mid-teens for the full year fiscal year 2027" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]. Tell: gross capex near 6% of revenue; bit growth statements; BiCS 10 ramp dates holding.

**Edge recovery.** PCs and smartphones are "working through a period of adjustment"; "We expect these markets to return to growth in calendar year '27" [Q4 FY2026 call, CEO prepared remarks]. Tell: Edge bits and revenue direction in the MD&A.

**High-bandwidth flash (HBF).** A future product, not revenue: the Investor Day roadmap shows "FIRST HBF MEMORY DIE TAPED OUT" and "FIRST HBF INFERENCE PRODUCT SAMPLES" in 2027 (a die is "taped out" when its design is finished and sent for manufacture) [2026 Investor Day slides, p.96]. Tell: sample dates and named customers.

## 4. Guidance

**Q1 FY2027**, from the release's "Business Outlook for Fiscal First Quarter of 2027" [Q4 FY2026 release]:

| (in millions, except per share amounts) | GAAP | Non-GAAP |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $10,300 - $10,800 | $10,300 - $10,800 |
| Gross Margin | 83.0% - 84.9% | 83.0% - 85.0% |
| Operating Expenses | $574 - $614 | $520 - $540 |
| Tax Expense | N/A | 15.0% |
| Diluted Net Income Per Share | N/A | $44.00 - $46.00 |
| Diluted Shares Outstanding | ~ 155 | ~ 155 |

The CFO: "we expect revenue between $10.3 billion and $10.8 billion with sequential growth driven by both bit growth and higher pricing" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]. Two items guided in April are absent: the interest and other income row ($10–30M non-GAAP for Q4) and a dollar tax range ($775–875M), replaced by a 15.0% rate [Q3 FY2026 release; Q4 FY2026 release].

**FY2027** (no revenue, margin or EPS guidance): "Consistent with our long-term objective of growing supply mid- to high teens, our capital spending will increase year-over-year, primarily as we ramp BiCS 8 and BiCS 10, yet our investment relative to revenue will come down to approximately 6% for the full year." "We plan to operate at higher inventory days, consistent with current levels to support our NBMs and account for higher component costs. The higher inventory levels reduce sellable bits to mid-teens for the full year fiscal year 2027." [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]

**Capital return:** "Sandisk's Board of Directors has authorized an additional $14 billion share repurchase program, bringing our total remaining authorization to $15.5 billion" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]. On pace: "We expect to be very consistent in our execution of this program" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Melius Research].

1. **Q1 FY2027 revenue lands between $10,300M and $10,800M.** "Expect first quarter 2027 revenue to be in the range of $10.30 billion to $10.80 billion" [Q4 FY2026 release]
2. **Q1 FY2027 non-GAAP gross margin is at least 83.0%.** Gross Margin, Non-GAAP: "83.0% - 85.0%" [Q4 FY2026 release]
3. **The Q1 FY2027 release attributes sequential revenue growth to both higher volumes and higher pricing (both positive).** "sequential growth driven by both bit growth and higher pricing" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]
4. **Datacenter revenue in Q1 FY2027 exceeds Q4's $2,977M (our sharpening of "fastest-growing end market", not management's number).** "Datacenter is our fastest-growing end market and a central pillar of our long-term growth" [Q4 FY2026 slides, p.8]
5. **(Disclosure check, quarter-end balance.) RPO in the Q1 FY2027 10-Q's revenue note is above the $59.8B reported at July 3, 2026.** "would be $91.1 billion including the 2 NBMs signed after the quarter closed" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]
6. **Management reports NBMs with more than eight customers by the Q1 FY2027 call.** "we're definitely still in deep conversations with additional customers" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Goldman Sachs]
7. **(Disclosure check, quarter-end balances.) Contract liabilities plus refund liabilities on the Q1 FY2027 balance sheet exceed the $2,742M at July 3, 2026 (computed).** "For each of the existing deals, the financial guarantees are released towards the end of the agreement" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]
8. **Days in inventory reported for Q1 FY2027 is at least 160 (our sharpening of "consistent with current levels", which were 178 days, not management's number).** "We plan to operate at higher inventory days, consistent with current levels to support our NBMs" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]
9. **Q1 FY2027 gross capex on the slides' capex page exceeds Q4's $562M (our sharpening of "will increase year-over-year", not management's number).** "our capital spending will increase year-over-year, primarily as we ramp BiCS 8 and BiCS 10" [Q4 FY2026 call, CFO prepared remarks]
10. **Sandisk repurchases at least $3.0B of stock in Q1 FY2027 (our sharpening of "very consistent" against $4.5B in Q4, not management's number).** "We expect to be very consistent in our execution of this program" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Melius Research]
