## 2. What management says about the core business

Nadella's frame is cost per result: "We are advancing the frontier on the cost-to-outcome curve, ensuring every customer can turn tokens into business results" [Q4 FY2026 release, highlights]. Underneath is an architecture claim: the software around the model is built so that "every model is substitutable", and "The frontier is about every firm having a frontier" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella] [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Keirstead/UBS]. The pricing model is moving "beyond per-seat to per-seat-plus-consumption" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella].

Hood's frame is supply. "Customer demand continues to exceed available capacity", and unlike April, when she said "we expect to remain constrained at least through 2026", she gave no end date [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood] [Q3 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]. Asked whether capex returns had changed: "I don’t know that, quite frankly, my math has changed in terms of how I do it over the past year" [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Borges/Goldman Sachs].

## 3. Growth engines

**Azure and AI infrastructure.** Azure passed $100 billion in FY2026, up 41% [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. Microsoft added "another gigawatt of capacity this quarter" and is "on track to roughly double our overall capacity in just two years"; Foundry, its platform for building AI applications, has "100,000 Foundry customers, and revenue more than doubled year-over-year" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. Working if Azure reaches the "approximately 45%" guided for Q1 with Microsoft Cloud gross margin near 65%.

**Microsoft 365 Copilot and the Copilot family.** "over 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, with net seat adds more than doubling quarter-over-quarter"; GitHub Copilot has "50 million users" and its revenue "accelerated over 60% quarter-over-quarter" after a move to usage-based pricing [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. No Copilot revenue figure is given [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. Working if Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud growth accelerates through FY2027 as guided and the seat count keeps rising.

**OpenAI and the multi-model strategy.** Foundry offers "over 11,000 models, including the latest from OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, xAI, as well as our own MAI family" (MAI: Microsoft's own models), and customers building with several providers rose "5X" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. Nearly 90% of cloud revenue comes from "customers outside of frontier model companies" (the companies that build the leading AI models); OpenAI itself was barely discussed, and the $24.1 billion of FY2026 revenue from it and the roughly 25% stake are in the 10-K, not the call [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood] [10-K FY2026, Note 1]. Working if bookings and RPO excluding OpenAI keep growing at double digits while the headline figures do.

**Security.** Project Perception, "a complete multi-model agentic security system", launched that week [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]. No security revenue was given [Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, Zelnick/Deutsche Bank]. Working if a figure returns.

Xbox and LinkedIn were not framed as engines; Xbox is to "return the business to growth in fiscal 2027" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella].

Verbatim, where the wording matters:

- **Q1 capex:** "We expect CapEx spend will be over $50 billion including the lease reclassification impact from the useful life update." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
- **Calendar 2026 capex and the definition change:** "Outside of this useful life impact, our calendar year 2026 CapEx investment expectations remain unchanged. However, the shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to approximately $175 billion." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
- **FY2027:** "At the company level, with strong commercial momentum, we continue to expect another fiscal year of double-digit revenue and operating income growth." "Even as we invest to meet growing demand, full fiscal year operating margins should be down less than a point. In addition, we expect to remain free cash flow positive in FY27." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
- **Azure:** "In Azure, we expect revenue growth of approximately 45% in constant currency and we remain focused on delivering efficiencies that help us bridge the gaps we see as customer demand continues to exceed supply. Even with the strong close to Q4, we continue to expect H1 growth to accelerate." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]

Not guided: earnings per share, a FY2027 revenue growth rate, a FY2027 capex dollar figure, headcount [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood].

## 5. Claims to verify next quarter

1. **Q1 FY2027 Azure and other cloud services revenue growth in constant currency is at least 45%.** Our sharpening of "approximately" into a floor. "In Azure, we expect revenue growth of approximately 45% in constant currency" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
2. **Q1 FY2027 capital expenditures including finance leases exceed $50 billion.** "We expect CapEx spend will be over $50 billion including the lease reclassification impact from the useful life update." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
3. **Q1 FY2027 Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage is at least 64%.** Our sharpening of "relatively stable" from Q4's 65% into a one-point floor. "Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage should be relatively stable quarter-over-quarter." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
4. **Q1 FY2027 total revenue is at least $89.85 billion.** "revenue should be between $89.85 and $90.95 billion or growth of 16% to 17%" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
5. **Q1 FY2027 operating margin is within one point of Q1 FY2026's 48.9% (computed from 37,961 / 77,673).** Our sharpening of "relatively flat" into a one-point band. "Operating margins should be relatively flat year-over-year." [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood] [IR workbook FY26Q4, Segment History]
6. **Q1 FY2027 Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue growth in constant currency, as reported, is at least 15%.** Our sharpening of "approximately" into a floor. "approximately 16% in constant currency on an adjusted basis" ... "or 15% on a reported basis" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
7. **On the Q1 FY2027 call, management again discloses a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seat count, and it is above 30 million.** "we now have over 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, with net seat adds more than doubling quarter-over-quarter" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Nadella]
8. **On the Q1 FY2027 call, management reaffirms or raises the calendar-2026 capex expectation of approximately $175 billion.** "the shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to approximately $175 billion" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
9. **On the Q1 FY2027 call, management reaffirms FY2027 double-digit growth in both revenue and operating income.** "we continue to expect another fiscal year of double-digit revenue and operating income growth" [Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, Hood]
