## Reference scheme used in notes-transcript.md

The Word documents have no printed page numbers and `transcript.txt` carries no line numbers. Every note is tagged `[Q4 FY2026 call, prepared remarks, <speaker surname>]` or `[Q4 FY2026 call, Q&A, <analyst surname>/<firm>]`; supplement notes use `[Q3 FY2026 call, <section>]`. Where a quote does not open its paragraph, or a bullet paraphrases, the note adds `¶“<first five words of the paragraph>”` so the paragraph can be found with `grep -F` (the text uses curly apostrophes). The writer and reviewer should use the same tags.
