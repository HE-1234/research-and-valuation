## 1. Business description and segments (company's own words)

- Mission: "Our mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more." Self-description: "Microsoft is a technology company committed to making digital technology and artificial intelligence ('AI') available broadly and doing so responsibly." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- "Founded in 1975, we develop and support software, services, devices, and solutions that deliver new value for customers and help people and businesses realize their full potential." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- How money comes in (MD&A wording): "We generate revenue by offering a wide range of cloud-based solutions, content, and other services to people and businesses; licensing and supporting an array of software products; delivering relevant online advertising to a global audience; and designing and selling devices. Our most significant expenses are related to compensating employees; supporting and investing in our cloud-based services, including datacenter operations; designing, manufacturing, marketing, and selling our other products and services; and income taxes." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Three "ambitions" that drive R&D: "Reinvent productivity and business processes"; "Build the intelligent cloud and intelligent edge platform"; "Create more personal computing experiences". [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Cloud economics in the company's words: "Our cloud business benefits from three economies of scale: datacenters that deploy computational resources at significantly lower cost per unit than smaller ones; datacenters that coordinate and aggregate diverse customer, geographic, and application demand patterns, improving the utilization of computing, storage, and network resources; and multi-tenancy locations that lower application maintenance labor costs." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- "Our future growth depends on our ability to transcend current product category definitions, business models, and sales motions." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Three reportable segments: Productivity and Business Processes; Intelligent Cloud; More Personal Computing. The chief operating decision maker is the CEO; the segment profit measure is operating income; assets are not allocated to segments. [10-K FY2026, Note 18]

**Productivity and Business Processes (recast composition):**
- Microsoft 365 Commercial products and cloud services: "Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud, comprising Microsoft 365 Commercial, Enterprise Mobility + Security, the cloud portion of Windows Commercial, the per-user portion of Power BI, Exchange, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 Security and Compliance, and Microsoft 365 Copilot; and Microsoft 365 Commercial products, comprising Windows Commercial on-premises and Office licensed on-premises." Revenue "is mainly affected by a combination of continued installed base growth and average revenue per user expansion, as well as the continued shift from Office licensed on-premises to Microsoft 365." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Microsoft 365 Consumer products and cloud services: subscriptions, Office licensed on-premises, other consumer services; revenue "mainly affected by the percentage of customers that buy Office with their new devices and the continued shift from Office licensed on-premises to Microsoft 365 Consumer subscriptions", plus Outlook.com/OneDrive demand "largely driven by subscriptions and advertising". [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- LinkedIn: free services plus paid "Talent Solutions, Marketing Solutions, Premium Subscriptions, and Sales Solutions"; revenue "mainly affected by demand from enterprises and professionals for subscriptions ... as well as member engagement and the quality of the sponsored content delivered to those members to drive Marketing Solutions." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Dynamics: cloud (Dynamics 365, Power Apps, Power Automate) and on-premises ERP/CRM; revenue "driven by the number of users licensed and applications consumed, expansion of average revenue per user, and the continued shift to Dynamics 365". [10-K FY2026, Item 1]

**Intelligent Cloud:**
- Server products and cloud services: "Azure and other cloud services, comprising cloud and AI consumption-based services, GitHub cloud services, Health and Life Sciences cloud services (formerly Nuance Healthcare cloud services), virtual desktop offerings, and other cloud services; and Server products, comprising SQL Server, Windows Server, Visual Studio, System Center, related Client Access Licenses ('CALs'), and other on-premises offerings." "Azure revenue is mainly affected by infrastructure-as-a-service and platform-as-a-service consumption-based services." Server products revenue "mainly affected by purchases through volume licensing programs, licenses sold to OEMs, and retail packaged products." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Azure AI: "We offer supercomputing power for AI at scale to run large workloads, complemented by our rapidly expanding portfolio of AI cloud services (including the latest models) and hardware, which includes custom-built silicon and strong partnerships with chip manufacturers. Azure AI Foundry is a unified platform for developers to design, customize, and manage AI applications and agents." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Enterprise and partner services: Enterprise Support Services, Industry Solutions, Microsoft Partner Network, Learning Experience. [10-K FY2026, Item 1]

**More Personal Computing:**
- Windows and Devices: "Windows OEM licensing (Windows Pro and non-Pro licenses sold through the OEM channel) and Devices, comprising Surface and PC accessories." "Windows OEM revenue is impacted significantly by the number of Windows operating system licenses purchased by OEMs, which they pre-install on the devices they sell." Other listed drivers: device mix by form factor and screen size, developed vs growth markets, "Growth of the AI PC category", attachment, customer mix, "Changes in inventory levels in the OEM channel", pricing/promotions, supply-chain constraints, piracy. [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- XBOX (renamed from "Gaming" in FY2026): "XBOX hardware and XBOX content and services, comprising first- and third-party content (including games and in-game content), XBOX Game Pass and other subscriptions, XBOX Cloud Gaming, advertising, and other cloud services." "XBOX revenue is mainly affected by subscriptions and sales of first- and third-party content, as well as advertising." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Search advertising (renamed from "Search and news advertising" in FY2026): "Bing, Copilot, Microsoft News, Microsoft Edge, and third-party affiliates." "Growth depends on our ability to attract new users, understand intent, and match intent with relevant content on advertising offerings." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Distribution: direct sales to enterprises/public sector/SMBs, an indirect partner network, OEMs that "license and preinstall certain Microsoft software on devices", online channels and digital marketplaces; volume licensing programs for organisations of all sizes. [10-K FY2026, Item 1]
- Revenue recognition pattern: on-premises licenses recognised upfront; cloud subscriptions ratably; consumption-based cloud "based on the customer utilization of such resources"; Office 365 treated as one performance obligation recognised ratably; hardware recognised when ownership transfers to resellers or end customers. [10-K FY2026, Note 1]
- Seasonality: "Our revenue fluctuates quarterly and is generally higher in the fourth quarter of our fiscal year. Fourth quarter revenue is driven by a higher volume of multi-year contracts executed during the period." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Competition (FY2026 wording no longer names rivals): "Our AI offerings compete with AI products from hyperscalers, as well as products from other emerging competitors and other open-source offerings, many of which are also current or potential partners." [10-K FY2026, Item 1]. The FY2024 10-K did name them: "Azure faces diverse competition from companies such as Amazon, Broadcom, Google, IBM, Oracle, and open source offerings ... Our AI offerings compete with AI products from hyperscalers such as Amazon and Google, as well as products from other emerging competitors, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and other open source offerings"; Search "competes with Google, OpenAI, and a wide array of websites, social platforms like Meta". [10-K FY2024, Item 1]
- History milestones stated in filings: founded 1975 [10-K FY2026, Item 1]; ZeniMax acquired March 2021 for $8.1 billion; Nuance acquired March 4, 2022 for $18.8 billion [10-K FY2023, Item 1A; 10-K FY2022, Note 8]; Activision Blizzard acquired October 13, 2023 for $75.4 billion "consisting primarily of cash", goodwill $51,001 million and intangibles $21,969 million, reported in More Personal Computing [10-K FY2026, Note 7]; OpenAI partnership "originally established in 2019", "third phase" announced January 2023, extended October 2025 and April 2026 [10-K FY2026, Item 7; 10-K FY2025, Item 1A]; segment recast August 2024 [10-K FY2025, Note 1]; Secure Future Initiative launched November 2023 and expanded May 2024 [10-K FY2026, Item 1C].
- Sustainability goals: "In 2020, we announced goals to become a carbon negative, water positive, and zero waste company by 2030." Risk factor adds: "AI development and deployment has and will likely continue to raise energy use and emissions, making it harder to meet these goals." [10-K FY2026, Item 1; Item 1A]
- Properties: approximately 112 million sq ft (58 owned, 54 leased); 14 million sq ft in King County, Washington, on ~520 owned acres. [10-K FY2026, Item 2]

### 2g. FY2026 growth drivers by line (management's words)

- Microsoft 365 Commercial products and cloud services +$14.2 billion or 16%; "Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue grew 17% with growth in revenue per user driven by Microsoft 365 Copilot and Microsoft 365 E5. Microsoft 365 Commercial seats grew 6% driven by small and medium businesses and frontline worker offerings. Microsoft 365 Commercial products revenue grew 13% driven by growth in the Windows Commercial on-premises components of Microsoft 365 suite sales, as well as an increase in Office 2024 transactional purchasing." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Microsoft 365 Consumer +$1.8 billion or 24%; "Microsoft 365 Consumer cloud revenue grew 28% driven by growth in revenue per user and Microsoft 365 Consumer subscriber growth of 7%." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- LinkedIn +$2.0 billion or 11% "with growth across all lines of business." Dynamics +$1.2 billion or 15%; "Dynamics 365 revenue grew 18% with growth across all workloads." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Server products and cloud services +$31.0 billion or 31%; "Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 41% driven by demand for services across the platform with continued growth across all workloads. Server products revenue increased 1% primarily driven by higher purchases of licenses running in multi-cloud environments, offset in part by continued customer shift to cloud." Enterprise and partner services +$500 million or 6%. [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Windows and Devices −$230 million or 1%: "Windows OEM and Devices revenue decreased slightly driven by a decline in Devices, offset in part by Windows OEM growth of 5% with inventory levels that remained elevated." The 10-Q for Q3 adds the reason: "growth in Windows OEM as OEM partners continue to build inventory due to increasing memory pricing." [10-K FY2026, Item 7; 10-Q Q3 FY2026, Item 2]
- XBOX −$1.7 billion or 7%: "XBOX content and services revenue decreased 5% on a prior year comparable that benefited from strong first-party content performance, offset in part by growth in XBOX Game Pass. XBOX hardware revenue decreased 29% driven by lower volume of consoles sold." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Search advertising +$1.3 billion or 9%; ex-TAC +12% "driven by higher search volume and revenue per search, as well as benefit from third-party partnerships." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
- Prior-year growth rates for comparison: FY2025 Azure +34%, Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud +15%, seats +6%, Consumer subscribers +8% to 89.0 million, LinkedIn +9%, Dynamics 365 +19%, Windows OEM and Devices +3%, Xbox content and services +16%, Search ex-TAC +20% [10-K FY2025, Item 7]. FY2024 Azure +30%, Office 365 Commercial +16% with seat growth 7%, Consumer subscribers 82.5 million (+10%), LinkedIn +9%, Dynamics 365 +24%, Windows OEM +7%, Devices −15%, Xbox content and services +50% "driven by 44 points of net impact from the Activision Blizzard ... acquisition", Search ex-TAC +12% [10-K FY2024, Item 7].
- Foreign currency: FY2026 "Revenue and operating income both included a favorable foreign currency impact of 2%." [10-K FY2026, Item 7]
