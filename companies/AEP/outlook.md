## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is growth paid for by customers who have signed up for it. Fehrman: "Our future is all about growth well into the next decade." [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Fehrman]. The CFO: "One of the most important drivers of our sustained long-term growth outlook continues to be large load demand." [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Mihalik].

Affordability is the flip side: "As new large load comes online, it enables a shift of fixed costs currently borne by existing customers to new data centers and hyperscalers." [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Fehrman]. On regulatory lag the CFO named "the UTM in Texas, SB 998 in Oklahoma, and a forward-looking test year in Ohio starting in 2028" (a Texas cost tracker, an Oklahoma cost-deferral law, and rates set on projected rather than past costs) as the path "for regulated earned ROE to improve to 9.5% by 2030" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Mihalik]. On generation: "it's a scarce resource and will become increasingly more valuable" [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Morgan Stanley]. Management is "clearly looking into the GenCo structure" (an unregulated generation company; speaker label unreliable) [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Wells Fargo].

## 3. Growth engines

**Large-load contracts.** 69 GW by 2030: 45 GW in ERCOT (preliminary letters of agreement only), 18 GW in PJM ("~95% ESAs", the binding electric service agreements), 6 GW in SPP ("~55% ESAs") [Q2 2026 slides, p.14]. AEP Texas submitted 45 GW into ERCOT's Batch Zero backed by "nearly $2B in customer cash or collateral commitments"; ERCOT names eligible projects in August 2026 and allocates capacity in April 2027 [Q2 2026 slides, p.15]. Working if the GW count rises, ERCOT accepts the projects and commercial kWh growth stays in double digits.

**Generation.** "approximately 13 GW" of gas turbines secured through 2031 and "up to 10 GW" more under evaluation to 2035, against 27 GW of projected resource needs [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 slides, p.25]. "roughly about half" of the 13 GW is in the current plan [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Truist Securities]. Working if commissions approve the plants (Sycamore order expected December 2026; Rockport Energy Center Q1 2027) and generation capex rises in the 2027–2031 plan [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2].

**Transmission.** $33 billion of the $78 billion plan; Holdco rate base projected from $15 billion in 2025 to $26 billion in 2030; the Piketon 765-kV project ($4.2 billion) still outside the plan [Q2 2026 slides, p.21] [Q2 2026 slides, p.22]. Working if Holdco earnings turn favourable year over year by December and Piketon signs.

**Regulatory constructs.** Large-load tariffs approved in five of eight states, the rest targeted "by end of 2026"; earned ROE targeted at 9.5% by 2030 [Q2 2026 slides, p.8] [Q2 2026 slides, p.28]. Working if earned ROE rises from 9.2% and approved ROEs hold above 9.5%.

## 4. Guidance

AEP guides for the full year only. Verbatim:

- **2026 operating EPS (earnings per share):** "AEP is raising its full-year 2026 operating earnings guidance to $6.25 to $6.55 per share from its previous guidance range of $6.15 to $6.45 per share" [Q2 2026 release, p.1]
- **GAAP basis:** "the estimated earnings per share on a GAAP basis would be $6.16 to $6.46 per share." Excluded items: mark-to-market hedging (paper gains or losses on hedges) $0.03; WVPSC (West Virginia commission) order $(0.07); Pirkey Plant disallowance $0.06; Unified Tracker Mechanism disallowance $0.04; wholesale customer contracts $0.04; tax effect $(0.01). [Q2 2026 release, p.4]
- **Long-term growth:** "reaffirmed its annual operating earnings growth rate of 7% to 9% through 2030, with an expected operating earnings compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of greater than 9%, based on the 2025 guidance midpoint and supported by AEP's five-year, $78 billion capital plan." [Q2 2026 release, p.1]
- **Capital:** "line of sight to incremental capital investments of more than $10 billion, including the fuel cell project in Wyoming, the Piketon transmission opportunity in Ohio, and incremental generation" [Q2 2026 release, p.1]; "When we introduce a new five-year plan for 2027 through 2031 during our third quarter earnings call" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Fehrman]
- **Credit and equity:** "our targeted FFO to debt ratio of 14%-15%"; "With this transaction, we have addressed all the anticipated marketed equity needs to support the $78 billion five-year capital plan." [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Mihalik]
- **Load and turbines:** "bringing total contracted load growth through 2030 to 69 GW"; "approximately 13 GW for potential deployment through 2031 ... up to 10 GW of additional turbine capacity through 2035." [Q2 2026 release, p.2]
- **Segment timing:** "Transmission Holdco earnings are expected to be favorable year-over-year by end of 2026, while income tax impacts are expected to reverse by the end of the year." [Q2 2026 slides, p.12]
- **Earned ROE and tariffs:** "Forecasted Regulated ROE is expected to improve to approximately 9.5% by 2030." [Q2 2026 slides, p.28] "targeting to obtain approval of remaining tariffs by end of 2026" [Q2 2026 slides, p.8]
