## 2. What management says about the core business

The frame is unchanged: demand is structurally rising, the fleet is built for it, and hedging smooths the ride. The CEO: "We remain on track to achieve another record result in 2026 as the business continues to perform very well" [Q2 2026 call, l.21]. The two markets have split: "the power price environment in ERCOT has softened recently. We view this as normal" [Q2 2026 call, l.37]; ERCOT North Hub (the main Texas price point) power averaged $31.19 per MWh in the first half of 2026 against $31.46 a year earlier [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2], and "30 is not going to get new stuff built" [Q2 2026 call, l.77]. His answer: "2026 being soft is not that big of a surprise to us. That's also why we do the comprehensive hedging that we do" [Q2 2026 call, l.71].

The load (electricity demand) view was restated a notch lower, "at least 4%-6% in ERCOT and 2%-3% in PJM through 2030", against "at least 5% to 6%" for ERCOT in May [Q2 2026 call, l.33] [Q1 2026 call, l.32]. Management declined to detail hedging by region or Helix megawatts [Q2 2026 call, l.215] [Q2 2026 call, l.249].

## 3. Growth engines

**Data-center PPAs (AWS at Comanche Peak, Meta at the PJM nuclear plants).** Now: 1,200 MW plus 2,609 MW signed, no revenue yet [8-K 2026-01-09, Item 7.01] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2]. Claim: at Comanche Peak "we're looking to energize at the end of 2027" [Q2 2026 call, l.69]. Working if: Meta deliveries start in late 2026 and the AWS date holds in each 10-Q.

**Cogentrix and Lotus gas plants.** Now: Lotus (about 2,600 MW) closed in October 2025; Cogentrix (about 5,500 MW) has FERC (federal energy regulator) approval and is "expected to close in late 2026" [Q2 2026 release, p.1] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2]. Claim: with the Meta PPAs, "you could reasonably conclude that they'd add roughly $700 million to our midpoint opportunity, absent any other impacts" [Q2 2026 call, l.167]. Working if: it closes by year-end and the next 2027 range includes the uplift.

**Helix Digital Infrastructure.** Now: "up to $1 billion" committed, nothing called; the 2026 plan assumes about $500 million goes out this year [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 7] [Q2 2026 slides, p.26]. Claim: "We would only do projects that hit our return targets" [Q2 2026 call, l.247]. Working if: a first Helix-sourced contract at a Vistra plant is announced.

**Uprates (extra output from upgrading existing plants) and new gas and solar builds.** Now: 433 MW of nuclear uprates listed "Under Development"; 860 MW of Permian gas peakers (plants run only at peak demand) under construction, "expected to be online by Q2 2028" [Q2 2026 slides, p.27] [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2] [Q2 2026 release, p.1] [Q2 2026 slides, p.10]. Working if: dates hold and uprates move to "In Construction".

**Retail.** Now: $773 million of Adjusted EBITDA versus $756 million a year ago [Q2 2026 release, p.2]. Claim: "the second and fourth quarters are typically the strongest quarters for retail" [Q2 2026 call, l.43]. Working if: full-year Retail Adjusted EBITDA lands near 2025's $1,622 million [Q2 2026 slides, p.35].

## 4. Guidance

Vistra guides annually. Verbatim:

- **2026:** "Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA | $6,800 - $7,600"; "Ongoing Operations Adjusted FCFbG | $3,925 - $4,725" [Q2 2026 release, p.2]. "Given our performance through the first half of the year, we are confident in our ability to deliver at or above the midpoint of these ranges" [Q2 2026 call, l.45].
- **2027 (not guidance):** midpoint opportunity "range of $7.4 billion to $7.8 billion for 2027" unchanged; "The ranges exclude any potential benefits from the pending acquisition of Cogentrix and the signed power purchase agreements with Meta, part of which are expected to contribute to our Adjusted EBITDA in 2027" [Q2 2026 release, p.2]. On the call, with ERCOT forwards (prices for future delivery) "meaningfully lower": "we would be trending towards the lower end of that range" [Q2 2026 call, l.45] [Q2 2026 call, l.165].
- **Next update:** "we'll provide a guidance update for 2026 and 2027 on the third quarter earnings call. If Cogentrix hasn't closed at that time, we'll wait and likely provide an update to earnings for 2027 on our next earnings call after it closes" [Q2 2026 call, l.165].
- **Capital plan:** "we will generate more than $10 billion of available cash in 2026 and 2027" [Q2 2026 call, l.47]; "Targeting at least ~$1 billion in share repurchases and ~$300 million in common dividends annually" [Q2 2026 slides, p.10].
- **Hedges:** "approximately 100% of its expected generation volumes for 2026, approximately 94% for 2027, and approximately 72% for 2028" as of August 3 [Q2 2026 release, p.2].
