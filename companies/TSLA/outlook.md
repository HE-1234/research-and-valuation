## 2. What management says about the core business

The deck: "Our focus remains on strengthening these core businesses and making the necessary investments that will deliver Amazing Abundance" [Q2 2026 Update, p.3]; the profit sentence in §4 is unchanged since January [Q2 2026 Update, p.10] [Q4 2025 Update, p.13]. In plain words: cars and batteries pay the bills now; software, robotaxis and robots are meant to pay them later.

The CFO on cars: "a resurgence in demand for our vehicles across the globe" and "our largest order backlog since 2023", with one brake: "Production growth will be limited by our supply chain" [Q2 2026 call, Taneja prepared remarks]. The CEO: buyers are "actually buying Tesla full self-driving with a car attached", and 2026 is "a massive CapEx year" [Q2 2026 call, Musk prepared remarks]. Nothing was said about the fall in regulatory credit revenue, about pricing plans, or about the CEO award.

## 3. Growth engines

Everything here is management's claim, not fact.

**Energy storage.** How big: $3.1 billion revenue and 13.5 GWh in Q2 2026 [Q2 2026 Update, p.4] [Q2 2026 Update, p.5]. Claim: "Our order backlog of this business is robust"; the margin claim is claim 3 [Q2 2026 call, Taneja prepared remarks]. Working if GWh grow year over year and the margin holds above 20%.

**FSD subscriptions.** How big: 1.48 million active, up 56%; revenue not disclosed [Q2 2026 Update, p.5]. Claim: growth will come from subscriptions, not upfront purchases (claim 8) [Q2 2026 call, Taneja prepared remarks]. Working if the count and the attach rate (share of new deliveries taking FSD) rise each quarter.

**Robotaxi and Cybercab.** How big: seven US metros, six without a safety driver; fleet, rides and revenue not disclosed [Q2 2026 Update, p.9]. Claims: more metros this year (claim 7); "Cybercab began production at Gigafactory Texas" [Q2 2026 call, Taneja prepared remarks] [Q2 2026 Update, p.3]. Working if new metros go live and Cybercabs carry paying customers.

**Optimus.** How big: nothing sold; lines being installed at Fremont [Q2 2026 Update, p.6]. Claim: "we will soon start production with Optimus" (claim 6); no volumes or dates [Q2 2026 call, Musk prepared remarks]. Working if the capacity table shows Optimus in production.

**AI chips and Terafab.** How big: an Austin fab (chip factory) at "construction and equipment procurement" [Q2 2026 Update, p.8]. Claims: "The Terafab, we expect to announce the location soon"; "AI5, which hopefully is in volume production around the middle of next year" [Q2 2026 call, Musk prepared remarks] [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Truist]. Working if a location and a date appear.

**New models.** The Semi and Megapack 3 are promised for 2026 (§4; claims 4 and 5). Two disagreements: the CEO said "We have started production with the Tesla semi-truck" while the deck lists Nevada Semi as "Commissioning" [Q2 2026 call, Musk prepared remarks] [Q2 2026 Update, p.6]; and the Q1 Outlook promised "volume production" of "Cybercab, Tesla Semi and Megapack 3"; Q2 dropped Cybercab and "volume" [Q1 2026 Update, p.10] [Q2 2026 Update, p.10].

## 4. Guidance

No numeric guidance is given. The Outlook page, verbatim and in full [Q2 2026 Update, p.10]:

- "Volume We are focused on maximum capacity utilization at our factories. Deliveries and deployments will be impacted by aggregate demand for our products, supply chain readiness and allocation decisions between sale to customers or use for our owned and operated fleet." [Q2 2026 Update, p.10]
- "Cash We will manage the business such that we ensure a strong balance sheet, maintaining sufficient liquidity to fund our product roadmap, long-term capacity expansion plans – including further vertical integration – and other expenses." [Q2 2026 Update, p.10]
- "Profit While we continue to execute on innovations to reduce the cost of manufacturing and operations, over time, we expect our hardware-related profits to be accompanied by an acceleration of AI, software and fleet-based profits." [Q2 2026 Update, p.10]
- "Product We continue to evolve and augment our product lineup with a focus on cost, scale and future monetization opportunities via services powered by our AI software. We remain focused on growing our sales volumes through a differentiated and efficiently managed product portfolio, which includes leveraging and optimizing our existing production capacity before building new factories and production lines. Tesla Semi and Megapack 3 remain on schedule for production starting in 2026. First-generation production lines for Optimus are being installed in anticipation of production in 2026. Capacity build out and ramp related to our multi-year infrastructure initiatives, including AI compute, solar, battery material and semiconductor manufacturing are underway." [Q2 2026 Update, p.10]

The only other numbers and dates: capex "in excess of $25 billion in 2026" (claims 1 and 2) [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2], the energy margin range (claim 3), robotaxi expansion (claim 7), and:

- "CapEx will grow for the next two to three years"; operating expenses "continue to grow in 2026 and beyond" [Q2 2026 call, Taneja prepared remarks].
