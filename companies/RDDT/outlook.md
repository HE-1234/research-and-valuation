## 2. What management says about the core business

Huffman's frame is that AI makes Reddit more valuable, not less: "AI compresses the internet into summaries. Reddit delivers the opposite: deep discussions, passionate debates, and lived experiences. People don't want a summary of Reddit; they want Reddit" [Q2 2026 call, p.3]. The goal is unchanged and undated: "our goal of 1 billion daily users globally and 100 million in the U.S. That work takes time to gain traction" [Q2 2026 call, p.4]. The route is frequency: "Our top priority remains growing our daily active user base by improving the experience for new users, so they come back more frequently" [Q2 2026 call, p.4]. On search traffic he drew the line the whole report turns on: "external search is volatile, particularly logged-out web, but that's not where our business lives"; "the app users are worth multiples more than the search referral traffic" [Q2 2026 call, p.14] [Q2 2026 call, p.23]. He also conceded the product gap: the feed is "just behind the state-of-the-art" and its models use "like 10% of our user activity" [Q2 2026 call, p.13] [Q2 2026 call, p.19].

## 3. Growth engines

**Performance ads and automation: Dynamic Product Ads, Reddit Max, Shopify.** Dynamic Product Ads and app-install revenue each "more than doubling year-over-year" [Q2 2026 call, p.5]. Max (AI-run campaigns) is in private beta (an invite-only test); advertisers using it grew "over 60% from Q1" and its revenue "over 150%", and the plan is to "expand Max to a broader range of advertisers" [Q2 2026 call, p.6]. The integration with Shopify, the platform many small online stores run on, went generally available in May with "more than 500 accounts"; the mid-market and small-business channel "doubled year-over-year" (call figures) [Q2 2026 call, p.5] [Q2 2026 call, p.7]. Working if: the Max and Shopify figures are updated and higher, and ad-price growth in the 10-Q stays well above zero.

**Video.** Video views (awareness ads) were "our fastest-growing upper-funnel objective, more than doubling revenue year-over-year"; video in the home feed is for "testing later this year" [Q2 2026 call, p.6] [Q2 2026 call, p.17]. Working if: home-feed video reaches testing by year-end.

**Users: web-to-app conversion, retention, the feed rebuild.** New-app-user retention "was up 50% year-over-year on a relative basis ... from a small base"; the feed rebuild (bigger models, faster updates, more signals, older posts) runs "over the next year" [Q2 2026 call, p.4] [Q2 2026 call, p.19]. Working if: global DAUq keeps rising quarter on quarter, U.S. DAUq stops falling, and the DAUq/WAUq ratio turns up from 25%.

**Search and Reddit Answers.** "We grew both searchers and searches in the quarter"; ads on search are "a space that we're eyeing", no date [Q2 2026 call, p.17] [Q2 2026 call, p.18]. Working if: a search-ad product is announced, or a searcher count is disclosed again (the 10-K gave "over 80 million" weekly for Q4 2025) [10-K FY2025, Item 1].

**International.** International DAUq grew 28% and revenue 84% to $166.8 million, 21% of the total (computed) [Q2 2026 release, p.6]. Machine translation feeds it, but "we saw a decline in our machine translated content" in search [Q2 2026 call, p.15]. Working if: international DAUq growth stays above 20% and international ARPU keeps rising year over year.

**Data licensing.** "No change in strategy, working hard"; "an expanding marketplace with lots of different opportunities"; no renewal timing, no names [Q2 2026 call, p.13] [Q2 2026 call, p.15]. Working if: remaining performance obligations stop falling, or new 2027-and-later amounts appear in the 10-Q.

## 4. Guidance

Verbatim. Only the two Q3 ranges are in writing; the rest was spoken on the call. No full-year revenue or Adjusted EBITDA guidance; no user, gross-margin or capex guidance.

- **Q3 revenue:** "Revenue in the range of $860 million to $870 million" [Q2 2026 release, p.2] [Q2 2026 letter, p.12]; on the call, "representing 47% to 49% year-over-year revenue growth with a midpoint of about 48%" [Q2 2026 call, p.9].
- **Q3 Adjusted EBITDA:** "Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $385 million to $395 million" [Q2 2026 release, p.2]; on the call, "representing 63% to 67% year-over-year growth and an adjusted EBITDA margin of 45% at the midpoint" [Q2 2026 call, p.10].
- **Full-year stock-based compensation (lowered):** "we are lowering our full year expense guide for SBC from the high teens as a percentage of revenue to the low to the mid-teens as a percentage of revenue." [Q2 2026 call, p.10]
- **Q3 stock-based compensation:** "In Q3, we do expect SBC and related taxes to elevate to be between $140 to $155 million" [Q2 2026 call, p.10]
- **Full-year dilution (share-count growth):** "We also expect our full year dilution to be at the lower end of our 1% to 3% targeted range." [Q2 2026 call, p.10]
- **Long-term margin:** "The long-term goal is 50%." [Q2 2026 call, p.23]
