- [Q1 2026 call] CFO Stefan Murry: "While we continue to be encouraged by the conversations we are having with our customers pertaining to our 1.6T product, we continue to believe that our 800G products will drive the near-term data center ramp."

- [Q1 2026 call] CFO Stefan Murry: "We believe our in-house laser capabilities continue to be an advantage for the company.
