## 2. What management says about the core business

From the CEO in August: "Q2 was a pivotal quarter for AOI. We delivered record revenue for our fifth consecutive quarter and achieved an important milestone as we returned to non-GAAP profitability in the quarter. Further, we saw a strong volume ramp of our 800G products, which more than doubled sequentially," (sequentially: compared with the prior quarter) and "we forecast that demand will continue to outpace our production capacity through mid-2027", and "we are uniquely positioned as a key supplier to the AI, cloud infrastructure, and CATV markets" [Q2 2026 release]. On the May call the CFO named the constraint: 2026 revenue "is limited by our production capacity and supply chain, not market demand, which we believe is much larger" [Q1 2026 call].

## 3. Growth engines

**800G and 1.6T transceivers.** 800G volume "more than doubled sequentially"; the last disclosed 800G revenue was $4.6 million in Q1, said on the May call [Q2 2026 release; Q1 2026 call]. Capacity is "approaching 200,000 units per month" against a year-end target of "around 650,000 pieces of 800G and 1.6 Tb products per month" [Q2 2026 release]. In May the CFO said 1.6T was "on track to begin to contribute to our overall revenue later this year" [Q1 2026 call]. Signs to watch: data center revenue against the Q3 guide; any 1.6T statement.

**Texas manufacturing.** The 210,000 sq ft Sugar Land plant is to "begin production later in 2026"; in May the CFO said "initial production in this facility in the third quarter", with "about 30%" of year-end 800G and 1.6T output from Texas [10-Q Q2 2026, Item 2; Q1 2026 call]. US-manufactured revenue was $1.3 million in Q2, 0.7% of the total (computed) [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 17]. Sign to watch: that line in the 10-Q's geographic note.

**CATV 1.8 GHz amplifiers.** The release credits "high-volume adoption of our 1.8 GHz CATV products", and in May the CFO said "we now currently expect to generate over $325 million annually in CATV" [Q2 2026 release; Q1 2026 call]. Signs to watch: CATV at or above about $81 million a quarter (our arithmetic); Digicomm's share.

**A second hyperscaler; the Amazon warrant.** In May management described "another long-term major hyperscale customer" whose first 1.6T volume order and two 800G orders would "return this customer as a 10%-plus customer"; the sources do not say whether this is Amazon [Q1 2026 call]. The Q2 10-Q shows three unnamed customers above 10% (42%, 26%, 24%) and warrant-linked revenue of $46.8 million (computed), 24.4% of the quarter [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 2; Note 3]. Signs to watch: a third 10% customer persisting; further warrant tranches (portions of the warrant) vesting.

## 4. Guidance

**Q3 2026, from the release.** "For third quarter of 2026, the company currently expects:" "Revenue in the range of $255 million to $290 million." "Non-GAAP gross margin in the range of 29% to 30.5%." "Non-GAAP net income in the range of $10.1 million to $24.0 million, and non-GAAP income per share in the range of $0.11 to $0.26 using approximately 92.8 million shares." [Q2 2026 release] No GAAP or full-year guidance is given; the CFO says "we continue to anticipate steady sequential revenue growth this year" [Q2 2026 release].

**Standing statements from the Q1 2026 call that the Q2 2026 release neither repeats nor withdraws.** All said May 7, 2026 [Q1 2026 call]:

- "we now believe our 2026 revenue will exceed $1.1 billion, and we now expect to generate more than $140 million in non-GAAP operating income this year."
- "By the end of next year, 2027, we expect to grow our production capacity to be able to produce over 930 thousand pieces of 800G and 1.6T products per month, with over half of that output coming from Texas."
- By mid-2027, "about $471 million per month of data center transceiver revenue, with about 40% of this capacity in the U.S."
- CEO: "I would say we go to 35% gross margin by end of this year."

**Q2 2026, guided in May versus delivered.** Revenue "$180 million to $198 million" versus $191.9 million; non-GAAP gross margin "29% to 30%" versus 29.8%; non-GAAP net income "a loss of $2.5 million to income of $2.8 million" versus $5.5 million, above the range; CATV $75–80 million versus $80.6 million [Q1 2026 release; Q2 2026 release; Q1 2026 call].
