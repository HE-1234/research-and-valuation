## 2. What management says about the core business

Three priorities feed one loop: "We remain focused on consumer payments, commercial and new payment flows and value-added services and solutions, each with significant runway and together reinforcing Mastercard's virtuous cycle" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach]. The loop runs through the switch: "More switch transactions lead to more data to fuel our services and increase our ability to distribute services over our network" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach]. Switching penetration (the share of Mastercard-branded transactions that Mastercard itself switches) is "72%" (transcript-only figure), and the long-run case is that "only 1/3 of consumer transactions were carded" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach] [10-K FY2025, Item 1].

The CFO credited the beat to "lower-than-anticipated impact from the challenges in the Middle East, an uptick in cross-border spend out of Venezuela, where we are market leaders and strong demand for our value-added services and solutions" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Mehra]. On Europe's slowdown he defended passing on deals: "winning share of a portfolio which is growing at 1% helps me in the first year and then is a huge drag on growth for the years thereafter" [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Harshita Rawat].

## 3. Growth engines

**Value-added services and solutions.** $3,826 million in Q2, up 18% currency-neutral, led by security, consumer engagement, authentication, insights and pricing [Q2 2026 slides, p.6] [Q2 2026 release, p.2]. Working if growth stays in the high teens.

**Cross-border.** Volume up 12% and cross-border assessments up 20% currency-neutral to $3,460 million [Q2 2026 supplemental, p.8] [Q2 2026 slides, p.7]. Online cross-border spending excluding travel grew 20% in Q2 and travel 6%; July to the 28th ran 11% overall [Q2 2026 slides, p.9]. Working if the monthly table stays in double digits.

**Commercial and new payment flows.** Commercial credit and debit were 13% of 2025 GDV and grew 11%; Mastercard Move (its money-transfer service) reaches "more than 17 billion endpoints" (destinations it can pay out to) [10-K FY2025, Item 1]. Working if commercial GDV keeps outgrowing consumer in the FY2026 10-K.

**Agentic commerce (AI software that shops and pays on a person's behalf).** Agent Pay lets AI agents pay with tokenized cards; a machine-to-machine version launched with "more than 30 industry leaders participating" [10-K FY2025, Item 1] [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach]. No volume or revenue figure exists; working if one appears.

**Stablecoins (digital tokens pegged to a currency such as the dollar) and BVNK.** The pending $1.5 billion BVNK purchase is to make Mastercard "the trusted interoperable layer" across coins; Open USD, a consortium coin, "is set to go live later this year"; management sees "clear utility for stablecoins, for example, in some B2B and P2P flows, but no problem to solve in P2M" (business and person-to-person payments, not shop payments) [10-Q Q2 2026, Note 2] [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach]. Working if BVNK closes on time.

**Running the domestic switch.** The UAE central bank's payments arm chose Mastercard to build its switching; "These transactions are incremental to what we process through our global switch" [Q2 2026 call, prepared remarks, Miebach]. Working if switched transactions growth holds at or above GDV growth.
