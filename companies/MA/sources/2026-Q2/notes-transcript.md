## 4. Analyst Q&A, one bullet per question

1. **Ramsey El-Assal (Cantor Fitzgerald) — do agentic use cases need stablecoins, or can Mastercard credentials cover them; what unique problem do stablecoins solve?** Miebach: consumer agentic transactions "can happen very well through the existing card networks" because merchants "need reach. They need predictable user experiences"; "we really believe that cards will prevail in that world"; services upside via tokenization; verifiable intent (with Google) lets a cardholder challenge an agent purchase so "the chargeback process can kick back in". B2B agent purchasing likewise fits cards. Machine-to-machine payments ("low ticket, micro ticket transaction that happens at very high velocity") may need different infrastructure: Agent Pay for Machines is "the only network protocol that's out there today to facilitate that"; settlement "could involve stable coins, but it also could involve different types of settlements. We're actually quite open to that." Stablecoins "will play a role in the financial ecosystem of the future"; current approach is spending crypto/stablecoin balances by card, network settlement, money movement; "stablecoin isn't the answer to everything"; multiplicity needs "a trusted interoperable layer ... that is what BVNK will do for us." [Q2 2026 call, Q&A, Ramsey El-Assal / Cantor Fitzgerald]
