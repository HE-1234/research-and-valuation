# ROIC comparison while editing

The app compares three measures on Operating margin and Reinvestment, without changing the DCF calculation or owner assumptions.

- **Ratio-implied ROIC:** each resolved year's operating margin after tax, multiplied by its sales-to-capital assumption. Damodaran's [financial definitions](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/definitions.html) decompose total return into after-tax operating margin and total capital turnover. Our assumption sizes **new investment**, so the product is a constant-margin diagnostic rather than an identity for the return on all accumulated capital. Changing margins, investment timing and spending overrides can separate the two. His [growth value drivers](https://people.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/littlebook/growthvaluedrivers.htm) distinguish investment-led growth from changing profitability.
- **Forecast ROIC:** the existing engine's after-tax operating profit divided by beginning-of-year invested capital, rolled forward by reinvestment. No replacement calculation is introduced; `roic.return_comparisons` reads resolved engine rows. Zero or negative capital is labelled unavailable rather than interpreted as a meaningful positive or negative return. Terminal ROIC remains separately specified on Terminal value.
- **Historical ROIC:** a reported-accounting proxy calculated from annual operating profit, historical effective tax rates and beginning-of-year book equity plus debt and operating leases, less cash and short-term investments. Goodwill and acquired intangibles remain. Reported tax rates reflect non-operating items too; applying them to operating income is an approximation. Operating-lease interest and capitalized research adjustments are not invented. The app follows the engine's convention of no tax benefit on operating losses and retains unusual sourced tax rates without clamping.

The saved model's starting-period ROIC is a separate benchmark: its numerator may include owner/model adjustments, and its denominator is capital at the end of that starting period. It is not inserted into the annual opening-capital series or described as a directly comparable fiscal year.

Each `companies/<TICKER>/valuation/historical-roic.yaml` records schema version, ticker, information cutoff, capital definition, a short summary, comparability notes and annual inputs. Annual records contain period and end date, operating income, tax rate, opening capital, source tags and cached source paths; capital components and source-section details are retained for verification. The loader performs no writes and does not use evidence newer than the valuation cutoff. Missing inputs and unavailable cached sources do not become zero or estimated history.

Initial evidence covers five fiscal-year rows for AAOI, GOOGL, MRVL, MU and SNDK. Each has three calculable recent returns; the two earlier opening-capital balances are unavailable in the current source cache. Sandisk's separation and impairment history, Marvell's acquisitions, Micron's cycle, Alphabet's tax/capital definitions and AAOI's losses/zero-interest supplier notes receive explicit comparability notes. The original business-report ROE or property-return proxies are not relabelled as ROIC.

Tests use hand-calculated return identities, authored late margins and tax fades, spending overrides, loss years, nonpositive capital, anomalous historical taxes, missing/future evidence and live changes from both input pages. All calculated rates are diagnostics, not a new valuation assumption or an automatic judgment that a scenario is right or wrong.
