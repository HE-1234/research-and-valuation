## Scope limits and non-blocking observations

- This is a desktop screenshot and interaction audit, not an exhaustive accessibility or mobile audit. Every page was opened and its initial viewport inspected; not every lower section, expander, input, or table was exhaustively captured. GOOGL long-form reasoning and EXMP edit/save were specifically exercised in addition to those page captures.
- The sticky panel occupies substantial sidebar height, so earlier navigation and Review & save can require scrolling the sidebar back up. The selected valuation remains visible during that scrolling. This is usable at the tested width, although a more compact panel could improve smaller screens.
- Captions remain visually subdued. A sampled computed caption foreground `rgb(82,102,94)` against app background `rgb(247,248,245)` gave 5.76:1 before considering any ancestor opacity; no comprehensive contrast certification was performed.
- Invalid-ratio handling was tested; a separate engine-level stopped-case fixture and every stopped-case variant were not tested. GOOGL's unavailable management case explanation was present in the browser snapshot.
- Write report, repository commit, stale-file conflict, actual live-company data, and the production port 8501 were not exercised. They are outside the mutation scope of this audit. No commit or report write was performed.
- The npm wrapper intermittently attempted a registry request and timed out. The final pass used the already installed CLI directly with approved local-browser execution; this did not affect the app under test.
