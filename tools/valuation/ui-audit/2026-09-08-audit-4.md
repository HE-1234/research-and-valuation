## Punch list

1. **Results, 1180: the three action buttons are cut.** "Save to the assumpti…", "Write the report (valu…", "Record in the reposit…" in a four-column row; full labels at 1440. Use three columns, or shorter labels with the detail in the captions that are already there. `googl-1180/page-10-results.png`, `mrvl-1180/page-10-results.png` against `googl-1440/page-10-results.png`.
2. **The fade sentence assumes growth falls.** With a year-5 growth below terminal growth the growth page says "Years 6-10 by rule: 2.6% / 3.1% / 3.7% / 4.2% / 4.8%, easing from 2.0% in year 5 to the terminal growth of 4.77%" and the stories clause still says "growth eases to the risk-free rate by year 10". Choose the verb from the direction, or use a neutral one ("moves in equal steps to"). `googl-probe/growth-bear-y5-2pct.png`, `googl-probe/stories-bear-y5-2pct.png`, `mrvl-probe/` same two.
3. **Stories clause: one line for two paths, and no number.** §18.10 asks for a clause under each of the two defining lines with the number ("then easing to 4.8% by year 10"); the app gives one combined caption naming "the risk-free rate". Split it and quote the rate.
4. **Story boxes cut their last line at 1180** (base +20/+24 px, bull +39/+43 px). Size the box to its text or make it taller at narrow widths. `googl-probe/stories-textareas.json`.
5. **The bear case offers "Allow a large premium" with the wrong help.** Its premium must be 0 (the box's own help says so) but the checkbox help reads "Needed above 8 points in this case". Hide the checkbox in the bear case, or say "The bear case stays at zero by rule." `googl-probe/tooltips-terminal.json` entry 4.
6. **The chart drops a reference label when the bar is short.** MRVL bear 26.11 has no "5-year stop 24.72" at 1180 or 1440. Put the label above the bar when it does not fit inside. `mrvl-1180/page-10-results.png`, `mrvl-1440/page-10-results.png`.

9. **Diagnostics row labels are lowercase** ("bear case, free cash flow (USD millions)") while every table and heading elsewhere capitalises; and the red marker reads as part of the title ("6. Value against price flag", "7. The step … terminal year flag"). Use `CASE_LABELS` and set the marker apart, e.g. "(flagged)". `crop-mrvl-diag.png`, `crop-googl-diag.png`.
10. **Warnings sentences have no full stop** ("… the year-10 inputs disagree", "… should say what the return is without it"), while the case-box messages got theirs in audit 3. `googl-probe6/results-flagged-warnings.png`, `mrvl-1180/page-10-results.png`.
