# Valuation app UI audit 1 — 2026-09-07

Audited against AGENTS.md §18.10 (guided-walk shape, "Every factor page has the same layout", Rules) and §3 (smart 16-year-old reader). App running headless on http://localhost:8770/ (the rebuilt guided-walk version).

## Verdict: FIX FIRST

The walk itself is the right shape: one factor per page, explanations a 16-year-old can follow, reasons next to numbers, a live readout that answers instantly. What blocks shipping is what the owner sees the moment anything is off the happy path (engine messages, unsaved changes, error states) and three tables that hide their own content. Everything below is fixable inside `tools/valuation/app_core.py` / `app_pages.py` except where noted (YAML reasons, datasets).

## Blockers

1. **Every factor page, live readout (GOOGL 1366; `shots/googl-1366/page-04.png`…`page-08.png`, `full/probe/weights-105-t01.png`, `weights-105-t00.png`)** — When the engine cannot compute, `readout_line()` glues the error into the value sentence: "With your current inputs: Enter the market inputs on the Start page to compute. per share." and, with weights at 105%, "**With your current inputs: The assumptions do not validate: scenarios.*.weight: bear + base + bull must sum to 1, got 1.050000 per share, against a price of 338.46.**" The asterisks render literally because the message contains blank lines and bold cannot span paragraphs; the same raw text appears a second time in the sidebar and a third time in a red box at the top of the page, next to the one good message ("Weights add up to 105%. They must add up to 100% before the model computes."). — Fix: when `result is None`, return one plain sentence ("Not computed: weights add up to 105%; they must add up to 100%.") with no "per share / against a price" suffix and no bold; translate SchemaError/EngineError text to plain English in one place (`compute_result`), and suppress the top-of-page `st.error` when the page already shows the same problem.

2. **Terminal value page, case-stop note and readout; Results (GOOGL 1366; `full/probe/terminal-growth-6-t02.png`, `results-with-stop-t00.png`)** — A stopped case tells the owner: "scenarios.bull.terminal.growth.value: 0.0600 is above the risk-free rate 0.0475; set allow_above_riskfree: true and give a reason (section 18.4 rule 5)". Raw YAML path, rates as decimals, a YAML key to "set" when the screen has a checkbox for exactly that, a spec citation; then the whole string is repeated inside the readout ("bull not computed (scenarios.bull.terminal…)"), in the sidebar, and as a caption on Results. — Fix: map engine messages to screen language ("Bull case not computed: terminal growth 6.00% is above the risk-free rate 4.75%. Tick 'Allow terminal growth above the risk-free rate' or lower the number."); the readout should say only "bull not computed (see its box)"; format rates with `pct()`.

3. **Results page, Unsaved changes and Warnings (GOOGL 1366; `full/googl-1366/tiles/page-10-t01.png`)** — The list the owner reviews before pressing Save reads `scenarios.base.revenue_growth.values.0 | 0.2 | 0.25`; the warning reads "Risk-free rate (%) is a manual value (0.0425) typed in the app". These are the internals the walk was built to hide. — Fix: label each change in words ("Base case, revenue growth, Year 1") and show values as the page shows them ("20.0% → 25.0%"; "4.25%"); keep the path in a tooltip if the builder wants it.

4. **Results page, Year by year table (GOOGL 1366 `page-10b-expanded-t01.png`/`-t02.png`; MRVL 1280 `page-10b-expanded-t02.png`; GOOGL 1180 `page-10b-expanded-t02.png`)** — Eleven columns in a scrolling dataframe: at 1366 "Present value" and "Implied return on capital" are cut to "Pr" / "-1" and the Year column shows "Termin"; at 1280 the cut starts at "Discount f"; at 1180 at "Cost o". The two columns the table exists to show (present value, return on capital) need a horizontal scrollbar. — Fix: `static_table` (st.table wraps) with shorter headers ("After-tax profit", "Reinvest.", "FCF", "PV", "ROIC") and drop "Discount factor"/"Cost of capital" into the caption, or transpose so years are columns.

5. **Facts check bridge table and every dataframe over ten rows (GOOGL 1366 `page-09-t05.png`; guidance table GOOGL `page-04b-expanded-t02.png`, MRVL 1280 `page-04b-expanded-t02.png`)** — `st.dataframe` shows ten rows and hides the rest behind an internal scroll with no visible affordance: "The bridge for the base case" has eleven rows and the last one ("Enterprise value today …") is clipped to a sliver; "Guidance on record" shows 10 of 34 items for MRVL, cuts every quote mid-sentence ("…range to $195-205 billion, up fro"), shows Source as "[Q2 2026 call, p" and pushes "Used as" off-screen (at 1280 Source is invisible). The management case is the one place the owner sees what the company said. — Fix: `st.table` (wraps, no inner scroll) for all summary tables and for guidance (Item — quote — source — used as), or pass `height` that fits all rows.

## Major

## What works well (keep)

- The explanations at the top of each factor page: plain, concrete, correctly pitched ("20 means revenue ends the year a fifth higher", "fast growth at a low ratio eats most of the cash it creates").
- One factor per page with Back/Next, the progress line "Step N of 10", and the sidebar step list with ticks for visited pages; jumping from the sidebar works.
- The live readout in a bordered box at the bottom and mirrored in the sidebar; it recomputes immediately on Enter.
- Five year boxes in a row, labelled Year 1–5 (%), prefilled; "rule" and "shared" placeholders for empty override boxes.
- Reasons in full next to their numbers with the source tags in small grey text beneath; nothing behind a hover except definitions.
- The Terminal page per-case caption ("terminal growth 4.25%, return on capital 8.75%, cost of capital 8.75%; the terminal value is 89.6% of operating assets") is exactly the check §18.10 asks for.
- "Weights add up to 100%." / "Weights add up to 105%. They must add up to 100%…" in plain words.
- The Results table fits without horizontal scrolling at 1366, 1280 and 1180; the dashed price line and its label never collide with bars or labels; the two sensitivity heatmaps are legible with the entered cell outlined and the meaning of rows and columns spelled out under each.
- Edit facts toggle: read-only by default, reasons stay alongside the inputs when editing.
- `md()` escaping: zero KaTeX renderings on any page, dollar amounts in reasons show literally, no stack trace anywhere; a stopped case shows a message in its box rather than breaking the page.
- The management case is clearly marked as recorded-only and never weighted.
