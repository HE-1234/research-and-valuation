# Valuation app requirements

Read for app changes. For a valuation draft, read the Required draft app check section and its review-only loading/recording instructions; a new full app audit is required only under the delivery rule below. The [root instructions](../../AGENTS.md) preserve owner boundaries. These rules do not require an app audit for instruction-only or CLI-only changes.

<a id="section-18-10"></a>

## 18.10 Interactive app: a guided walk through the assumptions

`uv run --extra app valuation-app` starts a local Streamlit page (optional dependency group `app`; the engine stays on PyYAML/openpyxl/xlrd/ruamel). It is a view and an editor of `assumptions.yaml`; it holds no valuation logic and calls `valuation.compute`, `valuation.render`, and the YAML writer.

**Shape.** A Mercury-inspired workspace with a quiet, grouped sidebar and dedicated pages. The owner can follow Back and Next through the assumptions or jump directly to a page. Keep the company picker in the sidebar, the active destination clear, and a compact live-value strip above the factor content. Use a white canvas, pale neutral navigation, restrained indigo accents, subtle borders, readable sans-serif typography, and generous spacing. Short explanations introduce each decision; full analyst reasons stay visible with the relevant numbers. Tabs or additional pages are permitted when they make a distinct task easier to find; do not compress unrelated tasks into one page. Owner direction (2026-09-10): model the experience after Mercury's interactive demo, with intuitive navigation, prettier text and more focused pages.

**Pages, in order:**

1. **Overview.** The as-of quarter; price, risk-free rate, and equity risk premium with their dates and overrides; a short orientation; the factor ranking for this company. The company picker is available in the sidebar on every page.
2. **Scenarios.** Compact side-by-side bear, base and bull path comparisons, followed by full-width readable stories, adjacent story-to-number links and editable story controls. Include the management case. The owner agrees with the shape of the cases before changing inputs.
3. **Revenue growth.** 4. **Operating margin.** The five explicit years, with a read-only line for years 6–10 updated live. Then **Reinvestment**, **Cost of capital**, **Terminal value**, and **Taxes and weights** in computed impact order. Revenue and margin stay first.
5. **Source facts.** Base year and bridge as sourced read-only tables, with an Edit facts control for corrections.
6. **Valuation.** The [§18.5](../../.claude/skills/draft-valuation/references/valuation-output.md#section-18-5) case comparison and price chart, with the other-horizon reference labelled explicitly.
7. **Analysis.** Sensitivity grids and reverse DCF.
8. **Cash flow forecast.** Case selector and complete year-by-year results, with fade years marked; split wide tables into readable sections.
9. **Simulation.** The existing Monte Carlo experiment and its export, with settings and results separate from deterministic assumptions and saving.
10. **Model checks.** Diagnostics, transition checks and warnings.
11. **Review & save.** Saved-versus-edited comparison, note, Save, Write valuation.md, Commit and Start over. Quick review/save remains accessible while editing factors.

**Every factor page has the same layout, top to bottom:** (a) a short explanation for the 16-year-old: what the factor is and why it matters to the value, two to four sentences, with detailed method guidance available beside it; (b) the company's own history for the factor where the YAML carries it (the analyst's reasons hold the history tables; the `diagnostics.historical_*` cells feed a one-line comparison); (c) one block per case (bear, base, bull, management when computable) with the analyst's proposed values, the reason in full, the source tags, and the input widgets prefilled with the proposal; (d) a live readout line at the bottom: value per share for bear, base, bull, and weighted with the current inputs against the price, so the owner sees what their change did before moving on; (e) Back and Next.

**Rules.**
- Source references throughout the app are clickable: open a read-only cached evidence view at highlighted exact quotations or explicit retained page/section/paragraph locators, and retain access to the recorded original URL and full cached text. Resolve tags through the company's source index, retain locators, and never guess a filing URL or link an unknown peer citation to the current company's filing. Show multiple candidate passages when ambiguous and label them as candidates, not verified support. Whole-document references say when no exact passage is recorded and provide document search; never invent missing precision. Source views open separately so editing state is preserved; missing evidence remains explicitly unavailable.
- Markdown research notes open as readable articles with clear authorship/type labels, a contents navigation, formatted headings/tables/quotes, search and links for recorded citations. Preserve all source wording and numbers, keep raw text downloadable, and distinguish agent analysis and methodology notes from original company evidence.
- Scenarios includes editable bear/base/bull probability weights, synchronized with Taxes and weights and the live weighted result. Default to 25% / 50% / 25%, preserve existing saved choices, provide an explicit reset to that mix, validate a 100% total, and use the existing reviewed Save flow. Management remains unweighted.
- Terminal return controls permit a supported positive bear premium as well as zero. Show its existing large-premium override at the same 8-point warning threshold as base; this is a warning convention, not an economic target. Explain retained advantage without asserting moat loss from the case label. All cases receive transition diagnostics. Loading the updated app never changes saved inputs.
- Scenario narratives pair variables with readable current values: one variable per row, explicit units, and at most five year columns per table. Render percentages rather than raw decimal arrays and distinguish calculated forecasts from inputs. Current working assumptions are authoritative; preserve saved analyst mapping notes separately and label them when edits differ. Show management guidance as a separate unweighted evidence view, with its coverage limits and analyst translations explicit; do not imply that it is automatically the bull case.
- Reinvestment and Operating margin show ratio-implied ROIC, the engine's forecast ROIC and the cost of capital beside the case inputs, plus sourced historical ROIC. The ratio product uses after-tax margin and is labelled as a constant-margin check on new investment, not total-capital ROIC. Forecast ROIC reuses the engine's opening-capital roll-forward; spending overrides and missing/nonpositive capital are explicit. Historical observations live separately in `valuation/historical-roic.yaml`, with reported operating profit, sourced tax rates, beginning capital, definitions, dates, cached source paths and comparability notes. The app computes these returns read-only; no guessed missing capital, tax benefit on losses, silent tax-rate clipping, or ROE/PPE proxy presented as ROIC. Preserve historical facts during scenario edits and identify the saved starting-period model benchmark's different timing/adjustments.
- Motion is purposeful and brief: a subtle page entrance on navigation, responsive button feedback, and a short disclosure fade. Never replay a page entrance for input recalculation or animate financial values. Respect reduced-motion preferences; no looping decoration, staggered reading delays, or motion that blocks interaction.
- One factor per page; additional pages or focused tabs may organize distinct tasks. Per-year inputs are five wide number boxes in a row labelled Year 1–5, not a grid editor, with the read-only fade line for years 6–10 beneath; ten boxes only when the file carries ten-entry lists.
- Plain text only in prose: no LaTeX, no Unicode math symbols (×, Σ, ≤), no `$` sign in markdown (Streamlit reads `$` as a formula; write "USD" or escape it). Percentages one decimal; money in USD millions with separators; per share to the cent.
- Reasons are always visible next to their numbers, never hidden behind a hover. Short introductory explanations come first; longer method notes may be expanded. Difficult concepts have an adjacent clickable and keyboard-accessible question mark that reveals a one-sentence definition.
- During editing, keep the selected case’s current value and change since load visible alongside the market price, including after sidebar navigation. Label unavailable results explicitly. Provide review/save from factor pages, saved-versus-edited comparisons, scoped resets, and a nearby case-story reference. Preserve the saved analyst reasons and label them when the owner changes their inputs.
- A change that stops a scenario shows the engine's message in place of that case's number; never a stack trace.
- Save writes only changed values, preserves reasons, sources, and comments, appends a `changelog` entry per changed cell with the owner's note, regenerates `assumptions.md`, and refuses if the file changed on disk since load. Write valuation.md runs the same code path as `compute-valuation`; Commit runs its git step; never push.
- Before delivering a changed app version to the owner, a separate audit agent opens every page in a headless browser, takes screenshots, and checks: nothing cramped, nothing truncated, no rendering artefacts, one clear action per page, consistent number formats, readable at a laptop width. Findings go back to the builder; the audit report is kept under `tools/valuation/ui-audit/` and identifies the version checked. An unchanged version may reuse its recorded passing audit, but this never replaces the target-specific draft check below. Local startup checks and instruction-only or CLI-only changes do not require a new full visual audit. This is a delivery check, not a requirement to ask the owner for confirmation at each app page.

<a id="required-draft-app-check"></a>

**Required draft app check.** After building a valuation narrative and assumptions, verify the actual target in the running valuation app, including REVISE/BLOCKED drafts. Select and confirm the exact company, quarter and `<ASSUMPTIONS>` path; an old active file is not a check of a redraft candidate. Open Scenarios, every factor page, Source facts and a recorded source link, Model checks, and Review & save. Confirm the full narratives, values, reasons and source/missing-input messages are readable without exceptions or blank pages. Exercise navigation and a reversible disclosure or in-memory edit/reset; verify review controls remain usable and disabled actions explain why. Successful valuation calculation is not required: incomplete inputs must produce a clear, usable missing-data state, without replacing nulls or changing judgments to make the app pass.

Before the [§18.7](../../.claude/skills/draft-valuation/references/workflow-contracts.md#section-18-7) compute boundary, start the app with `VALUATION_APP_REVIEW_ONLY=1 uv run --extra app valuation-app --server.port <unused-port>`: do not run or expose valuation results, and do not click Save, Write valuation.md or Commit. Use a fresh server process after app code changes so cached imports cannot hide or preserve a rendering defect. For a separate candidate, stage its exact bytes under the same company's path in an isolated temporary `VALUATION_REPO_ROOT`, with the source context needed by the app; verify the staged and original hashes match. Never overwrite active assumptions or substitute another company. If safe loading remains unsupported, report that precise capability gap.

Record the company, exact original and loaded input paths and SHA-256, app version/revision, pages and interactions checked, and PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED outcome in `review/<QLABEL>-valuation-app-check.md`; confirm the original input bytes remain unchanged. Repeat after final narrative/input or app fixes, and record the final promoted path if applicable. This is a usability check within the existing workflow, not another financial review pass or an owner approval gate. A financial-review block does not excuse skipping unaffected rendering checks; report an app-check failure separately and do not claim the draft is app-ready.
