## 17. Lessons (owner feedback log)

- **2026-09-18 — Verify each valuation draft in the app.** The owner reported that AVGO's narrative/assumptions did not render. A readable Markdown file, successful validator, or prior app audit does not prove that a new draft works in the app. §18.10 now requires a check of the actual company's final inputs and narrative, including blocked drafts, before handoff. Missing inputs may prevent calculation; they must remain readable and reviewable without blank pages or exceptions. This check does not authorize changing assumptions or crossing the draft-before-compute boundary.

- **2026-09-18 — Automatically chain the skills needed for an initial valuation.** During the Oracle request, the owner confirmed the missing research prerequisite and then asked to use the skills automatically. Treat missing initial research as part of an initial valuation request; do not ask the owner to select skills or repeat that authorization. Preserve the existing rebuild, redraft and draft-before-compute owner gates.

- **2026-09-18 — Make research notes readable and identify their authorship.** The owner found that document links sometimes opened agent notes and requested a prettier layout. Markdown notes now use an article reader with headings, tables, section navigation, search and recorded citation links. Clearly distinguish agent analysis, methodology notes and source records; retain raw text and original-document access without rewriting the evidence or assumptions.

- **2026-09-18 — Separate rate units from economic bounds.** The owner flagged apparent percentage/decimal inconsistency in SNDK. Stored inputs use decimal fractions across companies; the validator incorrectly rejected an explicitly allowed return premium above 1.0 as a percentage-format error. §18.4 now makes the representation explicit: 1.0 means 100%, and return premiums use decimal percentage-point differences. Honor the existing large-premium override and reason without rescaling assumptions; retain warnings and field-specific limits.

- **2026-09-17 — Take citations to their supporting passages.** The owner wants a click to show and highlight the referenced parts. Citation links now open a cached evidence view with exact quoted-text or explicit retained locator matches, with the original document still available. Whole-document references must say that no exact passage was recorded; search matches and ambiguous headings must not be presented as verified support. Preserve assumptions and source text while improving navigation.

- **2026-09-17 — Open evidence and edit scenario probabilities where they are discussed.** The owner requested clickable filing references throughout the app and editable bear/base/bull weights with a 25/50/25 default. §18.10 now resolves citations to recorded original documents or cached source views, and puts synchronized weight controls directly on Scenarios. Preserve saved owner weights and require an explicit reset to restore defaults.

- **2026-09-16 — Make scenario variables and values easy to match.** The owner found the narrative number column ambiguous and management's AAOI story too similar to bull. §18.10 now uses labelled current-input matrices with units/year headings, preserves original numeric annotations as reference, and separates management's quoted guidance from independently chosen case assumptions. This feedback changes presentation; it does not authorize redrafting the owner's assumptions.

- **2026-09-11 — Compare implied returns with historical ROIC while editing.** The owner wants the return implied by margin and sales-to-capital beside the relevant inputs, with historical company returns to judge plausibility. §18.10 now shows both the ratio-based new-investment diagnostic and the accumulated-capital forecast, plus source-backed annual history with explicit accounting/timing differences and unavailable years.

- **2026-09-10 — Purposeful interaction motion.** The owner requested a little of Linear/Wealthsimple's polish when changing pages or clicking controls, without unnecessary animation. §18.10 now limits motion to short action/navigation feedback, keeps figures steady and respects reduced-motion preferences.

- **2026-09-10 — Mercury-style valuation workspace.** The owner preferred Mercury's interactive demo and asked for prettier, more readable text and easier movement between focused pages. §18.10 now uses grouped navigation, wider story prose, a compact live-value strip and separate destinations for valuation, analysis, forecasts, simulation, model checks and saving. More pages or focused tabs are permitted; preserving explanations and unsaved edits matters more than fitting everything on one screen.

- **2026-09-08 — Make valuation approachable while editing.** The owner found technical inputs hard to understand and their impact hard to see. Add clickable, keyboard-accessible question marks with one-sentence definitions beside difficult concepts. Keep a selected case’s current value, its change since load, and the market price visible during editing; offer review/save without completing the walk, scoped resets for experiments, and the saved reasoning and case story beside the relevant numbers. Distinguish edited assumptions from the unchanged analyst rationale.

Append here whenever the owner gives feedback that changes how reports should be written. Date, what was wrong, what rule changed.
